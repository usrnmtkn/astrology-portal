# Sky aspects: Lilith batch V1 (72 entries, needs_review, V2: people-first prose)

Five-beat source entries for the owner corpus, collective Current Sky register. No signs, dates, houses,
degrees, second person, or em dashes; the app supplies signs, timing, and titles. Anchored in the staged pair
sources (TLDR-Aspect-PairSources-Chiron-Lilith-Nodes-REVIEW.md) and the folder's books: the Black Moon Lilith
texts (Lilith as "a part of you suppressed into the unconscious... trauma, pain, shame, rejection"; "women who
were denied a voice"; "where we are unwilling to compromise"), Hamaker-Zondag's Aspects for aspect mechanics,
Rudhyar's Lunar Nodes for the axis entries. Node-axis rule: an aspect to one node is an event on the whole
axis; the opposition entry is written as the planet sitting on the familiar South Node end. V2 rewrites every
beat so that people are the subjects of sentences and the five beats read as one continuous paragraph when
joined.

---

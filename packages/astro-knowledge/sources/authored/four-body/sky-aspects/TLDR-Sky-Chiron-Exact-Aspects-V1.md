# Sky aspects: Chiron batch V1 (66 entries, needs_review, V2: people-first prose)

Five-beat source entries, collective Current Sky register. No signs, dates, houses, degrees, second person, or
em dashes. Anchored in the staged pair sources and the folder's books: Greene's Chiron in Love (the compliment
that hurts, the envy of someone who shines without effort, the compensating overwork, the tests set for loved
ones that backfire, the anger that turns into apology), Elizabeth Spring's North Node Astrology for the
Chiron-node axis ("Each one of us is a wounded healer, a 'Chiron' that knows best the medicine that cures. I
call that medicine the North Node"), Hamaker-Zondag for aspect mechanics. Per the brief: no generic healing or
wound language without a scene that shows what it means; the node opposition entry is written as the planet
sitting on the familiar South Node end. V2 rewrites every beat so that people are the subjects of sentences and
the five beats read as one continuous paragraph when joined.

## Sun-Chiron

Pair source: visibility presses on the old bruise, and other people's easy confidence can sting like an accusation (Greene: envy usually points at a gift waiting to be claimed).

### Sun conjunction Chiron
Key: `sky.sun.conjunction.chiron`
Human moment:
Some people are finding the award ceremony harder to sit through than the bad year was.
Development detail:
Someone watches a colleague shine without effort and feels the applause land like an accusation, as if every clap were counting the things that got missed.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun conjunct Chiron, people want to be seen in the same breath that they remember being seen going wrong.
Aspect mechanic:
The person they envy is standing exactly where they want to stand.
Conditional consequence:
The envy may be uncomfortable, but it tends to point at a gift waiting for its owner to claim it, and the people who read it that way stop treating the shining colleague as a rival.

### Sun sextile Chiron
Key: `sky.sun.sextile.chiron`
Human moment:
Somebody takes a modest turn in the spotlight this week, and it goes fine.
Development detail:
They give the toast, make the introduction, get through the short presentation, and discover that the room's attention is survivable, which is not what their history predicted.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun sextile Chiron, people are being offered a small stage in the exact place where stages used to cost too much.
Aspect mechanic:
Nobody gets pushed up there; someone has to step onto it.
Conditional consequence:
The moment may pass unclaimed, but the people who take that one good minute of visibility tend to retire more dread than a year of avoiding the room ever did.

### Sun square Chiron
Key: `sky.sun.square.chiron`
Human moment:
Compliments are landing badly this week.
Development detail:
Someone gets praised for good work and cannot take it in: they brush it off, change the subject, and go work twice as hard, as if the kind words were an advance they now have to pay back.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun square Chiron, people are being seen and recognized in the exact place where, once, praise only came with conditions.
Aspect mechanic:
The old lesson says approval has a catch.
Conditional consequence:
If the discomfort feels bigger than the moment, that is the old lesson talking, not the current room, and the people who can tell the difference get to keep the compliment.

### Sun trine Chiron
Key: `sky.sun.trine.chiron`
Human moment:
People are telling the story of their worst year and finding out it counts as a credential.
Development detail:
Someone describes the bad stretch without flinching, and a listener earlier in the same tunnel sits up, because the teller has clearly walked out the far end.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun trine Chiron, people get the platform and the hard material that earns it in the same week, and the telling costs less than it used to.
Aspect mechanic:
The listener needed the story more than the teller needed to protect it.
Conditional consequence:
The telling may cost almost nothing today, and that ease is the opening: a story people can share this cheaply tends to stop being an injury and start being equipment.

### Sun quincunx Chiron
Key: `sky.sun.quincunx.chiron`
Human moment:

Development detail:
Someone preps a presentation that needs confidence, spends the whole morning managing the ache so the confidence can appear, and finds that the performing and the recovering never share a calendar.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun quincunx Chiron, people book the appearances on one schedule and the repair on another, and nobody has introduced the two schedules.

### Sun opposition Chiron
Key: `sky.sun.opposition.chiron`
Human moment:
The confident one and the wounded one end up across the same table.

One reads the other's ease as arrogance, the other reads the caution as fragility, and both of them are wrong about the person they are facing.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun opposite Chiron, one person carries the untroubled shine while the other carries the history that cannot watch shine neutrally.

Conditional consequence:

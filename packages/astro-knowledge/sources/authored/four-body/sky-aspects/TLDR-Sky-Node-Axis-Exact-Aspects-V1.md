# Sky aspects: node-axis batch V1 (60 entries, needs_review, V2: people-first prose)

Five-beat source entries, collective Current Sky register. No signs, dates, houses, degrees, second person, or
em dashes; no destiny, fate, or guaranteed-outcome language. Anchored in the staged pair sources and Rudhyar's
The Lunar Nodes (North Node as the "point of intake," Dragon's Head; South Node as release, Dragon's Tail),
with Elizabeth Spring's North Node Astrology for the development frame. Keys use `north-node`, but every entry
is written to the whole axis: the conjunction opposes the familiar end, the sextile receives the familiar
end's support (a sextile to one node is a trine to the other), the square presses on both ends at once (the
skipped step), and the opposition is written as the planet sitting on the familiar South Node end.
V2 rewrites every beat so that people are the subjects of sentences and the five beats read as one continuous paragraph when joined.

---

## Sun-North Node

Pair source: current identity versus the assignment (pair source: the recognition that arrives for the old act, the role that fits the resume and not the direction, the small step toward the hungry side that feels oddly significant).

### Sun conjunction North Node
Key: `sky.sun.conjunction.north-node`
Human moment:
Small steps toward new things feel strangely important this week, and the people taking them can tell.
Development detail:
Someone signs up for the first class, sends the modest application, walks into an unfamiliar room and makes one introduction, and that small move outweighs everything bigger they did all week.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun conjunct the North Node, people feel most like themselves in the middle of that unfamiliar move, because who they are and who they are becoming briefly point the same way, and the practiced act loses its pull.
Aspect mechanic:
The old role suddenly fits like a costume.
Conditional consequence:
The move may look minor on paper, but people tend to build whatever they let themselves be seen doing, and this week they are being seen in the new room.

### Sun sextile North Node
Key: `sky.sun.sextile.north-node`
Human moment:
People are getting invited into new territory on the strength of old work.
Development detail:
Someone's established reputation opens a door in an unfamiliar field: the credential they already earned covers the entry fee, and the room welcomes them as a beginner without making them start from zero.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun sextile the North Node, people can spend the standing they built on the familiar road as a deposit on the unfamiliar one, and the old crowd cheers instead of blocking the doorway.
Aspect mechanic:
The door only counts if someone walks through it.
Conditional consequence:
If nobody accepts the invitation, the reputation keeps working anyway; the difference is whether it funds the next chapter or only maintains the last one.

### Sun square North Node
Key: `sky.sun.square.north-node`
Human moment:
People are getting applauded for the exact act they want to retire.
Development detail:
Someone gets asked to play the old role for one more season: the room loves it, the request feels flattering, and saying yes means another year in a part that no longer fits, while saying no means disappointing people to their faces.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun square the North Node, people feel pushed from both sides at once, tired of the road behind them and unready for the road ahead, and the reinvention they keep postponing stops waiting politely.
Aspect mechanic:
The applause is for someone they used to be.
Conditional consequence:
They may accept the renewal one more time, but crowds tend to clap for what is already finished, and the people who notice that start rehearsing the next act anyway.

### Sun trine North Node
Key: `sky.sun.trine.north-node`
Human moment:
The new direction is starting to recognize people back.
Development detail:
Someone who showed up as a beginner hears their name used correctly in the unfamiliar room, gets real acknowledgment for early work, and realizes people now know them for what they are building rather than what they archived.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun trine the North Node, people become visible exactly where they are growing, and the familiar crowd raises no objection, so nobody has to run a campaign to be seen in the new place.
Aspect mechanic:
The new room is learning their names on its own.
Conditional consequence:
The shift may be gradual, but people follow where attention goes, and attention has quietly changed neighborhoods.

### Sun quincunx North Node
Key: `sky.sun.quincunx.north-node`
Human moment:
People keep needing two different versions of themselves this week.
Development detail:
Someone shows up polished in the established rooms and finds the same polish reads wrong in the new ones, so every venue change comes with a costume change nobody budgeted for.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun quincunx the North Node, people keep adjusting who they appear to be, because the self that works on the familiar route and the self the new direction keeps requesting refuse to merge on command.
Aspect mechanic:
Each room gets its fit by making the other room a misfit.
Conditional consequence:
The double wardrobe may hold for a while, but people who keep alternating costumes tend to end up building one integrated self, and that is construction, not a quick change.

### Sun opposition North Node
Key: `sky.sun.opposition.north-node`
Human moment:
The comeback offer is arriving, and it is excellent, and it points backward.
Development detail:
Someone gets called back to the old stage on better terms than the new path can match: the familiar role, the guaranteed audience, the version of success they already mastered.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Sun opposite the North Node, people shine brightest exactly where they have already been, while nobody stands in the new territory yet to hand out recognition, so the most affirming option in view reruns the previous chapter.
Aspect mechanic:
The encore would go beautifully, and everyone knows it.
Conditional consequence:
They may take the encore, but chapters rarely advance on encores, and the people who decline tend to spend that season building the stage nobody has applauded yet.

Aspect mechanic:

Conditional consequence:

### Moon quincunx North Node

### Moon opposition North Node
Key: `sky.moon.opposition.north-node`

The homesickness is getting specific: one place, one era, one table.
Development detail:
Someone remembers the old neighborhood, the old crew, the way they used to spend their evenings, all in high resolution, and the new territory cannot compete on warmth yet.
Planetary dynamic:
With the Moon opposite the North Node, people feel most nourished by exactly what is finished, because they keep their comfort stored at the familiar end of the road while the new rooms do not smell like anything yet.

Conditional consequence:

# The Lunar Nodes Enter Aquarius and Leo

Some transits ask for effort. The nodes ask for a direction. The dragon's head is the point of appetite, where the hunger arrives before the plan does. The dragon's tail is the point of release, where what is finished gets set down, willingly or otherwise. Together they are less a moment than an assignment, and the assignment just changed.

On {{entryDate}}, the North Node entered Aquarius and the South Node entered Leo, where they will work until {{exitDate}}. {{coEvents: same-day stations or ingresses, engine-confirmed, one sentence when present}} For the last year and a half the axis ran through Pisces and Virgo: surrender versus service, the checklist versus the mystery. That chapter is closing with its lessons finished or not. The new one runs through a different tension: standing out versus belonging.

Leo is about showing up, sharing your gifts, and letting yourself be seen. Aquarius is about the collective: working together, creating change that benefits everyone. With the North Node in Aquarius, the hunger points at the group: the community that needs rebuilding, the cause that could use your evening hours, the future that requires more than one person to build. Collectively, we get hungrier for systems that work, for belonging that is chosen rather than performed, for the rules we actually agreed to.

The South Node in Leo sets down the performance. Not the light itself: the applause-dependence. The version of you curated for the reaction. The leadership that cannot share a stage. The story where you are the main character of every situation, including other people's. The South Node does not take the skill away; it takes the hiding place. Leo's gifts stay: the warmth, the creative generosity, the courage to be seen. What gets released is being seen as the point.

The shadow work of this axis, named plainly: the times you shaped yourself to be accepted instead of showing up authentically, and the moments you withdrew just to prove you didn't need anyone. Both are the same wound wearing different costumes. Can you shine without making it all about you, and contribute to the group without disappearing? That question runs until {{exitDate}}, and it does not accept a one-word answer.

You don't have to disappear into the group to belong to it. You're allowed to be fully yourself in a crowd that didn't ask for a show. It may help to say this part out loud: "My people are waiting for exactly who I am."

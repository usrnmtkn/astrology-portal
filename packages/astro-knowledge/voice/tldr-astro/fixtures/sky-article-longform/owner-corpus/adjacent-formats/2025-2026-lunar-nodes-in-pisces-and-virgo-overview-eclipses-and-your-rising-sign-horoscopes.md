The lunar nodes, often called the Nodes of Destiny, are celestial markers of the soul’s journey through time. The North Node, a ravenous dragon’s head, marks the uncharted territories your spirit hungers to explore in this incarnation. The lessons yet to be learned, the challenges that call us forward, and the growth that awaits. Opposite stands the South Node, the dragon’s tail, holds the wisdom of what’s been earned in prior incarnations. The skills and tendencies that come as effortlessly as breathing; familiar and comforting, yet all too easy to sink into stagnation if held onto too tightly. Together, they trace a map of where we’ve journeyed and illuminate the path toward where we’re destined to grow.

On January 11, 2025, the lunar nodes shift from the Aries-Libra axis to the Pisces-Virgo axis, ushering in an 18-month cycle that centers on learning to balance intuition and analysis, spirituality and practicality. This is the axis of dreaming and doing, vision and action, surrender with the steady grind of effort - themes that will shape 2025 into 2026 (January 2025 to July 2026).

The Pisces / Virgo axis invites you to honor your dreams (Pisces) by surrendering to the unknown, while Virgo, the maker, grounds you in the reality that dreams are not enough; they demand effort, precision, and care to be forged into reality. The challenge of this nodal cycle is to hold both: the boundless expanse of your imagination and the steady groundedness of your actions. The work lies in keeping faith without losing your footing.

Living in this tension between inspiration and practicality is the work of this nodal cycle. It’s not about favoring one over the other but allowing them to work together, each strengthening and balancing the other. The vast, dream-filled expansiveness of Pisces nourishes the garden that Virgo has planted, its waters feeding the soil of intention and care. Meanwhile, Virgo’s structure and precision give form and purpose to Pisces’ creative outpour, shaping visions into reality.

Growth is born from the alchemy of water and earth. Water, like inspiration, moves with a restless hunger, seeping into every crack, awakening what sleeps beneath the surface. Earth, steadfast and patient, cradles the seed, giving it the structure it needs to root deeply and rise steadily. Without water’s life-giving touch, the soil hardens, and the seed withers in its shell. Without earth’s firm embrace, water’s flow scatters aimlessly, leaving nothing to take hold. Together, they are fluid yet grounded, yielding yet enduring—birthing a life that reaches both toward the heavens and into the depths below.

The polarity of Virgo/Pisces is about balancing order (Virgo) and chaos (Pisces). From disorganization to organization, we are seeking stability within the rise and fall of the emotional waves. Pisces is represented by two fishes swimming in opposite directions, one toward the euphoric highs, the other toward the sabotaging lows—symbolizing the conflict of our emotional currents. Together, they offer a lesson in balance: how to find stability amidst the emotional tides, moving from disorganization to alignment, from overwhelm to clarity. Together, they remind us that both chaos and order have a role to play in the art of creation. The universe rewards both the vision and the effort.

## Reflecting on the 2006 – 2008 Nodal Cycle

Where were you called to balance the practical with the spiritual? How did you navigate the tension between trusting the flow and staying grounded in your day-to-day life? The energies of that time are resurfacing, offering you a chance to revisit, refine, and move through these lessons with greater awareness and intention. Past experiences hold the keys to navigating this cycle with clarity and purpose.

From 2006 to 2007, the North Node in Pisces called for spiritual growth, compassion, and the breaking down of boundaries, while the South Node in Virgo urged the release of perfectionism and overly critical tendencies. As the lunar nodes return to this axis in 2025, expect themes from that period to resurface, offering an opportunity for reflection, course correction, and deeper integration of the lessons left unresolved.

The rise of social media platforms like Friendster, Myspace, Facebook, and Twitter began to transform global communication, creating a sense of interconnectedness aligned with Piscean ideals. The release of Al Gore's documentary "An Inconvenient Truth" in 2006 brought climate change to the forefront of public consciousness, prompting a collective call to address environmental issue; a reflection of Virgo's concern for the Earth and Pisces' universal compassion.

As we move into 2025, the digital landscape mirrors the evolving tides of accountability and care. In November 2024, the Australian Parliament made a bold move, passing legislation that prohibits individuals under the age of 16 from accessing platforms like TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, and X (formerly Twitter). This moment marks a shift in the global conversation about technology, security, and connection. Social media, once celebrated as a space for dissolving boundaries and fostering interconnectedness — a true Piscean ideal — now reveals its shadow side: unchecked influence and exploitation. Virgo’s energy steps into this dialogue, demanding discernment, accountability, and action. It reminds us that safeguarding our collective well-being often requires making tough, but difficult choices to ensure safety and integrity in our interconnected world.

This shift challenges us to balance the expansive potential of digital connection with systems that prioritize care and safety. Pisces dissolves boundaries, while Virgo calls for discernment and accountability, reminding us that vision must be grounded in sustainable action; even in spaces meant to bring us together.

## The different nodal events in your lifetime

The nodal cycle doesn’t follow a straight path—it spirals. With each turn, it revisits familiar themes, deepening the story and sharpening the lessons. At every crossroads, the nodes challenge you: Will you meet the moment? Will you rise to what fate demands?

### Nodal Return

Every 18.5 years, the nodes call you home. This is your nodal return. It’s the snake swallowing its tail, a cosmic reminder of who you are and why you’re here.

When the transiting North Node aligns with your natal North Node (and the South Node with your natal South Node), the universe asks: Will you meet the moment? Will you rise to what fate demands?

This event occurs around the ages of 18–19, 37–38, 56–57, and 74–75. In moments like these, life feels filled with a sense of destiny. You may find yourself at a crossroads, faced with decisions that feel both terrifying and divinely guided. Pay attention, this is not the time to shrink from your calling.

### Nodal Square

If the nodal return is the homecoming, the nodal square is the trial. It’s the grindstone that sharpens your edges and clarifies your course. When the transiting nodes form a square (90°) to your natal nodes, tension arises. The universe asks: What will you choose when the path forward isn’t clear? Will you confront the resistance, or let it stop you?

This event unfolds around ages 4–5, 13–14, 22–23, 31–32, 40–41, 49–50, 58–59, 67–68, and 76–77. The nodal square brings challenges that expose misalignments between your choices and your destiny. It’s a crossroads, marked by external events or inner turmoil that force you to reevaluate your direction. The friction is here to refine you.

### Nodal Opposition

At the nodal opposition, the cosmic wheel turns, and you’re pulled in two directions. The North Node stands where your South Node once was, and the South Node where your North Node began. This is the axis of integration, where the past meets the future, and the universe asks: Have you learned from where you’ve been? Are you ready to step into where you’re going?

This event occurs around ages 9–10, 27–28, 46–47, 65–66, and 84–85. It’s a moment of reckoning with the push-pull of your life’s polarity: comfort versus growth, familiarity versus potential. The nodal opposition is the mirror that reflects your journey so far. What have you carried forward that no longer serves you? What lessons from your past must you now embody to step into your becoming?

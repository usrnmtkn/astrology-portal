Chiron is the wounded healer: the teacher whose credential is the injury that never fully closed. A Chiron transit does not break anything new. It finds the old break, the one that healed crooked because nobody set it properly at the time, and it aches on schedule until the ache gets your attention. The wound Chiron points to is never random and never fair; it arrived before you had any say. What you do have a say in is the second half of the myth: the wound that cannot be cured can still become a skill. The people who have hurt exactly where you hurt are the only ones who can teach the way through, and under this transit, some of them will be teaching you, and eventually you will be one of them.

The generational math is personal. Everyone born with Chiron in {{sign}} gets their Chiron return near age fifty, and everyone else meets this transit in one house of their chart. Either way the assignment reads the same: stop pretending to be well in the wounded place, let it be tended by people who know the territory, and notice the day you catch yourself helping someone through the exact thing that once had you on the floor. That day is the transit working.

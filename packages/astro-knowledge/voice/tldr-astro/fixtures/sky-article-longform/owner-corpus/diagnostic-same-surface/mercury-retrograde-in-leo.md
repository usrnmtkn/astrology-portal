Retrograde: July 18 to August 11, 2025
Shadow Period: June 30 to August 25

Mercury is retrograde in Leo from July 18 to August 11, moving between 15° and 4° of this fixed fire sign. But the work begins earlier. On June 30, Mercury enters the pre-retrograde shadow, initiating a review process that won’t fully resolve until August 25.

The shadow period runs from June 30 to August 25. The shadow begins when Mercury first passes the degrees it will later revisit, and ends when it clears those same degrees again. The shadow is where the reflection deepens, the patterns repeat, and the realignment begins.

Jump to Horoscopes

The core theme of this retrograde is the story you tell about yourself, and why you need others to believe it. This Mercury retrograde wraps itself around the Leo New Moon on July 24, a lunation that opposes Pluto and asks us to rebirth a more honest version of ourselves. This is about letting go of the version of yourself that was built to impress, and making space for the one that’s built to live.

If you're noticing communication issues, creative confusion, or people from the past resurfacing, you're likely already feeling this transit. The most grounded response during Mercury Rx is to surrender. Sometimes the only move is to make peace with not knowing. No amount of overthinking will force clarity to arrive before it's ready.

Leo is a sign of bold expression, performance, pride, and presence. When Mercury retrogrades here, it can challenge your voice, your confidence, and the way you tell your story. The ground here is theatrical, personal, and prideful. The sign of Leo governs recognition, narrative identity, and the rituals of attention. This is a time to checking in with how authentic your message really is.

When Mercury moves through Leo, it highlights how we seek recognition. It tunes us into the stories we tell about ourselves. It reminds us that while pride can protect us, it can also isolate us. During this retrograde, we’re asked to look more honestly at how our communication, creativity, and public persona reflect who we really are.

Some themes that may arise:

- Feeling unsure about how you're being perceived

- Conversations that feel more loaded than usual, especially when pride is involved

- The resurfacing of old collaborators, friends, or lovers

- Creative projects that return for a second look

- Branding, storytelling, and identity questions that require a deeper edit

- Learning where ego is blocking growth, or where self-worth needs repair

Leo wants to be heard. Mercury retrograde wants the truth. Together, they challenge us to strip the performance from our message and speak from our heart. This is a time to be honest with why we want to be seen, and what we’re hoping to receive in return.

Leo is ruled by the Sun, the force that shines without apology. But when Mercury moves retrograde through this sign, the light warps. You might feel exposed, misread, or invisible, like the spotlight’s on, but no one really sees you. Who you truly are when no one else is watching.

#### Key Dates

-
June 30: Mercury enters the pre-retrograde shadow at 4° Leo. Watch what themes begin to emerge.

-
July 18: Mercury turns retrograde at 15° Leo. This is the midpoint of the story, where pressure increases and edits begin.

-
August 11: Mercury turns direct at 4° Leo. The fog starts to lift and you begin to apply what you've learned.

-
August 25: Mercury leaves the post-retrograde shadow at 15° Leo. By this point, the story is complete and clarity returns.

Treat this retrograde as a creative audit. If you need to speak, choose your words carefully. If you need to be seen, let it be with intention. Performances, branding, and bold declarations made from ego, or without reflection, are more likely to misfire.

Use this time to revisit what you’ve already created. Refine your message. A time to strengthen the structure of your story before sharing it with the world.

The last time Mercury moved retrograde through this part of Leo was in the summer of 2012. From July 14 to August 8, Mercury traveled from 12° to 1° Leo. That moment marked the beginning of a shift in how we perform, share, and show ourselves to the world. Instagram had just launched. Social media was in its infancy. We were learning how to package our lives for visibility. Many of us learned that attention could be a stand-in for affirmation, but not a sustainable source of it.

Here in 2025, the pressure to be visible is still with us, but now it feels heavier, more draining, and far less satisfying. We've grown since 2012. We've learned what it costs to be constantly performing, and what happens when being seen takes priority over being real.

This Mercury retrograde offers a chance to step back and rewrite that script. To reconnect with the parts of yourself that got edited out in the name of likability or reach. You don’t have to vanish. But you do have to mean what you say. Let this retrograde mark the moment your online persona and story gets honest.

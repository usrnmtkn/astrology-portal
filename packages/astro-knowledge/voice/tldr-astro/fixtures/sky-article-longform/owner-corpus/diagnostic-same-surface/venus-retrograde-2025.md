### Venus in Aries (March 1 – April 3)

Aries is not a patient lover. Venus here doesn’t wait, doesn’t compromise, doesn’t soften her wants to make them more acceptable. She lunges, she claims, she fights. But in retrograde, that fire doesn’t propel forward, it circles. Old desires return, old lovers reappear, old wounds refuse to close. The past becomes an echo chamber. The urgency doesn’t disappear; it just turns inward, burning from the inside out.

Aries is ruled by Mars, and Mars is not sentimental. So if you think this retrograde is going to be about nostalgia, about sweetly revisiting the past to find closure, think again. This is war magick. A moment of clarity with the self. A confrontation with how you have fought for love, or failed to.

Venus in Pisces (April 3 – April 12)

Then the retreat. Pisces. The battlefield dissolves into fog. Aries is direct action, but Pisces is the dream. The undoing. The love that slips through your fingers. What does Venus retrograde in Pisces bring? The love that never quite ended, the love that never quite materialized. The illusion of sacrifice, the self-delusion of martyrdom. Venus is exalted in Pisces, but in retrograde, she’s not quite herself. She second-guesses. She wonders if the sacrifice was worth it. She revisits the places she left herself behind.

Revisiting Past Relationships

Love doesn’t always end with finality. Some connections fade, not from lack of feeling, but from timing, distance, or the weight of what was left unsaid. Some end because they had to, because the version of you that loved them could not continue as you were. Sometimes, we walk away from something before we fully understand what we’re leaving. And yet, Venus retrograde pulls the past back into the present, placing old loves and lost desires in front of you once again.

Venus Rx allows for the possibility that something unfinished may still have life in it. If an ex reappears, if you feel pulled toward someone you once knew, this is the time to ask: Is there something here that still deserves exploration? This is not about throwing yourself into old patterns just because they feel familiar. This is about discernment. About seeing what you couldn’t see before. About revisiting past relationships with a clearer sense of who you are now. Questions to ask yourself: Am I reaching for this person because I miss them, or because I miss who I was when I was with them? Has anything truly changed, or am I just longing for comfort? If this person were brand new to me, would I still choose them?

Not every ember needs to be reignited. But, if you reconnect with a past partner, wait until Venus stations direct on April 12 before making any permanent decisions. What feels like a "meant to be" moment now may shift when Venus moves forward again.

How to Work with Venus Retrograde

The past does not return as an invitation. It returns as a test. What have you learned? What are you still gripping with both hands? What must be burned to the ground?

1. Don’t Rekindle Old Flames Without a Reality Check. If someone from your past resurfaces, observe rather than act. Nostalgia is not truth. Time will reveal what needs no further explanation. No sudden moves. No declarations. Let the story unfold before you decide if you still belong in it. Venus stations direct on April 12. Until then, nothing is certain.

2. Take Inventory of Your Self-Worth. Retrogrades pull energy inward, forcing an inventory of what we’ve ignored. Where have you undervalued yourself? In love, in work, in the quiet spaces where no one is watching? Venus does not reward what is unclaimed. If you want more, decide you are worth it first.

3. Hold Off on Major Beauty Changes. Venus rules adornment. Expression. The way you shape yourself to be seen. But during retrograde, vision is skewed. The hair dye, the tattoo, the wardrobe overhaul. What feels like reinvention now may feel like regret later. When do you feel most like yourself? When do you feel most real? Let that guide you forward, rather than the impulse to disappear and emerge as someone new.

4. Be Mindful with Money & Investments. Venus governs wealth and material security. Under retrograde, currency moves differently. What once felt stable may shift. What seemed impossible may return. Wait before making major financial commitments. Purchases made now may not hold their value. Investments may not be what they seem. Venus retrograde reveals financial blind spots.

5. Release Old Fantasies. Pisces dissolves illusions. The dreams you were once chasing, do they still belong to you? The love you swore would last forever, was it ever real, or just a story you needed at the time?

6. Revisit, Don’t Launch. Venus retrograde resurrects old inspiration. A forgotten creative project calls to you, a once-abandoned pursuit flickers back to life. This is not the time to start something new. Instead, return to what was left undone. Reread your old drafts. Dust off your unfinished paintings. Pick up the instrument, the brush, the poem that once felt like a lifeline. There is gold buried in the past, but it must be refined before it sees the light.

7. Look Back to 2017 & 2009 for patterns. Venus last retrograded through Aries-Pisces in March–April 2017 and March–April 2009. What was happening in your love life then? Your finances? Your sense of self-worth? What was unraveling? What was just beginning? If the same themes are resurfacing now, this is not a coincidence. This is a cycle repeating itself, offering you another choice.

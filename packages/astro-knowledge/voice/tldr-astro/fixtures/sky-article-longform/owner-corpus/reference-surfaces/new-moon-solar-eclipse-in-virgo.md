#### How to Work With This Eclipse (Hint: You Kinda Don't)

The eclipse doesn't need your participation. It needs your presence. But here's what you can do:

- Let things break: When something starts falling apart, resist the urge to immediately fix it, or to be the one to fix things. Just watch. Sometimes things need to collapse to show you they weren't working, or you were the only one trying to make things work in the first place.

- Quit something: Pick one exhausting commitment and end it. (Be realistic, this doesn’t necessarily mean to quit your job without a plan) But quit whatever it is for real. Not "I'll do less of it" or "I'm taking a break." Just be done. That volunteer position that drains you? That weekly obligation you dread? Send the resignation email today.

- Rethink your mornings: Before you dive into your to-do list, ask yourself: "What actually matters today?" Forget what looks impressive or productive. What genuinely needs your attention? The answer might surprise you.

- Listen to your body: Every ache, every wave of exhaustion, that knot in your stomach - they're not inconveniences, they're messages. The eclipse is turning up the volume on these signals. That afternoon crash? That Sunday night dread? Your body is trying to tell you something.

- Make peace with the chaos: Your life might feel messier than usual right now. Good. Stop trying to organize your way through it. Sometimes the mess is showing you exactly what doesn't belong anymore. Let it be messy while you figure out what stays and what goes.

Here's the thing about working with eclipse energy: you can't control it, and you shouldn't try. Your job is to work with what it's showing you. Start by simply observing what's breaking down without rushing to fix it. Some things need to fall apart. If it’s meant to be fixed, it can come back together, after some detached perspective.

The eclipse doesn't need your participation. It needs your presence. Show up, witness what's ready to leave, and let the last degree of Virgo do what it does best: separate what serves from what merely exhausts you in the name of service.

As you stand at this equinox threshold, this balance point between seasons, between light and dark, between holding on and letting go, remember that nature never apologizes for her cycles. Trees don't feel guilty for dropping leaves. Fields don't shame themselves for lying fallow. The earth herself is showing you that rest is part of the cycle, in preparation for what comes next.

Some truths are felt in the body before the mind can articulate them. Some changes happen not through force but through exhausted surrender. Some endings are medicine disguised as loss. This eclipse teaches you the difference between excellence you can sustain and perfection that slowly destroys you.

At 29° Virgo, mastery means knowing when the work is complete—not perfect, but complete. The most powerful edit is often deletion. The strongest boundary is sometimes an ending. The greatest service might be modeling what sustainable caring looks like.

The rest is tomorrow's work. And as the equinox reminds us, tomorrow's light will be perfectly balanced with tomorrow's darkness, there's no rush to do it all in today's light.

Between now and February 2027, everything about how you work, serve, care for yourself, and structure your days is getting dismantled and rebuilt from scratch. This isn't a renovation where you keep the foundation. This is bulldozers and blank slates.

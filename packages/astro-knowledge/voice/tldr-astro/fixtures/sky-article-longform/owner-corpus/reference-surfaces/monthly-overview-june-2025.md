June is a month of elemental tension, Cancer’s cardinal waters rising against the dry independence of Aries fire. Collectively, we begin to learn the cost of comfort, the limits of growth, and that real care needs a structure, a schedule and not just sentiment. There may not be a resolution this month, but there will be motion.

This month, you’re learning what safety means, and what it takes and costs to protect it. On June 6, Venus moves into Taurus, one of her home signs. This is her temple, and for a brief moment, the Earth is pleased. Here, she tends. This is a time to anchor relationships in the body, in food, in scent and soil. Build altars to what sustains you. Invest in comfort on purpose, re-organize your kitchen, switch to cashmere socks, stock your favorite herbal tea. Let your body tell you what it needs. Make choices that feel good in your gut, not just in theory. But Venus will be tested later this month by Mars in Virgo, sharpening the edges of desire. Stability requires maintenance.

Mercury enters Cancer on June 8, followed by Jupiter’s arrival in Cancer on June 9, a year-long transit that asks you to expand your definition of care. Of family. Of home. Jupiter wants to expand what it touches. Growth is complicated when you’ve been taught to suppress your needs. Start building a “soft infrastructure”: meal prep routines, bedtime rituals, boundaries that say yes to your nervous system. Practice saying: “This feels safe,” and “This doesn’t,” without needing to explain why.

Stories long buried may resurface through your voice, your dreams, your memories. You may find yourself repeating patterns that belong to the people who raised you. You might notice yourself repeating things your grandmother used to say, or worrying about the same things your mother did. Sometimes, we inherit more than we realize. Start conversations with older relatives, ask about what they never got to do. Listen with care, and engage with intention. Jupiter in Cancer amplifies not just joy, but also grief. The next year calls you to grow the hearth, not the empire.

The Sagittarius Full Moon on June 11. It arrives like a bright truth in the middle of an emotionally cloudy week. It calls back to unfinished stories from December. What beliefs did you adopt out of fear? What futures did you abandon to make someone else comfortable?

With Mars in the final degrees of Leo, this lunation is combustible. Speak carefully. Move with intention. Fire reveals, but it also scorches.

On June 15, Jupiter squares Saturn in Aries. This is a challenge. Jupiter seeks to expand, Saturn to contain. Saturn in Aries demands you act independently, but Jupiter in Cancer reminds you that no one survives alone. This square reactivates early April 2025 themes. A confrontation between what you want to build and what’s structurally possible. Look back to April and see what needs to be restructured.

Mars enters Virgo on June 17. This is focused energy. You’re here to be intentional. You’re here to protect your energy by caring for what you can control. Venus is still in Taurus. Venus in Taurus wants to slow down, savor, and feel secure. It’s about consistency, sensuality, and enjoyment. Mars in Virgo wants to improve, optimize, and fix what’s broken. It notices every crack in the foundation and can become restless with imperfection. This transit encourages you to protect your energy by tending to the systems that hold us. The ones you rely on for love, for money, for pleasure. It’s a time to repair, to refine, and to recommit to what keeps you rooted.

The New Moon in Cancer on June 25 invites a new beginning rooted in care. Not just for those around you, but for yourself. With Jupiter amplifying this lunation, the intentions you plant now have room to grow, if you keep tending to them. This is a moment to define what safety means to you. To build structures that support your emotional well-being. To honor the wisdom of your body. Let this New Moon be an agreement between you and your nervous system. A vow to move gently. To choose what feels like home.

Mercury enters Leo on June 26 and stays there until September 2. Yes, there’s a retrograde. Yes, your voice will feel weirder, more exposed. This time is about learning how to speak when you feel invisible. Learning how to lead when no one’s clapping. Stay real to yourself. Don’t go back to talking low just because it makes other people more comfortable.

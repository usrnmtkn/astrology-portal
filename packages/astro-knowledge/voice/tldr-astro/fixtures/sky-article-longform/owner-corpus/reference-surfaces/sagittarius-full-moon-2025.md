Jump to Horoscopes

The Full Moon in Sagittarius is a confrontation with the truth, your truth, the one you’ve been circling like a fire you weren’t ready to touch. Sagittarius, ever the archer, is the adventurous seeker, the one who redraws maps when the old ones lead to dead ends. When the Moon is full in this fire sign, it illuminates the places where we’ve outgrown our own narratives, especially when the story no longer fits or makes sense.

This lunation is the axis of Gemini and Sagittarius. The tension between information and insight. It pits facts against faith, data against belief, local against global. If Gemini asks, “What do I know right now?” Sagittarius replies, “But what does it mean in the bigger picture?”

The signs Gemini and Sagittarius represent different methods of absorbing information and gaining an understanding of the world. The Gemini/Sagittarius polarity is associated with learning, collecting, and assimilating information. As Sagittarius observes the big picture, the sandbox Gemini focuses on the details that make up that picture, the grains of sand. In both cases, the objective is the same: to find the truth. The Gemini Sun collects information. The Sagittarius Moon questions meaning. And when they oppose each other, as they do now, we’re pressed to answer a core question: What do you still claim to believe, and does your life reflect that belief?

In astrology, Gemini & Sagittarius represent the Third House of communication and the Ninth House of truth, forming an axis of the mind. Through this axis, you gain a clearer perspective on your life purpose and a deeper understanding of it. During this Full Moon, we have the chance to reflect on our soul's journey and story, asking ourselves, "What story do I want to tell about my life?" Now’s the time to reclaim authorship. To speak the truth you've been editing out. To let go of the limiting narratives that shrink your potential. This is about choosing. Choosing meaning. Choosing direction. Choosing yourself. Use this Full Moon to release the limiting stories you tell yourself, making way for great spiritual and mental expansion.

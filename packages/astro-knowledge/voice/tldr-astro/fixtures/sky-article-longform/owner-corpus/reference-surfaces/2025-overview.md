Capricorn season begins, offering us the structure and discipline to build on the lessons of the past year. This solstice is a moment of quiet power, a reminder that even in the darkest times, there is potential for growth.

Capricorn’s grounded energy helps us focus on long-term goals and practical steps. What legacy do you want to create? How can you honor the work it takes to bring your dreams into reality? The Winter Solstice is a celebration of resilience, a time to honor the light within as we prepare for its gradual return.

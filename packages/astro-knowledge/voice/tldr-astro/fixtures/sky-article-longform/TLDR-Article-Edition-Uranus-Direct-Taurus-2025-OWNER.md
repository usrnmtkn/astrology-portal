Uranus direct in Taurus 2025
JAN 31, 2025

planet-uranus, taurus

On January 30, 2025, at 11:22 AM EST, Uranus stations direct at 23º15’ of Taurus, ending a fivemonth period of retrograde; it arrives just hours after the New Moon in Aquarius, a sign ruled by
Uranus itself.
Uranus is the planet of disruption, innovation, and liberation. When retrograde, its shocks are
internalized. It turns its rebellious nature inward, challenging us to confront where we’ve been
clinging to outdated structures out of comfort or fear. Since August 28, 2024, Uranus has been
reworking our relationship with stability, security, and material resources, themes that Taurus holds
sacred.
But Taurus is fixed earth. It does not welcome change: it resists it, holds the line, and builds walls to
keep it out. Uranus doesn’t ask permission to break through those walls. Uranus in Taurus has been
shaking up financial systems, supply chains, ownership, and our relationship to nature, food, and
the body itself. The shifts we’ve seen in these areas, globally and personally, have not been random.
With Uranus now direct, what has felt like background noise becomes louder. Some of this will play
out in real-time. Financial markets may respond. Climate patterns may demand our attention.
Personal breakthroughs, particularly around money, self-worth, and what we truly value, arrive with
force. We cannot pretend we don’t see the elephant in the room.
The last time Uranus was in Taurus (1934–1942), the world was still reeling from the Great
Depression. Economic systems were collapsing, and in their place, new structures were born,
including the U.S. Social Security Act, a revolutionary policy that reshaped the way people thought
about financial safety. When Uranus moves through Taurus, money doesn’t work the same way
anymore.
Now, we are in another period of upheaval. The old ways of earning, spending, and saving are
becoming obsolete. Cryptocurrency challenges traditional banking. The gig economy and social
media influnecers redefines what it means to work. Universal basic income is a conversation, not a
fantasy. The ground we once stood on financially isn’t as solid as we thought. Uranus in Taurus
forces us to rethink security itself. What have we been told we need in order to feel safe? A stable
job? A house? A savings account? Uranus strips it all down to the bones. If the structures we’ve
built our security on disappear, what’s left? What do we actually value?
This isn’t the first time we’ve seen this pattern. From 1850 to 1858, Uranus moved through Taurus
and brought upheaval to wealth, security, and movement. Railroads expanded, trade routes

opened, cities grew. Entire industries were born, and others became obsolete. The way people
understood distance, connection, and economic power changed overnight. This is what Uranus in
Taurus does. The structures we once relied on are breaking. What comes next depends on what we
choose to create.
The fact that Uranus stations direct just hours after the New Moon in Aquarius adds an extra
charge to this moment. New Moons are beginnings, and in Aquarius, they speak of collective
progress, technological advancements, and the courage to stand apart. Uranus doesn’t just destroy,
it reinvents. If something is crumbling, let it. If something new emerges, explore it.

Uranus first entered Taurus in 2018
It briefly moved into Taurus from May 15, 2018, to November 6, 2018, before retrograding back
into Aries. That was just a preview. The real work began when Uranus re-entered Taurus on March
6, 2019, where it has been disrupting, innovating, and upending the Taurus-ruled realms of money,
resources, stability, and material security ever since.
Taurus resists change. Uranus does not ask for permission. It has been breaking apart outdated
systems: and challenging how we earn, spend, and sustain ourselves. Redefining ownership.
Reshaping the economy. And on a personal level, forcing each of us to confront what we rely on,
what we value, and what truly sustains us.
This transit isn’t over yet. Uranus will remain in Taurus until April 26, 2026, before shifting into
Gemini. Until then, the work continues.

Notice where things have felt sluggish since late August. That’s where the momentum will

Ask yourself: Where have I been resisting change? Why? The answer will tell you what you’re
most afraid to lose.
Where were you in May 2018? What changed?
What major shifts happened around March 2019?
How has your relationship with money, security, and self-worth evolved since then?

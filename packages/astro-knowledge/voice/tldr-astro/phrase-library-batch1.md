## Lint status
All entries: one sentence · 5–12 words · no em dashes · no fragments · no repeated word within the sentence · no "performing" outside Leo contexts · no status assumptions · no sky intentions · everyday vocabulary, stranger test passed. Rejected during drafting for rule violations: "Reread it before you send it" (repeated word), "Being loved and feeling loved are out of sync" (repeated word), "You can stand out without standing alone" (repeated word), "Not every silence means something is wrong" (echoed an approved canon line too closely).

**Natural-pairing rule (added after review, Jul 17):** every verb must act on an object people actually pair it with in speech. You reply to messages, not thoughts. You fix problems, not feelings. Days don't move you forward; progress does. Test each line by asking whether anyone would say it across a table. Corrected in this pass: "Not every thought deserves a reply" → "Not everything you think needs to be said" · "A quiet day still moves you forward" → "A quiet day still counts as progress" · "Energy is cheap today" / "energy to spare, so use it well" → "Put today's extra energy into something that matters" · "A twelve-year chapter is starting over" → "A new twelve-year chapter starts now" · "The plan is foggy" → "The plan looks blurry because it's still forming" · "Routine could use one small break" → "a small shake-up" · "identical from inside" → "the same from the inside."

**Editor ruling (Jul 17):** "Some feelings just want to be felt, not fixed" was approved and restored. The natural-pairing rule bends for established emotional idiom — "sit with a feeling," "feelings want out," "felt, not fixed" are how people already talk about feelings, even though the grammar is figurative. The rule targets constructions nobody says ("reply to a thought," "energy is cheap"), not idioms everybody says.

**"Today" rule (added Jul 17):** "today" appears in a title only when it carries the meaning ("Today feels heavier than it actually is," "The thing you've been putting off gets easier today"). Everywhere else the card's surface already says it's about today, so the word is a crutch — removed from six titles this pass. The three any-day borderliners were reworked on content instead of keeping the crutch: "Say yes today, within reason" → "Things are leaning your way" · "Optimism looks good on you today" → "It's a good time to ask for what you want" · "People are reading your face before you speak" → "Whatever you're feeling, it's showing."

**Any-day test (added Jul 17):** if a title would be equally true on any random day, it isn't carrying the transit. "Use today's extra energy on something you actually care about" failed — it's generic advice with the word "today" stapled on. The fix is to find the transit's distinctive fact and put that in the line: an easy Mars day means resistance is low, so the transit-specific message is "today is the cheap day to do the heavy thing" → "The thing you've been putting off gets easier today." A passing title is checkable by tonight. Apply this test to every future entry alongside the stranger test and the natural-pairing check.

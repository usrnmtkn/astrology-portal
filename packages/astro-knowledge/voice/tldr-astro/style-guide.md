# TLDR Astro Master Style Guide

One guide, two voices, routed by surface, built on a real Hellenistic interpretive engine. Natal content is warm. Sky and transit content is direct and practical. Relationship content has its own register, not yet fully built.

The deeper purpose of this guide is to stop word soup at the source. Word soup happens when a writer lists what the symbols mean instead of saying what they do when combined in one specific configuration. The cure is not better prose rules. It is interpreting condition, not just sign and house. A planet does not mean the same thing in its own sign as it does in its fall, or angular as it does cadent, or in sect as out of sect. Read the condition and the copy becomes specific on its own.

This file replaces the prior blended guide. Preserve any operational, surface-specific mechanics from the previous version that are not reproduced here (sky timing formats, relationship plumbing). This guide governs voice, interpretation, and content rules.

## Voice routing

| Surface / block family | Voice |
| --- | --- |
| Natal sign, house, ruler blocks | Natal (warm) |
| Natal aspect blocks | Natal (warm), evergreen |
| Sky sign / season blocks | Transit (direct) |

| Transit-to-natal aspect blocks | Transit (direct), personalized |
| Synastry / composite aspect blocks | Relationship (interim) |

Route by surface and block type. Never blend. A natal page is never the transit voice. A sky page is never the warm natal voice.

## Universal rules (all surfaces)

- Second person. Plain language. One idea per sentence.
- No em dashes anywhere. Commas or periods.
- No backend, source-row, or system language. The reader never hears about content keys, blocks, or generation.
- No fate language: no soulmate, no "meant to be," no "divine timing," no destiny.
- No mental health diagnosis. No predicting events as certainties.
- No moralizing closers: not "this placement asks you to," not "this aspect teaches you," not "the lesson is," not "it's not about X, it's about Y."
- Describe tendencies, not fixed traits. The reader always has agency.
- Name the actual subject. Never use a vague placeholder like "the thing," "the tender thing," or "the careful thing" to stand in for something you could name. If you cannot name it, you have not interpreted it yet.
- Never use the word "leak" or "leaks" for emotion or expression. Use surfaces, shows, comes out, spills.
- Cadence is full sentences with rhythm, not clipped fragments. Avoid strings of stacked imperatives or command-list sentences ("Send the message, define the terms, organize the facts"). Directness comes from complete thoughts with a subject, context, and consequence, not from chopping the line into instructions.
- Concrete is not the same as lived. Do not line up a family example, a workplace example, and a relationship example as proof that a theme applies everywhere. That is an administrative inventory, even when every noun is specific. Build pressure, movement, and consequence inside one charged sequence. One scene that changes is better than three representative scenarios.

## The interpretive engine

This is how to find the one specific claim. Work the factors in Hellenistic priority order and let condition modify the base meaning. Each factor below is tagged with which block may use it, because a factor is only reusable if it is fully determined by that block's inputs.

### 1. Planet to sign to house, never sign to personality
A planet is the actor. The sign is how it acts. The house is where it acts. Read in that order. "Mars in Aquarius in the 9th" is Mars (drive, assertion, cutting) behaving in an Aquarian way (detached, contrarian, principled) in the arena of belief and travel. Not "Aquarians are independent."

### 2. Essential dignity (determined by body and sign, so it belongs in the SIGN block, reusable)
Dignity is the single biggest lever for making a sign block specific instead of generic, and it is fully reusable because it depends only on the planet and the sign.

- Domicile (own sign): the planet acts in its own nature, with ease and authority. It does what it does well and without apology. Sun in Leo, Moon in Cancer, Mercury in Gemini and Virgo, Venus in Taurus and Libra, Mars in Aries and Scorpio, Jupiter in Sagittarius and Pisces, Saturn in Capricorn and Aquarius.
- Exaltation (honored guest): amplified, sometimes to excess, slightly idealized. Strong but can overreach. Sun in Aries, Moon in Taurus, Mercury in Virgo, Venus in Pisces, Mars in Capricorn, Jupiter in Cancer, Saturn in Libra.
- Detriment (opposite domicile): out of place, working against its grain, effortful. It still acts, but the style costs it something. Write the friction.
- Fall (opposite exaltation): undervalued, undermined, the planet's gifts go unrecognized or come out sideways. Write what gets missed or discounted.
- Peregrine (no dignity): no clear footing, adaptable but unanchored, takes its cue from whatever it aspects or whoever rules its sign.

The sign block should read the planet through its dignity. Mars in Aries (domicile) is clean, direct force. Mars in Libra (detriment) is force that hates to be the one who breaks the peace, so it acts late or through others. Same planet, opposite condition, completely different lived copy. That contrast is the cure for soup.

Modern planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) carry no essential dignity. Read them by sign behavior and by house, and as aspect partners. Do not assign them rulerships.

### 3. Angularity and house joys (determined by house, so it belongs in the HOUSE block, reusable)
Where a planet sits by house changes how strongly and visibly it acts. This is reusable because it depends only on the house number.

- Angular (1, 10, 7, 4): prominent, active, the planet shapes life directly and others see it.
- Succedent (2, 11, 8, 5): steady, supporting, the planet builds and sustains.
- Cadent (3, 12, 9, 6): more internal, background, processed privately, less on display.
- Joys, where a planet is especially at home: Mercury in 1, Moon in 3, Venus in 5, Mars in 6, Sun in 9, Jupiter in 11, Saturn in 12. A planet in its joy works through that house with unusual fluency.

The house block should read the planet's prominence through angularity. The Sun angular in the 10th is a life lived in public. The Sun cadent in the 12th is a self that forms in private and may stay there. Different copy, from the house alone.

### 4. Sect (determined by the whole chart, so SYNTHESIS or per-member only, never a reusable block)
Day chart (Sun above the horizon) or night chart (Sun below). This sets the tone of the malefics and benefics and cannot live in a reusable sign or house block because it needs the whole chart.

- Of the sect of day: Sun, Jupiter, Saturn. Of the sect of night: Moon, Venus, Mars. Mercury takes the sect of its solar phase.
- The benefic of sect (Jupiter by day, Venus by night) helps most cleanly. The malefic of sect (Saturn by day, Mars by night) is the manageable one, disciplined rather than destructive.
- The out-of-sect malefic (Mars by day, Saturn by night) is the harsher actor and where the real strain tends to show.

When the synthesis or a per-member read has the chart, let sect color the tone. An in-sect Saturn structures and matures. An out-of-sect Saturn restricts and chills. Do not write sect into a reusable block.

### 5. Solar phase and applying or separating (chart or ephemeris only, never a reusable block)
- Cazimi (within about 17 arcminutes of the Sun): empowered, in the heart of the matter. Combust (within about 8 degrees): obscured, overwhelmed by the Sun, hidden in plain sight. Under the beams (within about 15 degrees): partly veiled. These need exact degrees, so they belong to per-member or instance reads.
- Applying (the faster planet moving toward the exact aspect) means the matter is building and coming. Separating means it has crested and is fading. Natal aspect reusable blocks cannot know this, so they describe the standing pattern. Sky and transit instance cards get applying or separating from the ephemeris and must use it: applying reads as gathering, separating as releasing.

### 6. The dispositor chain (the synthesis layer)
Every planet answers to the ruler of its sign. Follow that thread to where the ruler sits, and the interaction between the two is almost always where the single specific claim lives. This is the synthesis card. See the modular rules below.

### Deriving the one specific claim
Put it together: take the planet, run it through its dignity (sign block), its prominence (house block), and for a per-member read its sect, phase, and dispositor. The specific claim is the sentence that could only be true for this exact combination of those. If your sentence would survive swapping the sign, the house, or the dignity, it is filler. The claim usually sits on a tension: a planet strong by dignity but weak by house, a benefic out of sect, a drive planet in a sign that hates its own method.

## Natal voice (warm)

The way you talk to someone you respect and do not need to perform for. Tender without being soft. Validating without flattering. Never therapy-speak, never clinical, never fierce.

Core moves:
- Open on a recognizable lived experience, not a label.
- Weave the astrology in plainly so the reader can trace any claim back to the planet, sign, house, ruler, or condition, without being lectured.
- Name the gift and the pressure point together, without judgment. The pressure point is the overuse of the gift.
- Close on recognition, not advice and not reassurance.
- Present tense. One idea per sentence. No stacked hedging.

### Per-body texture (so warm does not flatten every planet into the same voice)
The warmth is constant, the subject is not. Let each body keep its nature.
- Sun: identity, vitality, what you are oriented around. Steady, central, a little proud.
- Moon: needs, moods, what soothes and what unsettles. Tender, fluctuating, honest about comfort.
- Mercury: how you think, talk, sort, and connect ideas. Quick, curious, exact.
- Venus: what you value, want, and find beautiful or worth choosing. Warm, relational, drawn toward pleasure and worth.
- Mars: drive, anger, how you go after things and defend yourself. Direct, with heat under it.
- Jupiter: belief, growth, where you reach and trust. Expansive, a little restless for more.
- Saturn: limit, duty, time, fear, mastery. Serious, slow, earns its conclusions.
- Uranus: where you break pattern and need freedom. Sudden, contrarian, electric.
- Neptune: where boundaries dissolve, longing and imagination. Soft-edged, yearning, easily fogged.
- Pluto: power, depth, what you bury and what consumes you. Intense, private, transformative.
- Nodes: direction, not drive. North as the unfamiliar growth pull, South as the familiar default.

### Sign-specific intelligence, not vague virtue language
Do not flatten signs into adjectives or moral virtues. Each sign has a way of perceiving, organizing, protecting, testing, or disrupting reality. Name that mechanism.

- Aries detects where movement has stalled and acts before consensus arrives.
- Taurus detects what is worth keeping, feeding, repeating, and making real.
- Gemini detects the moving parts of a thought, conversation, or system.
- Cancer detects emotional weather, memory, belonging, and what people need before they say it.
- Leo detects where life needs authorship, courage, visibility, and creative heat.
- Virgo detects the flaw in the process and the practice that could make it cleaner.
- Libra detects imbalance, social contracts, aesthetics, and the cost of keeping peace.
- Scorpio detects power, secrecy, attachment, threat, and what is buried under the polite story.
- Sagittarius detects the larger frame, the belief system, the doctrine, the map, and where meaning is being stretched too far.
- Capricorn detects the structure, standard, hierarchy, responsibility, and price of ambition.
- Aquarius detects the frequency of the collective: the group agreement, the future being proposed, the system people are living inside before they understand what it asks of them.
- Pisces detects permeability, longing, dreams, escape, devotion, and where the boundary has softened.

Avoid vague virtue words as the center of a claim: truth, authentic, honest, alignment, purpose, expansion, growth, healing, freedom, power, wisdom, potential, transformation. These words can appear only when the sentence has already named the concrete mechanism. Prefer "what people are agreeing to," "what the group requires," "what the belief system organizes," "what has to be muted to belong," "what the structure rewards," or "what the pattern costs."

### Aquarius register
Aquarius is not a synonym for quirky, detached, or unique. Aquarius is signal intelligence. It hears the frequency of a group, platform, belief system, movement, technology, or future before everyone else knows what they are tuning into. It notices the agreement under the language, who gets included by the system, who has to perform belonging, and what kind of person the future being promised asks everyone to become.

When Aquarius appears in a natal block, write through collective pattern and system pressure:
- "You may notice when a group, belief system, school of thought, or spiritual language is asking people to agree before they understand what they are agreeing to."
- "You are not questioning ideas because you like being different. You are trying to understand which ideas organize people, which ones limit them, and which ones create room for a different kind of future."
- "Aquarius can hear the frequency of the future before everyone else knows what they are tuning into."

For Aquarius in the 9th house, the 9th house makes the signal philosophical, educational, spiritual, and doctrinal. It is about the ideas people organize their lives around, the systems that teach people what to believe, and the cost of inheriting a worldview whole.

### Register check, same placement three ways
- Too clinical: "This placement correlates with deliberative decision-making and risk-aversion."
- Too fierce (wrong voice for natal): "Stop stalling and calling it discernment."
- Right: "You like to know how something works before you trust it. That patience protects you from a lot of bad calls, and now and then it costs you the opportunity that needed a faster yes."

### Structure
2 to 4 short paragraphs. Vary the order and entry point every time. Never run a fixed observation, then gift, then caution sequence.

### Worked exemplars

Sign block, dignity carrying the specificity. Mars in Cancer (fall):
"Your drive does not come at things head on. Mars in Cancer wants the goal but hates the confrontation, so you tend to move sideways, through care, through protecting someone, through patience that can look like passivity until you realize how much you were holding the whole time. The force is real. It just runs underground. The cost is the slow burn: anger you tuck away until it surfaces as mood, or a want you defend so indirectly that no one knows you were fighting for it."
Why it works: it reads Mars through its fall (indirect, undermined directness) rather than listing Cancer keywords. It would not survive being swapped to Mars in Aries.

House block, angularity carrying the specificity. Saturn in the 10th (angular):
"This is the part of you that is built in public. Saturn in the 10th puts your sense of duty and your fear of not being enough right out where the world can see it, in your work, your reputation, the role you carry. You take responsibility seriously because it is visible, and visibility raises the stakes. The gift is real authority earned slowly. The weight is feeling that your worth is only ever as current as your last result."
Why it works: it reads Saturn through angular prominence (public, visible, high-stakes) rather than defining the 10th house. Cadent Saturn would read inward and private instead.

Natal aspect block, evergreen pair, no dates. Moon square Venus:
"Two soft parts of you want different things. The Moon wants what is familiar and safe, the comfort you already know. Venus wants what is lovely and worth choosing, which is not always the same as what is comfortable. So you can find yourself pulled between the relationship or pleasure that feels good and the one that feels like home, and reaching for one can leave the other quietly unmet. Knowing which one is talking is most of the work."
Why it works: it says what the two bodies produce in tension, with one concrete example, no keyword swap, no orb, no now.

### Approved textures (reference for register, not a fill-in kit, and not to be repeated until they become a tic)
- Openers on behavior: "You tend to...", "You may notice yourself...", "Before you commit, you...", "What gets your attention first is..." Each followed by a concrete behavior, never an adjective.
- Holding gift and cost: "The same [strength] that helps you... can also...", "That [quality] protects you from..., and now and then it costs you..."
- Closing on recognition: "What you are really after is...", "Underneath it is...", "The pattern is not..., it is..."

## Transit and Sky voice (direct)

Grounded, direct, emotionally intelligent. The smartest friend in the room, not a guru. Present tense. Names patterns plainly, uses concrete imagery, tells the truth without softening, and stays practical: what is happening, what it feels like, what to do with it.

Core moves:
- Lead with the truth, not a warmup. Name the pattern, then the cost.
- Concrete over abstract. Pull from universal lived experience, a message sent and not answered, a conversation that loops in your head, waiting longer than you expected. Avoid location-specific imagery.
- Anchor in the body and in behavior: the held breath, the second-guessing, the coping move and what it costs.
- Direct "you" throughout. End on a line that is concise and undeniable.

### Name the actual form of self-reduction

Do not use vague shrink or shrinking shorthand when the behavior can be named precisely. Diagnose the move before writing the imperative:

- **Self-negotiation (`self_negotiation`)** means accepting less before anyone has responded. Examples: "Stop negotiating against yourself before you even open your mouth." "Stop pretending a compromise is what you actually asked for." "Stop reducing what you want to seem agreeable." "Stop treating your needs as an inconvenience."
- **Softened certainty (`softened_conviction`)** means weakening how clearly someone speaks. Examples: "Stop softening your stance to keep the room comfortable." "Stop putting a question mark at the end of a conviction." "Stop waiting for permission to sound sure of yourself." "Stop understating what you know." "Stop backing away from what you already know you want."
- **Downplayed desire (`downplayed_desire`)** means pretending a desire or need matters less. Use verbs such as downplaying, minimizing, dismissing, pretending not to care, acting as though you do not want, or lowering the volume on what matters. Example: "Stop treating your hunger like a bad habit you're trying to break."
- **Reduced ambition (`reduced_ambition`)** means disguising power, drive, or aspiration. Examples: "Stop translating your drive into something less threatening." "Stop making your ambition sound smaller than it is."
- **Avoidance disguised as caution (`avoidance_disguised_as_caution`)** means giving fear a more respectable name. Examples: "Stop using safety as an excuse for standing still." "Stop calling your reluctance patience when it is really avoidance."

These are not synonyms. The editor and judge must identify the correct mechanism rather than collapsing all five into generic self-erasure language.

### Craft discipline
- Verbs do the work. Use exposes, tests, clarifies, recalibrates, surfaces, breaks. Avoid soft abstract verbs like heals and reveals.
- Cut filler. No "this energy," no "right now" as a throat-clear, no "the universe is."
- Metaphors are diagnostic, not decorative. They should expose a hidden dynamic in concrete physical terms (drained, buried, folding into origami, the grip, the wall), not float as imagery. No ethereal metaphors. No dance metaphors.
- Name at least one specific coping behavior, not a category. "Rewriting the message instead of sending it," not "self-doubt."
- Permission is a move, not a phrase. Give the reader one permission they think is forbidden, but never use the literal words "permission slip."
- Conditional reveals are powerful but overused. "If you've been..." is fine sparingly. The banned contrast constructions below are not.
- The smartest friend in the room, not a guru. Street-smart and direct, never mystical for its own sake.
- Spelling and terms: write magick, not magic. Name self-sabotage as self-harm rather than softening it to self-punishment or self-abandonment. No cursing.

### Structural moves (use, do not stack all at once)
- The shadow callout: name the specific coping behavior, then what it costs. "You keep rewriting the message instead of sending it, and calling that being careful."
- Permission: give one clear permission the reader thinks is forbidden. "You are allowed to not have an answer yet."
- The closing line: one short, true, screenshot-worthy sentence. "Not every delay is punishment. Some are protection."
- Fourth wall, reserved for major transits only (returns, eclipses): a direct aside that drops the format. Use sparingly, never on a routine daily aspect.

### Timing is required and comes from the ephemeris
Sky and transit are time-based and must never fake it.
- Sky aspect cards state current signs, orb, and forming or separating from the ephemeris: "Mercury in Cancer sextile Mars in Taurus, forming." Use applying as gathering, separating as releasing.
- Transit-to-natal cards add what the transiting body is touching in the member's own chart, by natal sign and house. Personalized, present, practical.
- A time-based card with no real timing data is a bug.

### Scope note: cards versus long-form
This guide governs the app's short content: sky and transit aspect cards, sign and season blocks. Long-form lunar and seasonal articles (new and full moon write-ups, season overviews) use the same voice but carry extra structure that does not belong in a card: the six-month manifestation arc, full-moon callbacks to the new moon six months prior, axis integration work, ritual and altar correspondences, and sign-by-rising breakdowns. Keep that machinery in the dedicated lunar template, not in aspect or sign cards. A sky aspect card is a few tight sentences, not an article.

### Worked exemplars

Sky aspect, time-based, collective. Mercury in Cancer sextile Mars in Taurus, forming:
"For the next few days there is an easy line between what you feel and what you are willing to do about it. Mercury in Cancer reads the room and registers what is actually going on under the surface, and Mars in Taurus is slow to start but hard to stop once it commits. The sextile is forming, so this is building rather than behind you. It is a good window for the honest conversation you have been circling, because right now you can say the difficult part clearly and still follow it with action instead of letting it sit."
Why it works: real signs, real aspect, forming used as building, and it names what the conversation is about instead of standing on a placeholder. The cadence flows in full sentences rather than clipped fragments.

Transit-to-natal, directional and personalized. Saturn transiting your natal Moon:
"Saturn is sitting on your Moon, and the mood of the season is heavier than usual. The part of you that needs comfort is being asked to grow up a little, to need less reassurance and more structure. It can feel lonely, like the usual soothing is not available. It is not punishment. It is the part of you that runs on feeling learning to also stand on something solid. Build the small steady routines now. They hold when the mood does not."
Why it works: directional (Saturn to the Moon, not the reverse), present, names the felt experience and the practical move.

## Relationship voice (interim)

Synastry and composite need their own register, not yet fully built. Until it is:
- Second person about the two people or the relationship itself. Synastry is a dynamic between two charts. Composite is the relationship's own shared pattern.
- Direct, plain, no therapy-speak, no fate or soulmate language, no em dashes.
- Do not reuse the natal kit. The natal voice is built for one person and is wrong here.
- Flag relationship blocks for a dedicated voice pass rather than treating this as final.

## Preserved operational mechanics

These mechanics are product rules from the previous guide. They do not override the voice routing above.

### Content modes

Use different writing modes for different product surfaces.

Feed mode is for quick Sky cards, daily feed items, circle updates, and short timely prompts.
- 1 to 2 paragraphs.
- Immediate, social, and specific.
- Gives one practical move.

In-Depth mode is for long-term transits, natal placements, friend profiles, compatibility details, and Go Deeper pages.
- 3 to 5 paragraphs.
- Keep factual astrology labels when supplied by the content row. Put the human theme in the summary or body.
- Use date ranges when timing matters.
- Name the area of life being activated only when the facts provide it, in plain words (work, home, relationships, money). Do not use the phrase "life area," which is banned everywhere.
- Use "you" for the user's chart, a name or pronouns for friend profiles, and "you and {partnerName}" for relationships.

Article mode is for collective astrology, New Moons, Full Moons, eclipses, and seasonal essays.
- Full essay length.
- More lyrical and collective, while still concrete, source-backed, and free of vague motivational language.
- Lunar and seasonal articles may use ritual, altar, axis, callback, and manifestation-arc machinery only when the dedicated template supplies it.

### Page pattern and field logic

Every generated interpretation should be built from the same editorial chain:

```text
event type + current astrology facts + source meaning + voice layer = one authored paragraph set
```

The default user-facing structure is:

```text
Headline
What the reader may notice
Why the astrology produces it
What is useful to do or recognize
Timing, when timing is real
```

The headline should stay factual and astrological when the row is an aspect, placement, transit, season, retrograde, or lunation. Use `Mercury square Neptune`, `Gemini Season`, `Moon in Aquarius trine Uranus`, `Pluto retrograde`, or `New Moon in Cancer`. Do not turn the headline into a mood title unless the content row is explicitly asking for an editorial card title.

The summary and body carry the human interpretation. Start with observable experience, then show the astrology, then name the useful recognition or action. Put extra technical explanation in the drilldown when the surface supports it.

Use generated rewrite examples as field logic, not as copy:
- `observableExperience`, `observableTendency`, and `observableCurrentActivation` become what the reader may notice.
- `baseMeaningRewrite`, `symbolicStory`, and `tldr` become why it is happening.
- `shadowPattern`, `pressurePoint`, and `whereItCanBecomeDifficult` become what can get messy.
- `bestMove`, `whereItHelps`, and `closingReflection` become what to do or recognize.
- `readerFacingSummary` becomes the pacing model for the summary.

Do not expose these field names to the user.

### Surface routing mechanics

Sky content is current weather. Write about the moment, the day, the season, or the active transit. Do not write it as a natal personality trait. Sky headlines stay factual and astrological.

You content can describe stable chart patterns, current transits to the chart, and natal tendencies. Keep it personal and specific, but avoid defining the person as fixed.

Transit content should tell the reader what is being activated, how long it matters, and what to do with it. Transit-to-natal content must preserve direction.

Bonds content should describe the dynamic between two people. Do not write two separate natal descriptions and stitch them together. Name what happens when the charts interact.

### Content resolution and display

How a placement page selects what to show, in order. Each step falls through to the next when it has no live content.

1. Authored block for the exact key (sign, house, ruler, aspect).
2. Generated block for that key.
3. Legacy alias that maps an old key into the new modular key.
4. Fallback frame, last resort, so a section never renders blank.

Only rows with status LIVE are loaded for display. Draft, reviewed, archived, and error rows do not reach the reader. This matters for per-member generation: a freshly generated draft is not visible until it is set live or auto-published, so the display path must account for that rather than assuming a draft will show.

Authored content wins over generated. Generated wins over alias. Alias wins over fallback. Do not remove the fallback until real modular coverage is confirmed for a full chart, or sections will render empty.

### Title style

Titles may name a human theme when the surface asks for an editorial title, but factual content rows keep factual astrology headlines. Good editorial title patterns include:
- Relationship Metamorphosis
- Sensitive & Adaptable
- Hard Worker & Dependable
- Find Happiness Within
- Conversations Blur Today
- Turn Scattered Ideas Into Action
- Common Ground
- Expectations

Avoid title patterns like:
- Mercury Square Neptune Interpretation
- Current Sky Placement
- Source-Backed Transit
- Planetary Weather

## Banned lists

### Universal (all voices)
- Em dashes. Backend or source language. Mental health diagnosis. Event prediction as certainty.
- Fate language: soulmate, "meant to be," "divine timing."
- Moralizing closers: "this placement asks you to," "this aspect teaches you," "the lesson is."
- Vague placeholders: "the thing," "the tender thing," "the careful thing." Name the actual subject.
- "leak" or "leaks" for emotion. Use surfaces, shows, comes out, spills.
- Soft or mystical filler words: profound, whisper, tapestry, reckoning, dance, weave, woven, shrink, self-erasure, journey, sacred, alignment, vessel, container, hold space, transmute, alchemize, integrate (unless literally about moon phases).
- Banned phrases: truth bomb, cosmic permission slip, permission slip, editing your truth, voice shakes, performing normalcy.
- No cursing. Not fuck, shit, or fucking, ever.
- Spiritual platitudes ("everything happens for a reason"). Soft-focus healing ("gentle reminder").
- Spelling: write magick, not magic. Name self-sabotage as self-harm, not self-punishment or self-abandonment.
- Inviting or universe constructions: "this is inviting you to...", "the universe is asking you to...", "this is an opportunity to...", "what if this challenge is actually...", "consider that perhaps...", "notice how you might be..."
- The contrast-reveal constructions, which are overused: "this isn't about X, it's about Y," "it's not about X, it's about Y," "not as punishment but as foundation," "this challenge is actually preparing you for...," "what feels like limitation is really liberation." State the certainty plainly, then the real choice, without the reversal scaffold.
- Word replacements to reach for instead: for "performing," use borrowing, overcompensating with, going through the motions of, showcasing for proof. For "cage," name the actual source of limitation (someone else's timeline, your own perfectionism, a standard that was never yours). Pick the specific behavior over the abstract label.

### Natal voice only
- "you are" as an identity statement.
- The gift-then-work closer: "The gift is ___. The work is ___." in any variant. Gift and caution are content woven into the body, never a fixed final paragraph.
- Single words, never use: this defines you, broken, trauma response, healing journey, inner child, nervous system, perform, activates, integration, life area, themes, energy.
- The templated ruler sentence: "Because {Sign} starts the {House} house, {Ruler} rules this part of the chart. In [name]'s chart, {Ruler} is in {Ruler Sign}..." Say what the ruler link produces, do not announce it.
- Defining the planet, sign, or house like a textbook before interpreting.
- Treating the Ascendant like a planet.
- The skeleton tells: "These are not just background circumstances," "develops, reacts, and finds stability," "When these conditions are supported... When they are strained or neglected..."
- The keyword-swap aspect frame: "can make it easier to move between [X] and [Y]."
- "has to be read alongside" / "has to be understood alongside" as the only link between two factors.
- Hated openers: "There can be...", "You may recognize this as...", "At its best, this gives...", "When it becomes difficult...", "This often works through...", "The useful question is...", "Care often feels most believable when..."
- Tic watch: "you may notice" and "you may find yourself" are allowed but must not open multiple entries in a row.

## Modular block rules

Each block stays in its lane, and each may use only the condition factors its own inputs determine. This is what keeps global blocks reusable and what lets condition deepen them without breaking reuse.

- Sign block ({body} in {sign}): how the sign colors the body as behavior, read through essential dignity. May use dignity (domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall, peregrine), because dignity is fixed by body and sign. May not use house, ruler, sect, phase, or aspects.
- House block ({body} in {house}): where the body operates, read through angularity and joy. May use angular, succedent, cadent, and house joys, because those are fixed by house. May not use sign, ruler, or chart-specific condition.
- Ruler block ({body} as dispositor): the connective only. The ruler's own sign and house come from its reused Sign and House blocks, routed by the member's chart.
- Synthesis (per member, optional): the only place sign, house, ruler, sect, and phase combine into one chart-specific claim. Must depend on the real ruler position and may use sect and solar phase here. If it would read fine for anyone with this body in this sign, it is a sign block by mistake.
- Aspect families: natal (symmetric, evergreen, warm, standing pattern only, no applying or separating), sky (symmetric, time-based, direct, ephemeris timing required), transit-to-natal (directional, time-based, direct, personalized), synastry (directional, evergreen, relationship), composite (symmetric, evergreen, relationship). Directional families never canonicalize order.

Nodes take no dignity. Read them as direction. North Node as the unfamiliar pull, South Node as the familiar default, the sign coloring how that direction shows up. Nodes never take a retrograde note.

## Word-soup diagnostic

Before any card ships, check it against the failure it exists to prevent. It is soup if:
- It lists what the symbols mean instead of what they do together.
- It would survive swapping the sign, the house, or the dignity without changing.
- It connects two factors and stops, instead of saying what the connection produces.
- It hedges every sentence ("may," "might," "can") until nothing is claimed.
- It closes on a formula (the gift/work closer) or on advice.
- A reusable block reaches for chart-specific condition it cannot know (sect or phase in a sign block), or a time-based card has no real timing.

If any of these is true, the fix is almost always the same: go back to the condition. Read the dignity, the angularity, the sect, the applying or separating, and write the one thing that is only true because of them.

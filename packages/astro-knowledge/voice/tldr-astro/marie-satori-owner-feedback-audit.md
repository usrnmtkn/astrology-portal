#### OW-001 — Uranus in Cancer v3

**Authority:** Exact owner approval for collective Current Sky judge calibration only.  
**Scope:** Calibration-only; noncanonical; not generation evidence; not production approval.

**Tagline:** Change who carries it

**Hook:** The person who remembers every birthday, keeps the spare key, and gets called when someone needs help may stop doing all of it. Uranus in Cancer changes the private rules around home and family, especially the ones that only worked because one person was always available.

**Lived:** Uranus spends roughly seven years in a sign, long enough to pry open the quiet rules nobody admits are rules. A sibling stops hosting every holiday, a guest room turns into an office, and the emergency contact list gets rewritten because the person everyone relied on is no longer willing to be the only plan. What looked like connection turns out to be heavy obligation, and home changes shape the moment the old arrangement demands more than anyone should give.

**Turn:** Everyone may agree that the old setup was unfair and still expect the same person to fix it. They make the schedule, explain the new boundary, remind everyone what changed, and take the complaints when someone else is inconvenienced.

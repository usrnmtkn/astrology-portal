# Sky Placement Flatness Audit — 2026-08-02

## Decision

The six-card tuning set is not ready for promotion. The four previously advancing candidates remain mechanically clean, but the lived sections repeat a generated coverage shape that the owner has already rejected: three peer examples lined up to represent different parts of life without enough pressure, movement, or consequence.

This is a system-level voice failure, not a request for the owner to keep correcting individual lines.

## The recurring pattern

The drafts often mistake specific nouns for lived writing:

1. Name the transit pace.
2. Supply three examples separated by commas or semicolons.
3. Move from one category to another: work, money, relationship, household, public life.
4. Summarize the theme.

The result can be accurate and mechanically clean while still reading like generated coverage. Owner voice builds a pressure, lets it change a choice or relationship, and lands the consequence. One charged sequence carries more voice than three representative scenarios.

# Aspect-pattern authored tables v1 (token values - v3.5 contract locked)

Two 10-row tables plus one three-row outer-planet table supply values for the
locked v3.5 tokens. Primary planets only; Chiron held for secondary participation.
Sign and house remain separate resolved inputs so the narrative stays chart-specific.
You-voice, non-fatalist, no banned words, no em dashes.

## focal-demand-by-planet  (Kite: focal.focal_demand / focal.focal_interruption)

focal_demand fits "...{focal.planet} ... adds {focal_demand}." (a noun phrase, no
internal "that"). focal_interruption fits "...is where {focal_interruption} turns
up." and "...has to hold up under {focal_interruption}." (singular head noun).

| planet  | focal_demand | focal_interruption |
|---|---|---|
| Sun     | a real reason to be seen, not just volume | the moment the work has to stand on its own |
| Moon    | a check on whether it feels safe, not only exciting | a wave of feeling the plan did not expect |
| Mercury | a thought-through version, clear enough to explain | the need to explain it and answer the questions |
| Venus   | proof it is wanted and worth the cost | the question of whether it is really wanted |
| Mars    | real effort behind the enthusiasm | resistance that wanting cannot skip |
| Jupiter | a promise sized to what you can deliver | the gap between the promise and the delivery |
| Saturn  | a standard, a delay, or a plain fact | the pressure of deadlines and repeated effort |
| Uranus  | a form that survives being done differently | a sudden change that scrambles the plan |
| Neptune | a reality check under the wish | the point where clear detail goes soft |
| Pluto   | an honest look at what the easy version buries | pressure that will not let the shallow version stand |

## pattern-narrative-by-planet

base_contribution completes "you may ___" across every pattern. The remaining
fields are Yod-specific: lived_need completes "{planet} brings ___," and the
sentence fields are complete reader-facing thoughts without terminal punctuation.
These values replace independent placement dumps with one lived narrative while
the sign and house tokens keep the result chart-specific.

| planet | base_contribution | lived_title | lived_need | incomplete_first_answer | returning_lived_example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | know what deserves your full commitment | The solution keeps leaving your own priorities out | a need to recognize yourself in the choice | An answer can satisfy everyone else and still feel like a role you agreed to play | The subject can return when you feel overlooked, underused, or disconnected from what you actually want |

## background-anchor-by-planet

background_anchor is a noun phrase used only when Uranus, Neptune, or Pluto appears
in a base, corner, or opposition-end group. It locates the slow planet in the chart
without treating its sign as a personal need.

| planet | background_anchor |
|---|---|
| Uranus | disruption and the need for a different form |
| Neptune | ideals, uncertainty, and what remains hard to define |
| Pluto | pressure, power, and what has to change |

## focal-demand-by-planet  (Kite: focal.focal_demand / focal.focal_interruption)

focal_demand fits "...{focal.planet} ... adds {focal_demand}." (a noun phrase, no
internal "that"). focal_interruption fits "...is where {focal_interruption} turns
up." and "...has to hold up under {focal_interruption}." (singular head noun).

| planet  | focal_demand | focal_interruption |
|---|---|---|
| Sun     | a real reason to be seen, not just volume | the moment the work has to stand on its own |
| Moon    | a check on whether it feels safe, not only exciting | a wave of feeling the plan did not expect |
| Mercury | a thought-through version, clear enough to explain | the need to explain it and answer the questions |
| Venus   | proof it is wanted and worth the cost | the question of whether it is really wanted |
| Mars    | real effort behind the enthusiasm | resistance that wanting cannot skip |
| Jupiter | a promise sized to what you can deliver | the gap between the promise and the delivery |
| Saturn  | a standard, a delay, or a plain fact | the pressure of deadlines and repeated effort |
| Uranus  | a form that survives being done differently | a sudden change that scrambles the plan |
| Neptune | a reality check under the wish | the point where clear detail goes soft |
| Pluto   | an honest look at what the easy version buries | pressure that will not let the shallow version stand |

## apex-pressure-by-planet  (Yod: apex.apex_pressure / apex.repeating_question)

apex_pressure fits "...{apex.planet} ... adds {apex_pressure}." (noun phrase).
repeating_question fits "...comes back later with {repeating_question}." (noun phrase).

| planet  | apex_pressure | repeating_question |
|---|---|---|
| Sun     | a pull to be recognized on terms the pair never set | the question of whether you are seen for something real |
| Moon    | an emotional need that keeps not fitting the easy rhythm | the question of whether your needs got counted |
| Mercury | a fact the smooth version keeps missing | the question of what the tidy version left out |
| Venus   | a question of worth and fairness the easy exchange skips | the question of whether it is fair, and what it is worth |
| Mars    | a push to act the comfortable pair keeps deferring | the question of what you will actually do about it |
| Jupiter | a bigger ask the modest rhythm cannot hold | the question of whether you can deliver what you promised |
| Saturn  | consequences that are harder to smooth over | the question of what is sustainable, what is owed, and what you can carry |
| Uranus  | a break from the pattern the other two rely on | the question of what you are willing to change |
| Neptune | a longing the practical answer never satisfies | the question of what is real underneath the wish |
| Pluto   | a depth the surface arrangement keeps avoiding | the question of what you keep avoiding, and what it costs |

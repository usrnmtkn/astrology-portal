# Rejected style pilot

Status: rejected by owner; non-serving; do not import or use as a style model.

Reason: the pilot repeatedly compressed ordinary meaning into polished slogans that do not read like normal English. Passing automated voice scores did not establish owner-voice fidelity.

Owner-rejected examples:

- “We may win the old fight and still lose the next chapter.”
- “The test usually creates the distance it was meant to prevent.”
- “The right opportunity may ask us to grow less at once and go farther.”

Superseding rule: make the writing quotable and immediately clear. Every sentence must first sound like normal English a person would actually use.

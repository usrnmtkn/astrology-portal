## Mars trine Chiron

We can find the nerve to confront unfairness for someone else, then go quiet when the same thing happens to us. With Mars trine Chiron, the words come quickly: stop speaking to them that way, correct the bill, give the credit where it belongs. But our own anger ends with an apology, and a firm boundary gets followed by an exception nobody asked for. Action comes easily; taking up space for our own needs is where the old hesitation slips in.

Because nothing slows this pattern down, it can keep working well enough to escape notice. We solve the problem, defend the person, handle the conflict, and leave our own request unsaid. The same readiness can support a clean no or a direct ask once we stop reserving our courage for everyone else. Asking clearly may cost less than another year of making ourselves smaller.

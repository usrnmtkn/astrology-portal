## TEMPLATE 1: NEW MOON horoscope

Temperature: beginning, planting, desire. The house gets a fresh cycle.
Movements: house landing -> what opens ("opening a fresh cycle in this part of your life")
-> the desire test (education beat: sign + ruler decide WHAT kind of start) -> aspect weave
(what supports, what complicates the start) -> imperative close pointing at the start
itself.
Arc claim (owner ruling): ordinary New Moons mention the lunar arc; the six-month claim
belongs to eclipses only.

## TEMPLATE 2: FULL MOON horoscope

Temperature: culmination, illumination, release. Something built since the matching New
Moon comes to light or comes due.
Every ordinary Full Moon write-up includes the matching New Moon anchor immediately after
the house-specific setup: "Six months ago, consciously or not, this lunar cycle began with
the New Moon in {{matchingNewMoonSign}} on {{matchingNewMoonDate}}." Both values are
calculation-owned facts. The date renders as month and day when the New Moon and Full Moon
fall in the same calendar year, and adds the New Moon year when the cycle crosses a calendar
year.
This is the ONLY template where axis naming is allowed (your standing rule): the opposition
IS the event, so the two houses converse. Your model: "The Cancer-Capricorn axis is a
conversation between care and control."
Movements: house landing WITH its axis partner -> what is illuminated (the tension named as
behavior: "your need for stability versus the transformation that stability actually
requires") -> education beat (what the Moon's sign does, what the Sun's sign answers:
"Here's what Scorpio does: composting... Taurus wants to keep things") -> aspect weave ->
release close (name what gets put down, kept, or finished).
Close (owner ruling): the Full Moon close matches the theme of the Full Moon; the event's
own temperature picks the register, sharp or gentle.

## TEMPLATE 3: ECLIPSE horoscope

Solar eclipse = supercharged New Moon; lunar eclipse = supercharged Full Moon. The base
template applies, PLUS the eclipse layer (from your eclipse sets and today's sample):
- The six-month arc is claimed: "What you plant here over the next few days grows for six
  months."
- The REVEAL beat, eclipse-derived and belonging only here: "This week, the missing
  information arrives, someone gives their answer, or the practical cost of the plan
  becomes harder to ignore."
- Lunar eclipse temperature is collapse-first: "The collapse comes first." / "Virgo doesn't
  ask what you want to keep, it tells you what you can no longer sustain." Endings arrive
  on their own schedule; the horoscope does not soften them into choices.
- Closes are sharp, never benedictions: "If the scale is tipped, stop being the
  counterweight."
(Owner ruling: the solar eclipse is what the event itself is; it begins, interrupts, or
reveals. No desire test; the reader did not choose the event.)

## What the writer must have before drafting any set (fact gate)

Engine facts for the event: exact date/time, degree and sign, aspects to the lunation with
dates; the whole-sign house per rising; active outer-planet placements per rising. Every
aspect sentence traces to a supplied fact. No fact, no sentence.
Ordinary Full Moon packets also require the exact date/time and sign of the verified matching
New Moon. The matching New Moon must precede the Full Moon and share its sign.

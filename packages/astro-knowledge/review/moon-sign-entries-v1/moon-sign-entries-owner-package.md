### Moon in Aries  (tagline: Feel it before sending it)

The Moon moves into Aries on {{entryDate}}. Aries doesn't wait for permission. Irritation shows up before its reason does. The reply goes out while the feeling is still hot.

The Aries Moon strips the delay between feeling and action. For two and a half days, that is the gift; we know how we feel immediately and can say it without a week of rehearsal. Courage comes cheaper. The thing that needed saying gets said today.

The trouble starts in Aries' shadow: it wants what it wants immediately. The first flash gets the final word. A sharp reply starts a fight the feeling did not need, and seeing it from the other side becomes impossible for exactly as long as it matters. Under the impatience there is usually something unacknowledged, and it comes out as anger. Aries doesn't need us to act instantly. It needs us to act honestly.

### Moon in Gemini  (tagline: Name the feeling once)

The Moon moves into Gemini on {{entryDate}}. Gemini wants it talked about. Every feeling becomes a conversation, questions get three follow-ups, and silence starts to feel like something going wrong.

The Gemini Moon opens doors, and asking the questions is how it takes care of itself. It is a time to approach life with playfulness, curiosity, and childlike wonder. For two and a half days, that is the gift. The stuck conversation finds its opening. The right message arrives for the person who needed it. The thing that felt unsayable turns out to take one sentence.

The trouble starts in Gemini's shadow: all talk, no commitment. Plans get made warmly and dropped quietly. The story about someone gets told to everyone but them. Talking about the decision replaces making it. Without grounding, curiosity becomes avoidance. Words become noise. Gemini doesn't need us to say everything. It needs us to say the one thing that has been waiting, to the person it belongs to; and sometimes that person is us.

The Moon moves into Cancer on {{entryDate}}. Cancer pulls everything home. Feelings come in waves, and the waves set the schedule. Who checked in and who did not starts to register.

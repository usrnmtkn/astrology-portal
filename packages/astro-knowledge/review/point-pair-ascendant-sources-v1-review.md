- **lilith/hard**: {{holder1}}'s refusal to soften can make {{holder2}} feel judged for having any polish at all. {{holder2}}'s composure reads to {{holder1}} like hiding, and neither is entirely wrong.
- **lilith/soft**: {{holder2}} does not have to negotiate their edges away around {{holder1}}. What is uncompromising in one makes room for what is unedited in the other.
- **north-node/conjunction**: {{holder1}} pulls how {{holder2}} shows up toward who {{holder2}} is becoming. Stretching, sometimes uncomfortable, often worth it, and never a guarantee.
- **north-node/hard**: Around {{holder1}}, the way {{holder2}} naturally enters situations keeps being asked to stretch. The invitation is real, and so is the tiredness of always being asked.
- **north-node/soft**: Showing up as themselves around {{holder1}} already moves {{holder2}} in the direction they were trying to grow. No strain required.
- **south-node/conjunction**: {{holder1}} feels familiar to {{holder2}} within minutes, and the ease is real. So is the pull toward an older version of {{holder2}} that stopped fitting.
- **south-node/hard**: {{holder1}}'s comfort with who {{holder2}} used to be keeps casting {{holder2}} in an old role. The warmth is genuine, and it argues quietly against anyone becoming new.
- **south-node/soft**: Being known this easily takes no effort from either of them. The only cost is that nothing about it asks {{holder2}} to grow.

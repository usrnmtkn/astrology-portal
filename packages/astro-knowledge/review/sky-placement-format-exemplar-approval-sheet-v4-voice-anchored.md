## 1. Mars in Capricorn — final text

Tagline: The drive is back, and this time it wants proof, not promises.
[Anchors: owner:saturn-enters-aries — "The fire is back. And it's asking what..." (the two-beat
declarative open). Replaces the flatter "finish the work that actually matters."]

Hook: Make the plan, then make it real. Mars governs the part of us that acts, pushes, and fights for
what matters; in Capricorn, it stops waiting to feel ready and starts asking what all this effort is
actually building. The work may be slow, but the aim gets sharper.
[Changed: "stops wasting energy on distractions and puts that force into work we can measure" (project
management register) → anchored in owner:saturn-enters-aries — "It doesn't care if... It asks what
... building." (the transit-that-asks device).]

Lived: From {{transitStart}} to {{transitEnd}}, we may want extra proof that our time is going
somewhere productive: the proposal revised until it holds, the budget faced without flinching, the
difficult task handled before it grows teeth. Motivation looks less like a burst of feeling and more
like returning to the daily work after the novelty has worn off. We may feel most alive when there is
a clear finish line and a solid way to reach it.
[Owner-edited opening line preserved verbatim.]

Turn: Tunnel vision sets in when the mountain is all anyone can see. We push past the point where the
effort serves the goal, and the grind quietly becomes the way we prove our worth. That is not ambition
anymore - that is trying to earn rest we never take. The smart move is to cut the climb when the
summit turns out to be just another place to keep climbing from.
[Anchors: VB-005 "trying to earn," "need to prove"; owner:libra-season — "That's not a real choice -
it's a trap" (the "That is not X - that is Y" construction). Replaces "self-inflicted punishment,"
which is not in the owner's register.]

Moves:
- We can choose one long-delayed practical task and give it a two-hour block with a defined finish.
- We can write the next three steps for a goal that has been living only in our heads.
- We can cancel one obligation that looks productive but only proves we are busy.
[Third move sharpened with VB-005 "need to prove"; first two unchanged.]

## 2. Saturn in Capricorn — final text

Tagline: Building something that lasts should not mean disappearing into the work.
[Unchanged from V3; already carries the owner's inversion shape.]

Hook: Saturn in Capricorn proves that life is not a report card. Saturn handles limits, standards, and
the kind of work that holds its value over time; in Capricorn, it forces us to build from the ground
up instead of chasing quick wins. This transit turns ambition into a structure we can actually stand
inside.
[Unchanged; "report card" is the owner's edit.]

Lived: From {{transitStart}} until {{transitEnd}}, we may choose the training that takes longer,
repair the budget instead of making excuses for overspending, or become the person who keeps a hard
promise when nobody is watching. Work gets more serious because we can see what it could become if we
stop treating every effort like a temporary experiment. The real shift is not doing more - it is
building something sturdy enough to carry the life we are heading toward.
[Unchanged.]

Turn: But competence can become a hiding place. The way one more task replaces the conversation, the
way rest keeps getting postponed until the next milestone, the way the bar keeps rising so no
achievement has time to feel like enough. If nobody can reach us until the work is done, the work is
running our lives.
[Anchors: owner:virgo-season — "The way perfectionism becomes protection against criticism, the way
busy-ness becomes a hedge against feeling, the way fixing others becomes an escape from our own
healing." The V3 version said the same things as one even sentence; this is her parallel-run device.
Closing line unchanged - it already lands.]

Moves:
- We can choose one long-term goal and give it a weekly appointment with a defined stopping point.
- We can write down one responsibility carried alone, then ask a specific person to take a defined
  part of it.
- We can leave work at the promised time once this week, even with more still left to do.
[Unchanged.]

## 3. Neptune in Libra — final text

Tagline: Peace that depends on one person staying quiet is not peace.
[Anchors: owner:libra-season — "This season challenges every dynamic where peace means one person
stays quiet." Replaces "A quiet relationship is not automatically a peaceful one," which said it at
half strength.]

Hook: A relationship can look calm while someone disappears inside it. Neptune blurs the edges
between us, and in Libra it makes fairness, beauty, and mutual understanding feel like needs we cannot
live without. This transit can make the ideal partnership, workplace, or handshake deal feel close
enough to reach for.
[Unchanged.]

Lived: Neptune stays in Libra from {{transitStart}} until {{transitEnd}}, giving ideas about fairness
and relationship time to shape a generation. That long shift shows up in ordinary rooms: the meeting
that keeps circling until nobody remembers the decision, the shared project polished past usefulness,
the couple making every plan together because separate wants feel rude. The gift is real: more
imagination in how we work, love, and make room for another person.
[Changed: dropped "negotiate" from the closing list - it sits on the administrative-register list the
moves lint now enforces, and the sentence is stronger without it.]

Turn: The blur arrives when keeping everyone comfortable becomes more important than telling the
truth. We edit our needs into something harmless, agree to terms we do not understand, and call it
being the bigger person when one of us is simply the only one who adjusts. A relationship that needs
someone's silence to stay peaceful is not peaceful.
[Anchors: owner:libra-season — "Where has being 'the bigger person' become being the only person who
adjusts?" Also replaces "keeping both sides comfortable" ("each side" is on the facilitation list)
with "keeping everyone comfortable." Closing line unchanged.]

Moves:
- We can name one decision we have been softening and state the actual preference in a single
  sentence.
- Before calling a compromise fair, we can ask who benefits if things stay exactly as they are.
- We can make one shared space more beautiful with a choice everyone involved explicitly agrees on.
[Second move rebuilt on owner:libra-season — "the scales are also exposing who benefits when things
stay unbalanced." The V3 version ("what each person is giving up and what each person is getting")
read as mediation-workshop language.]

## 4. Venus in Aries — final text

Tagline: This is the season where wanting stops waiting for permission.
[Anchors: owner:saturn-enters-aries — "It doesn't wait for permission." Replaces "works better than
hinting ever did," which explained instead of declaring.]

Hook: Attraction should not require a translator. Venus governs what we love and what we reach for; in
Aries, it quits circling the subject and goes straight for the spark. This transit makes desire feel
less like a private theory and more like a reason to act.
[Unchanged.]

Lived: From {{transitStart}} to {{transitEnd}}, we may wear the thing we usually save for a better
invitation, buy the ticket before the doubt has finished talking, or be the one who names the
flirtation in the room. Affection gets bolder and taste gets cleaner: less collecting, more choosing.
A small risk can feel strangely life-giving when it lets us move toward what we actually like.
[Unchanged.]

Turn: Chasing the thrill can make attraction feel like something to win. We may pursue the person who
resists us, lose interest once they are available, or keep score over every difference in taste. The
spark is real. Chasing it to prove we can catch it is how we burn through it.
[Anchors: VB-005 "need to prove"; owner:full-moon-in-aries register of short declarative closes.
Replaces the semicolon epigram "Wanting the spark is real; confusing pursuit with love is how we burn
through it" with two plain sentences.]

Moves:
- We can ask someone on a date with a real plan, not a vague invitation.
- We can wear or make one thing that feels too bold for our usual taste.
- Before a quick purchase, we can name whether the pull is the object or the rush of choosing it.
[Unchanged.]

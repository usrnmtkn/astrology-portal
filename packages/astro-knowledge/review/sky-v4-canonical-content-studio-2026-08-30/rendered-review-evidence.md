# Venus in Aries

March 6 to April 1, 2026

## TLDR

**What:** Venus moving through Aries makes desire, attraction, taste, and preference harder to disguise.

**Takeaway:** Wanting something is useful information; it does not have to become a race for the answer.

As Venus moves through Aries, desire does not wait around to become polite. Attraction is immediate, preferences get harder to soften into something more acceptable, and the collective mood is less willing to pretend that wanting less will make the situation easier. This can bring useful honesty to love, money, pleasure, and the things people are finally ready to choose for themselves.

Desire turns urgent here, which is where self-respect matters. Wanting something strongly does not tell you what it will cost or what the terms will be once you have it. Let the preference be honest without turning the answer into a contest. Venus in Aries does not need less desire. It needs enough room to notice what the desire is actually asking for.

Venus retrograde rewrites desire; Mercury retrograde scrambles the signal. In Aries, wanting something and saying something can both happen faster than clarity, so the first answer may not be the final one.

## Node axis

# North Node in Aries / South Node in Libra

During this Aries–Libra node cycle, automatic accommodation gets easier to see and self-direction becomes the less familiar practice.

You may already know how to read the room, soften the disagreement, and find the version of the plan everybody can live with. The South Node in Libra makes that skill easy to reach for, especially when conflict feels more expensive than giving ground. The problem is not consideration. It is how quickly your own preference can disappear before anybody has actually asked you to surrender it.

The North Node in Aries makes the less familiar response more important: say what you want before you negotiate it away, make the first move when waiting has become another form of avoidance, and let a relationship show you if it can handle the person who is actually in it. Aries can move fast, so the growth is not impulse for its own sake. It is learning that self-direction and relationship do not have to cancel each other out.

## Lilith

# Lilith in Sagittarius

While Black Moon Lilith moves through Sagittarius, certainty gets tested against evidence.

Someone has decided their belief is the only fact in the room, and disagreement now sounds like ignorance, disloyalty, or bad character. Lilith in Sagittarius brings old fights around truth, belief, freedom, morality, and who gets to define what is right back into the open. A claim everybody has been repeating as fact gets challenged when somebody checks what it is actually based on. Someone who stayed quiet about a belief for years finally says it publicly.

Belief gives people somewhere to stand. The problem starts when certainty becomes proof. A person can make a very good case and still be wrong. A belief can matter deeply and still need to survive new information. Conviction can give people courage. It can also make them stop checking the facts that would complicate the story. Lilith in Sagittarius exposes what happens when being challenged starts feeling more threatening than being inaccurate.

## Exact owner-register passages used

### 1. Saturn square Lilith

Source: `packages/astro-knowledge/data/transits/saturn-square-lilith.json`

> A rule becomes harder to obey when compliance repeatedly requires the same non-negotiable boundary to be violated. When Saturn squares Lilith, structure and autonomy create friction, bringing duty into conflict with a refusal that is no longer willing to carry the old cost. What looks like defiance can be the point where keeping the arrangement intact becomes more damaging than dealing with the consequence of challenging it. The rule is easier to judge once the cost of obedience is counted along with the cost of refusal.

Why it matters: collective pressure is already present without generic collective-language scaffolding or a personal diagnosis. This passage is locked unchanged in the new packet.

### 2. Mars opposition Lilith

Source: `packages/astro-knowledge/data/transits/mars-opposition-lilith.json`

> Questions of authority become harder to ignore when pressure to act meets a refusal to be managed. When Mars opposes Lilith, a disagreement can keep circling the details while the deeper issue is who gets to decide and whose refusal is being treated as negotiable. Compromise works only when everyone involved still has agency. Exhausting one side until it gives in may end the argument without creating agreement.

Why it matters: the astrology is collective, but the human conflict is immediate and recognizable. The new candidate keeps this argument and adds one conditional reader turn already established by the Calendar style guide.

### 3. Mars square Saturn

Source: `packages/astro-knowledge/data/transits/mars-square-saturn.json`

> Momentum meets resistance when action runs into rules, deadlines, delays, or limits that will not move on demand. When Mars squares Saturn, the push to act can make every constraint feel like a personal obstruction even when the restriction is simply part of the structure. More force is unlikely to make a fixed limit disappear. The useful question is which constraint is fixed, which one can change, and where effort can still produce movement.

Why it matters: stronger aspects do not need exaggerated language. The pressure comes from a clear human problem and a concrete distinction. This passage is locked unchanged in the new packet.

## Voice comparison

The candidates are designed to preserve the sentence behavior visible in the selected owner passages:

- recognizable human condition first;
- astrology mechanism in ordinary language;
- one clear increase in shared pressure;
- concrete consequence or distinction at the end;
- short direct sentences only when they earn the emphasis;
- no forced inspirational close.

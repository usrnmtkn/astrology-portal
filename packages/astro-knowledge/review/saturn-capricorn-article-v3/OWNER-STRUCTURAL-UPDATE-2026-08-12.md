## Standing Saturn planet-education block

Saturn is the farthest planet easily seen without a telescope. Saturn, the Taskmaster, governs time, limits, responsibility, and the work required to make something last. Saturn stands at the threshold where a choice becomes a consequence. Its lessons show up when a deadline arrives, a cost comes due, or a weak structure finally fails.

Saturn takes about 29 years to move through all twelve signs, spending around two and a half years in each. Saturn last moved through Capricorn from December 20, 2017 to December 17, 2020. By the end of that transit, the pandemic had made hidden work impossible to ignore. Hospitals, schools, households, and workplaces all discovered how quickly a system can strain when the people carrying its invisible work are pushed past capacity.

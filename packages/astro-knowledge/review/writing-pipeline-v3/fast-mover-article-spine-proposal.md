## Proposed spine

1. **Planet** — what the planet governs, kept brief.
2. **Condition, when relevant** — include dignity only when it materially changes how directly the planet can do its job. Venus rules Libra, so Venus in Libra receives this home-ground layer, parallel to Saturn in Capricorn.
3. **Handoff** — one opening sentence naming the prior sign and its dates, rather than the standalone contrast paragraph slow movers carry.
4. **Job** — what this planet-sign transit is asking the reader to notice, change, test, or do during its actual window.
5. **Thesis** — the cultural rule or familiar assumption the transit challenges.
6. **Lived evidence** — one coherent sequence of observable behavior and consequence; examples support the article rather than becoming separate mini-scenes.
7. **Failure mechanism** — how the useful impulse becomes the problem.
8. **Strategy** — the practical bias that answers the failure mechanism, without a tip list or coaching scaffold.
9. **Close** — one consequence or choice that completes the thesis. The collective test may be folded into this close when earned; it is not a required separate layer.

## Excluded from the fast-mover spine

- era frame
- recurrence
- older historical analogs
- standalone collective-test paragraph

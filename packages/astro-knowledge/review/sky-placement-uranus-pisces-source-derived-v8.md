## Source-derived paragraph model

Sentence 1: begin with a person, message, cancellation, repeated request, or other ordinary event. Do not begin with “a pattern,” “evidence,” or a thesis about society.

Sentence 2: repeat or deepen the same event through two or three actions that belong to one sequence.

Sentence 3: let recurrence change the explanation. This is where someone realizes the event is not new, exceptional, or solved.

Sentence 4: name what no longer works or what no longer has to be accepted. Keep the close direct; do not force a dramatic metaphor.

## Why the V7 lived paragraph failed

> Uranus spends roughly seven years in Pisces, long enough for separate complaints to expose a pattern.

This begins with the conclusion. The monthly-overview source instead shows repeated phrases and worries before saying that inheritance is larger than expected.

> Someone spends months appealing the same denial and assumes the case is unusual.

This introduces a case file rather than an ordinary circumstance. The Venus-in-Cancer and Chiron paragraphs begin with a recognizable role and what the person repeatedly does.

> Then three families compare letters and find identical wording, identical delays, and identical reasons for rejection.

This is ordered like a report, and “compare letters” is dated. The owner sources use ordinary speech, rest, help, conversations, bodies, and responsibilities. When communication is needed now, messages, screenshots, emails, or notices are the natural objects.

> The unanswered emails become evidence, and what was treated as private bad luck becomes a rule that can be named and challenged.

This explains how an institution should evaluate the cases. The owner paragraphs close on what someone realizes, stops defending, or can no longer continue.

## Uranus-in-Pisces boundary

Uranus contributes disruption, refusal of an old arrangement, sudden recognition, broken precedent, and collective changes that develop across several years.

Pisces contributes compassion, overwhelm, sacrifice, rescue, avoidance, private absorption, blurred limits, grief, and explanations that make recurring difficulty seem unavoidable.

The selected lived pressure is a recurring late-night request. Friends keep responding with real care, but the same crisis returns. Uranus appears when recurrence can no longer be treated as a surprise; Pisces remains present in the sympathy, rescue, and difficulty setting a limit.

This avoids the V7 complaint-to-evidence case study and does not borrow Uranus-in-Cancer’s hidden-family-labor structure.

## Recommendation

## Reader-address boundary for Calendar current-sky copy

Allowed when it stays scope-safe:

- `You may notice ...`
- `You might find ...`
- `If you catch yourself ...`
- `If this is showing up for you ...`
- another conditional construction that helps the reader recognize a possible manifestation without claiming it is happening

Not allowed:

- sentence-start assertions such as `You are ...`, `You have been ...`, or `Your X is ...` that invent a current personal fact
- invented reader history or motives
- invented current arguments, decisions, relationships, or emotional states
- commands whose authority depends on pretending the aspect is personally natalized
- mechanical second person added to satisfy a quota
- generic filler such as `people may feel` when the actual collective condition can be named directly

Summaries remain collective by default. The selective reader-address overlay applies only to bodies where the turn toward the reader improves recognition or warmth.

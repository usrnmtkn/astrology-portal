### 1st house

You may want the name, title, photo, or introduction you use to match who you are now. The Sun in Leo is moving through your 1st house of identity and self-expression, so the name, look, and role you present to other people may feel more important than usual. You may rewrite a bio, change your appearance, claim a title for work you have already been doing, or stop adding disclaimers before anyone has challenged you. Recognition can make it easier to step forward, but it becomes limiting when the response starts deciding how you dress, introduce yourself, or describe what you do. If an old label keeps introducing a version of you that no longer exists, replace it. You are not a product. Now choose yourself.

### 2nd house

You may care more about what you earn, what you charge, and what your money lets you say yes or no to. The Sun in Leo is moving through your 2nd house of disposable income and foundation, making pay, spending, and what you can afford to choose feel more personal than usual. You may ask for a higher rate, spend on a visible upgrade, cover the bill, or compare your numbers with someone who seems to be earning more. Leo can make money feel like proof of value. A slow month is a financial fact, not a verdict on your worth. Ask for the rate that matches the work. If you buy something for pleasure, let pleasure be the reason.

### 3rd house

You may notice yourself wanting to put your name directly on the work, ideas, and messages you share right now. The Sun in Leo is moving through your 3rd house of communication and daily reality, bringing more attention to your voice, your writing, and the conversations that shape your day. You may send the message you kept revising, speak earlier in the meeting, publish an idea, or correct someone who repeats your point without crediting you. Wanting a warm reply is natural. The more you write for the reply, the easier it is to lose what you meant to say. Say what you mean plainly, and let the reply be what it is.

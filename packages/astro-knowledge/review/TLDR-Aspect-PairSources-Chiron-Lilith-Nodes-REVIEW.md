# Aspect pair sources — Chiron, Lilith, and the node axis (needs_review)

The missing coverage behind the 9 unmatched aspects: the generator has no pair sources for these points. Each entry below gives the pair's single conflict per the aspect ruling: two wants, the conflict, behaviors, and a check. Verified against the folder sources (Greene's Chiron in Love for the Chiron pairs, the Black Moon Lilith texts for the Lilith pairs, Rudhyar's Lunar Nodes for the axis frame: North Node as deliberate chosen intake, South Node as release of what was built). The generator applies aspect type and signs on top (conjunction merges the two wants, square and opposition force the choice, trine and sextile let them cooperate, quincunx makes them talk past each other). Point meanings carry from the grounded house-card sources: Chiron is the old break that healed crooked and the medicine it becomes; Lilith is the banished want, the appetite that was ruled out of bounds; the node axis is the chapter's assignment, hunger forward and release backward. Node aspects are written to the axis: contact with one node is always contact with both.

Every write-up generated from these sources follows the Sun-Pluto exemplar: one conflict, event then reaction then reason, no coaching close, conditional language.

## Chiron pairs

**Sun-Chiron.** The Sun wants to be seen; Chiron aches exactly where being seen went wrong before. Conflict: visibility presses on the old bruise, and other people's easy confidence can sting like an accusation. Looks like: the compliment that hurts for no clear reason, the envy of someone who shines without effort, the fierce overwork that compensates for a private sense of not enough. Check: the discomfort may be old information, not current danger, and the envy usually points at a gift waiting to be claimed.

**Moon-Chiron.** The Moon wants comfort; Chiron carries the wound about needing it. Conflict: being tended touches the place that learned not to ask. Looks like: the care that makes someone cry, the need swallowed mid-sentence, the small test set to see whether someone will notice without being asked. Check: accepting the comfort may be the day's actual work, and the unspoken test is not a fair exam.

**Mercury-Chiron.** Mercury wants to say it; Chiron holds the wound about being heard, the old feeling of being the slower or less articulate one. Conflict: the words come close to the tender place and either go quiet or come out with spikes. Looks like: the sentence rehearsed for days, the sharp reply to anyone who sounds condescending, the cheerfulness worn like a mask over the real answer, the conversation that heals more than expected once it starts. Check: the same wound that doubts the voice also digs deeper than most; the imperfect version said out loud may do more than the polished one still waiting.

**Venus-Chiron.** Venus wants to be wanted; Chiron carries the wound about whether that is safe, the standing expectation of rejection. Conflict: affection arrives and the old injury answers the door. Looks like: the sweetness that feels suspicious, the pulling back just as it gets good, the small tests set for loved ones to prove they care enough, which usually backfire. Check: the love may be real even where the doubt is loud, and the test pushes away exactly what it was checking for.

**Mars-Chiron.** Mars wants to act; Chiron holds the wound about taking up space. Conflict: assertion presses on the old lesson that force was not allowed. Looks like: the anger that turns into apology, the boundary stated and immediately softened, the fight picked on someone else's behalf but never your own. Check: the direct ask may cost less than the years of not asking.

**Jupiter-Chiron.** Jupiter grows what it touches; Chiron is both the wound and the medicine. Conflict: expansion makes the sore spot bigger and the healing bigger, and it can be hard to tell which is loudest. Looks like: the growth spurt that surfaces old grief, the teacher whose lesson lands in a wound, the generosity toward pain that used to be hidden. Check: more feeling is not more damage; it may be more access.

**Saturn-Chiron.** Saturn wants the standard met; Chiron aches where the standard was once used as a weapon. Conflict: discipline presses on the crooked-healed place. Looks like: the deadline that summons an old inadequacy, the authority figure who sounds like the original one, the structure that helps once it stops being a verdict. Check: the requirement may be reasonable even if the feeling it triggers is old.

**Uranus-Chiron.** Uranus breaks patterns; Chiron marks where the pattern grew around an injury. Conflict: the sudden change rips the familiar bandage off. Looks like: the disruption that exposes what the routine was protecting, the shock that turns out to free something, the healing that arrives sideways and unscheduled. Check: what got uncovered may have been waiting for exactly this.

**Neptune-Chiron.** Neptune dissolves edges; Chiron needs the wound to keep its edges to be tended. Conflict: personal pain blurs into everyone's pain. Looks like: the sadness with no clear owner, the compassion that turns into carrying strangers, the escape that medicates instead of treats. Check: name whose hurt it is before deciding what to do with it.

**Pluto-Chiron.** Pluto excavates; Chiron is the oldest thing buried. Conflict: the deep work reaches the original injury. Looks like: the therapy session that finds the root, the crisis that retells the first story, the power that returns once the oldest fear gets faced. Check: the intensity may be proportional to how long it waited, not to present danger.

## Lilith pairs

**Sun-Lilith.** The Sun wants approval in daylight; Lilith is the want that was ruled unacceptable. Conflict: the public self and the banished self ask for the same stage. Looks like: the persona that suddenly feels like a costume, the outrageous urge in a respectable meeting, the relief when someone else says the unsayable first. Check: the exiled want may be information about the life, not a threat to it.

**Moon-Lilith.** The Moon wants safety; Lilith is the hunger that safety was built to silence. Conflict: comfort and the banished appetite cannot both run the evening. Looks like: the restlessness inside a pleasant routine, the mood with no name that is actually a want with no permission, the comfort eating that stands in for the actual desire. Check: ask what the want underneath would order if it could speak.

**Mercury-Lilith.** Mercury wants to keep the conversation pleasant; Lilith holds the sentence nobody is supposed to say. Conflict: politeness versus the true thing at the edge of the tongue. Looks like: the meeting where everyone knows and nobody says, the message drafted and deleted, the room that exhales when someone finally names it. Check: the unsayable sentence, said plainly once, may cost less than the silence has been costing.

**Venus-Lilith.** Venus wants to be liked; Lilith wants without asking permission. Conflict: likable love versus undomesticated desire. Looks like: the relationship polished for the audience while the real want goes hungry, the attraction that ignores the approved list, the no that arrives with unusual force. Check: desire that gets named can negotiate; desire that stays exiled only erupts.

**Mars-Lilith.** Mars carries the sanctioned drive; Lilith carries the banished rage and the wildness that refuses taming. Conflict: the urge to tame the wild thing versus the wild thing's refusal, in the world and inside one person. Looks like: the small irritation carrying decade-old heat, the fury that surprises its own owner, the battle of wills that is really about who gets domesticated. Check: the size of the reaction may measure the age of the exile, not the size of the offense.

**Jupiter-Lilith.** Jupiter amplifies; Lilith is the want kept in the cellar. Conflict: the banished appetite grows louder under the amplifier. Looks like: the craving that gets loud, the transgression that suddenly seems reasonable, the honest conversation about wanting more that finally happens. Check: the amplifier blurs the ethics too; bigger appetite is not a mandate, and the line that seemed obvious last month deserves a sober look before crossing.

**Saturn-Lilith.** Saturn enforces the rules; Lilith is what the rules exiled. Conflict: the structure and the refusal meet at the table. Looks like: the obligation that suddenly reads as a cage, the compliance that snaps into defiance, the renegotiation that works because the refusal finally got representation. Check: some rules deserve the refusal and some refusals are just old anger; sort them before acting.

**Uranus-Lilith.** Uranus liberates; Lilith has been waiting for exactly that. Conflict: the exile and the revolution recognize each other, and the exit can get theatrical. Looks like: the sudden quit, the dramatic boundary, the freedom claimed overnight after years of silence. Check: the liberation may be right while the timing and the exit speech still deserve a day of thought.

**Neptune-Lilith.** Neptune dresses things in fog; Lilith is the want that hides well. Conflict: the banished desire returns costumed as fantasy, longing, or somebody else's story. Looks like: the daydream with a repeating theme, the parasocial pull, the escape that is actually an unmet want wearing a disguise. Check: follow the fantasy to its plain content; the want underneath is usually ordinary and speakable.

**Pluto-Lilith.** Pluto rules the underworld; Lilith lives there by sentence, not by choice. Conflict: two kinds of buried power negotiate who surfaces. Looks like: the obsession that is really an old exile demanding daylight, the power struggle that repeats an ancient banishment, the reclamation that changes what the person will accept forever after. Check: what comes up wants integration, not revenge.

## Node axis pairs

**Sun-nodes.** The Sun wants to be somebody now; the axis says who the chapter is asking you to become and what to set down. Conflict: current identity versus the assignment. Looks like: the recognition that arrives for the old act, the role that fits the resume and not the direction, the small step toward the hungry side that feels oddly significant. Check: what gets applauded may be behind you; what feels unqualified may be ahead.

**Moon-nodes.** The Moon pulls toward the familiar; the south node is where the familiar lives. Conflict: comfort points backward while the assignment points forward. Looks like: the homesickness for a chapter that is over, the old habit that returns under stress, the new territory that feels wrong precisely because it is new. Check: uncomfortable and wrong are not the same reading.

**Mercury-nodes.** Mercury carries the day's conversations; the axis decides which ones belong to the chapter. Conflict: the comfortable subjects versus the conversation the assignment needs. Looks like: the catch-up call that rehearses the past, the introduction that belongs to the future, the question that keeps arriving from different mouths. Check: the repeated question may be the chapter speaking.

**Venus-nodes.** Venus brings who and what we love; the axis sorts arrivals from the past and the path. Conflict: the attraction that pulls backward versus the bond that belongs forward. Looks like: the ex resurfacing on schedule, the new person who fits the direction instead of the history, the pleasure that either feeds the assignment or postpones it. Check: chemistry is not a verdict; direction is also information.

**Mars-nodes.** Mars supplies the effort; the axis decides which direction builds on itself. Conflict: energy spent refighting the old chapter versus building the new one. Looks like: the battle that is really the previous era's unfinished business, the project that advances the hunger, the drive that finally has an address. Check: effort on the release side may win the fight and lose the chapter.

**Jupiter-nodes.** Jupiter grows what it touches; the axis says which growth is on assignment. Conflict: expansion in every direction versus expansion along the path. Looks like: the opportunity that is big and backward, the doorway that is small and forward, the luck that shows up once the direction is chosen. Check: not every yes serves the hunger; size is not the measure, direction is.

**Saturn-nodes.** Saturn brings the deadline; the axis brings the assignment. Conflict: the chapter's work stops being optional. Looks like: the postponed decision that acquires a date, the structure built for the future instead of the past, the release that finally happens because maintaining it got too expensive. Check: the pressure may be the assignment maturing, not the world failing.

**Uranus-nodes.** Uranus reroutes; the axis holds the destination. Conflict: the sudden detour versus the set direction. Looks like: the disruption that ejects someone from the outgrown chapter, the shortcut nobody planned, the release that happens all at once instead of gradually. Check: the interruption may be the path correcting for lost time.

**Neptune-nodes.** Neptune supplies the dream and the fog; the axis needs a readable road. Conflict: the vision that guides versus the haze that stalls. Looks like: the calling felt but not yet specified, the nostalgia that romanticizes the release side, the direction that clarifies through practice instead of certainty. Check: take one concrete step and let the fog answer it.

**Pluto-nodes.** Pluto is the buried power; the axis runs the chapter. Conflict: the assignment requires power that has to be dug up first. Looks like: the collective question getting personal, the authority reclaimed because the path demands it, the ending that transforms instead of merely closes. Check: keep this consistent with the nodes article's Pluto thread; the two surfaces describe one event.

## Cross pairs

**Chiron-Lilith.** The wound and the exile are one story told twice: what was hurt and what was banished are often the same want. Conflict: tending the injury versus reclaiming the appetite. Looks like: the healing that stalls until the forbidden want is admitted, the reclaimed desire that turns out to be medicine, the shame that dissolves when the two stories are told together. Check: treat the want and the wound as one case, not two.

**Chiron-nodes.** The medicine is on the path: the chapter's assignment runs through the wound work. Conflict: healing on schedule versus healing as prerequisite. Looks like: the old injury surfacing exactly when the new chapter needs the skill it blocks, the helper role arriving before the helper feels healed, the release that is also a treatment. Check: the timing is not cruel; the assignment needed the wound opened to finish closing it.

**Lilith-nodes.** The reclaimed want belongs to the future. Conflict: the exile returns and the chapter must decide its place. Looks like: the banished appetite that turns out to be the hunger the north node meant, the respectable habit revealed as south-node material, the want that stops being a secret and starts being a plan. Check: reclamation serves the chapter when it builds; it serves the past when it only avenges.

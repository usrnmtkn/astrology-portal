## Voice rule

The reader should understand the passage on the first read.

Use plain language such as:

- change your mind
- feel pressured
- keep explaining yourself
- let something slide
- take on more than you wanted
- say what you actually think

Avoid clever abstractions or phrases the reader has to decode.

Each passage still answers:

> **What does {{Name}} bring out in me?**

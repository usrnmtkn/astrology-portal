## Section D: record the owner writer directive (permanent, for future batches)

Record the following, verbatim, as the Sky Placement owner writer directive (prompt core for batch
generation going forward), and add the flagged terms to the watch list: exacting, candor,
ungracious, vitality, attachment (as jargon), reciprocity, preset role, full accounting, rescue

"Begin with the lived behavior, not a sentence explaining the sign or planet. Remove mechanical
transitions such as 'the pressure begins,' 'the same pressure returns,' and 'the pattern appears.'
Replace them with the exact moment the problem becomes visible. Put the action back on the person
instead of using passive phrases such as 'a choice gets made' or 'a task gets reopened.'
Replace formal, therapeutic, corporate, and textbook language with ordinary words. Do not replace
one abstraction with another. Name what someone does, what another person notices, and what becomes
harder afterward.
Avoid mediation language such as 'one side,' 'both sides,' 'neither side,' and 'neither of us.' Use
the person, the repeated behavior, or collective 'we' instead. Limit repeated references to 'the
bond,' 'the connection,' 'pressure,' and 'creative piece.'
Preserve the strongest consequence in each paragraph. Do not shorten a sentence by removing the
reason it matters. Keep uncertainty where astrology requires it, but do not turn possible patterns
into guarantees. Before approving a replacement, check that it sounds current, can be understood on
the first read, and describes a moment someone could recognize from their own life."

## 3. Replacement Sun in Leo article: the owner's season template (exact text)

This supersedes the currently serving Sun in Leo payload. Tokens replace literal dates at wiring
({{priorSign}} family, {{entryDate}}, {{exitDate}}).

After moving through {{priorSign}} from {{priorSignEntryDate}} to {{priorSignExitDate}}, the Sun
enters Leo on {{entryDate}}, bringing more attention to what we are proud of and what we want others to notice. A creative project gets shared before every detail is finished. Someone asks for
the title, credit, invitation, or opportunity that matches the work they have already been doing. We
stop hiding behind "not ready" and put our name on something that matters to us.

Wanting to be seen is not the problem. Recognition can bring confidence back, especially when the
work reaches the audience it was made for. The problem begins when the response starts deciding what
we make next. We check the numbers, reread the comments, and compare our reception with someone
else's. A quiet response feels like proof that the work failed. Someone else's success feels like
lost ground. Soon we are no longer making the work more honest or more interesting. We are making it
easier to approve.

Leo season can show us the difference between being visible and being known. A title may still look impressive and no longer describe the life we want. An
online persona may attract attention while leaving important parts of us out. A creative identity may
become another role we feel required to maintain. We can keep feeding the version that gets
rewarded, or admit when the image has started costing more than it gives.

Praise can give real energy, but it cannot tell us what the work means. Before {{exitDate}}, the
response may change, the audience may move on, or the role may stop feeling convincing. What we name
during Leo season is what Virgo season will ask us to build. The work worth carrying forward is the
work we still mean when the spotlight is off.

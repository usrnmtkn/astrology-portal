# Natal compatibility extraction: Moon pilot

Date: 2026-08-20  
Status: owner-review evidence only  
Serving impact: none  
Compatibility impact: none

## Purpose

Use the strongest sign-specific Moon material already present in the compatibility corpus to prepare clearer natal placement candidates without changing the compatibility page or its source files.

## Read-only source contract

The extraction reads these files without modifying them:

- `tldr-astro-phrasebank/phrasebank/cc-compatibility-writeups.json`
- `tldr-astro-phrasebank/phrasebank/moon-compatibility-library.json`

Their exact SHA-256 values and byte lengths are recorded in `natal-compatibility-evidence-manifest-v1.json`. The regression guard recomputes both values and fails if either compatibility source changes.

The complete manifest accounts for:

- 7 stable planet introductions;
- 84 planet-sign You evidence rows across Sun through Saturn;
- 12 Moon You review candidates in the pilot;
- 12 Moon Friend source-evidence rows retained for later observer-entry authoring.

Relationship synthesis, compatibility verdicts, match labels, and pair-specific comparison paragraphs are excluded from natal extraction.

## Childhood removal

Each Moon sign paragraph contains the same two-part history construction: a sentence beginning `What happened growing up...`, followed by an `If...` hypothesis about parents, caregivers, or childhood conditions. The builder requires both sentences to be present and adjacent, records their exact source text and hash, removes them, and refuses the row if any childhood/history language survives.

The pilot removes 48 sentences total: 24 from the 12 You paragraphs and 24 from the 12 Friend evidence paragraphs. It also changes the two inherited uses of `whether` to ordinary `if` in the review candidate, without mutating the compatibility source.

## You review candidates

The workbook presents the canonical Moon introduction and one direct-reader Moon-in-sign candidate for each sign. It also shows the currently approved natal sentence where one exists, the removed history sentences, the source paragraph hash, the candidate hash, and blank owner verdict/edit columns.

These rows are `needs_review`. They are not approved, imported, served, auto-published, or promoted to the writer corpus.

## Friend boundary

The compatibility Friend paragraphs are useful evidence, but they enter through the chart subject's interior experience. The natal Friend ruling requires observer-entry authoring. For that reason the workbook labels all 12 Friend rows as source evidence only; there are zero Friend natal candidates in this scope. No Friend passage will be generated by pronoun-swapping the You candidate.

## Owner review wall

The owner reviews the 12 You candidates only in this pilot. A later import requires an atomic, hash-validated verdict record. No serving row changes until the rendered wording is explicitly approved.

The remaining six planets stay at evidence-manifest stage until the Moon pilot establishes the accepted editing pattern.

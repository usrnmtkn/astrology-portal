# Sky Placement articles

Load [shared writing governance](../tldr-astro-writer/SKILL.md). This workflow is
for the current-sky placement article surface, not natal copy, Calendar cards,
weekly forecasts or single-contact Personal Transit passages.

Use the article workflow in [writing pipeline v3](../../docs/writing/WRITING_PIPELINE_V3.md)
and the [Sky Placement astrology contract](../../docs/writing/ASTROLOGY_CONTRACT.md).
The ten-line argument core, eight article-spine intentions, slow-mover additions,
and exact-outline owner gate belong to that article workflow. Internal planning
language and spine labels never become reader-copy evidence or sentence templates.

Use the [owner evidence compiler](../../.agents/skills/satori-writer/SKILL.md) and
[article evidence roles](../../docs/writing/SHARED_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md) for
meaning, register, scene, argument and relevant phrase evidence. Select eligible
complete owner passages by quality and relevance. Historical numerical retrieval
floors do not authorize adding weaker evidence. Cross-surface facts and scenarios
do not transfer, and a richer source cannot set a less-informed target's expected
specificity.

For developed long-form natal or Chiron passages, the
[argument-development standard](../../docs/writing/ARGUMENT_DEVELOPMENT_STANDARD.md)
is a reference for their own surface workflow; those passages do not inherit this
Sky Placement workflow merely because they are long.

After drafting, use deterministic checks and return the exact candidate to the
owner. Preserve failed candidates without automatic rewriting. No model prose
gate or judge replaces owner judgment. Keep provider authorization, exact wording
approval and publication authorization separate.

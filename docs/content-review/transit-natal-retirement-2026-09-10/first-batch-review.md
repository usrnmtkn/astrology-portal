## Proposed argument outlines for owner review

These are planning statements, not reader passages. All four share the scope guards above. The personal-node conjunction sources establish the node meanings; the exact Calendar aspect records may inform aspect mechanics but do not authorize personal wording. Square contacts to the two nodes describe the same geometric axis and must remain consistent rather than claim unrelated events.

### Sun opposite natal North Node

- **Thesis:** A familiar way of being capable or recognized can compete with an unfamiliar direction that needs practice.
- **Cultural rule:** Being good at an established role does not mean it should determine every next choice.
- **Transit function:** Bring the Sun's attention, identity, and conscious choice into tension with the natal North Node's developmental direction.
- **Failure mechanism:** Repeating the demonstrated skill because it is easier to justify, while postponing the less familiar step.
- **Lived scene 1:** Accepting another familiar responsibility while leaving a new responsibility untried.
- **Lived scene 2:** Letting an established description of oneself decide an answer before considering the present situation.
- **Lived scene 3:** Returning to an old method when learning a new one exposes a lack of practice.
- **Strategy:** Identify what the familiar competence still contributes, then choose one concrete unfamiliar action that is proportionate to the opportunity.
- **Intended close:** Land on a specific choice or first attempt; no declaration of destiny or guaranteed growth.
- **Scope guard:** No invented biography, fated meeting, career event, sign, house, or prediction of success. Acknowledge the complementary South Node axis without replacing the article with a conjunction label.

### Sun square natal North Node

- **Thesis:** A current choice can reveal a practical conflict between one's established self-description and a direction still being learned.
- **Cultural rule:** A worthwhile next step does not have to fit the old role without adjustment.
- **Transit function:** Explain the square as friction requiring a response, with the natal North Node identifying the less-practiced direction.
- **Failure mechanism:** Treating discomfort or an awkward first attempt as sufficient proof that the new direction is wrong.
- **Lived scene 1:** Being asked to make a decision that the usual role allows someone else to make.
- **Lived scene 2:** Discovering that an existing commitment leaves no room to try a different responsibility.
- **Lived scene 3:** Revising an approach after the first attempt makes a limitation apparent.
- **Strategy:** Name the specific conflict, adjust one expectation or commitment, and test a manageable next step.
- **Intended close:** End on what can be adjusted or attempted now; no motivational verdict.
- **Scope guard:** The friction is an interpretive possibility, not proof of a crisis or a mistaken life path. No fixed life domain or personal history.

### Sun opposite natal South Node

- **Thesis:** A present choice can ask for a different response from the one a familiar role has trained the reader to give.
- **Cultural rule:** Familiarity and competence do not create a permanent obligation to repeat the same role.
- **Transit function:** Explain the opposition through the reader's relationship to the natal South Node's established patterns and skills.
- **Failure mechanism:** Letting recognition for the old role settle a current decision without checking whether the role still fits.
- **Lived scene 1:** Receiving an invitation that requires participation beyond an established supporting role.
- **Lived scene 2:** Being expected to give the usual answer when the present circumstances call for a different one.
- **Lived scene 3:** Using an existing skill in a different assignment without carrying every old expectation with it.
- **Strategy:** Keep the useful skill, name the expectation that no longer applies, and make the present choice on its own terms.
- **Intended close:** End on a concrete difference in the reader's response, not a demand to reject the past.
- **Scope guard:** Stay consistent with the complementary North Node conjunction. Do not reuse the old 'former self/microphone' composition or imply that all familiarity is harmful.

### Sun square natal South Node

- **Thesis:** A familiar response can become less useful when the present situation requires an adjustment.
- **Cultural rule:** A skill that once worked remains a skill; its usefulness depends on the situation rather than repetition alone.
- **Transit function:** Explain square friction through the natal South Node's default strategy and the Sun's present choice or self-expression.
- **Failure mechanism:** Applying more of the same response after the situation has shown what it fails to address.
- **Lived scene 1:** Taking over a familiar responsibility before checking whether that is what is now needed.
- **Lived scene 2:** Repeating an explanation that previously worked while leaving the current disagreement unresolved.
- **Lived scene 3:** Spending effort on the well-practiced part of a task while delaying the unfamiliar part that determines completion.
- **Strategy:** Identify the specific default response and change one part of it; retain the competence that still helps.
- **Intended close:** End on a usable adjustment with an observable result to check, without diagnosing the reader.
- **Scope guard:** Keep the nodal-square axis consistent with the North Node square. No invented trauma, compulsions, separation, or prediction that an old relationship must end.

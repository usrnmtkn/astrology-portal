# Rhetorical-pattern protections — October 6, 2026

Authority: explicit owner request of October 6, 2026, titled “Add three additional prose-pattern protections to the AI writing system.” This is a scoped amendment, not approval of any reader copy. Applies to Daily, Sky, Calendar, Weekly, Seasonal, Natal, You/Friend and other generated reader prose sharing the owner voice rules.

The owner's governing distinction is: “Is this rhetoric carrying meaning, or substituting for it?” Preserve force, rhythm, humor, anger, specificity and useful imagery. Do not alter astrology calculations, source meaning, owner-authored material or approved source text.

## Three separate material blockers

- `CORRECTIO`: performative self-correction that manufactures psychological depth. Test whether the second formulation adds necessary factual precision or a consequential distinction. Meaningful contrast is allowed; the owner's “Not in defeat, but in strategy.” is not an automatic failure.
- `TRICOLON`: dispensable three-part parallelism for polish, decorative natal symbolic triplets or packaged three-command advice. Test what meaning is lost by removing an item. Three required factual categories are allowed. Meaning determines the number of examples.
- `PURPLE_PROSE`: ornament or elevated abstraction replacing recognizable behavior or consequence. Apply the plain-language replacement test. An image that materially clarifies the mechanism and fits the selected owner voice may remain.

Judge the complete passage, including development and conclusion. A substring or punctuation count cannot establish these semantic defects. Lexical signals are triage only; absence of a signal is not a semantic pass.

Preserve overlapping diagnoses with `POLISHED_ASTROLOGY_PROSE`, `ABSTRACT_MECHANISM`, `GENERIC_ASTROLOGY`, `GENERIC_ADVICE`, `VAGUE_CAUSALITY`, `KEYWORD_STACK`, `STACCATO_AI_PROSE` and `HOUSE_STYLE_PHRASE_REPETITION`. Do not collapse the three specific diagnoses into a general polish label. This amendment changes blocking policy for the three specified labels; it does not silently change the prior governance of unrelated diagnoses.

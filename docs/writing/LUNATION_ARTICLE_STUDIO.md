# Lunation article drafts in Content Studio

Open **Content Studio → Calendar Write-ups → New & Full Moons & Eclipses**.
The workspace opens **Dated articles & eclipses** first; **Reusable sign readings**
remains available in the adjacent tab.
**Saved write-ups** opens the existing Calendar New Moon and Full Moon content
entries, including published readings and drafts. Choose **Event readings** in
that library for lunar journal and eclipse passages. The library edits existing
reader sources; the dated writer's saved list contains generation workspaces.
Opening either list does not create, replace or publish content.
The direct library view is
`/admin/content#calendar-writeups?view=lunation-writing&writing=library`.
The dated view is `/admin/content#calendar-writeups?view=lunation-writing&writing=dated`;
old `view=lunations` links resolve to this view.

1. Choose a month and time zone. Calculated events load automatically.
2. Choose the **Moon or eclipse**, then select **Create new draft**.
   An existing workspace shows **Open saved draft** and preserves its saved text.
3. Review the prepared writing plan and select **Create draft**. This explicit
   action approves the displayed plan and starts one paid request. Optional
   changes live under **Adjust writing direction**; update the plan after editing.
4. Edit and **Save draft**, or select **Review & publish** to open the content
   editor. This handoff does not publish the article.

The list and selected draft are separate screens. **Back to drafts** returns to
the chooser and saved list; unsaved changes require confirmation. **Writing
settings** contains shared guidance and the saved-list refresh control.

To replace saved writing, select **Reject & regenerate**, enter a reason, and
choose **Review replacement plan**. The original stays in **Rejected drafts**.
Review the replacement plan, then select **Regenerate draft** to start one new
request. Rejecting alone never starts generation. While writing, the focused
progress screen hides the old text and plan. Returning to the saved draft resumes
the same request; completion opens its persisted result.

New Moons, Full Moons, solar eclipses and lunar eclipses are supported.
**Writing settings → Edit shared writing guidance** opens the same saved profile used by reusable
readings. The reusable view retains the argument editor, journal question and
exact-key saved corrections. See [the reusable writer](CALENDAR_LUNATION_WRITER.md).

**Review & publish** copies a saved dated article into the normal content
editor the first time. Existing reader edits are preserved on subsequent opens.
The server checks the saved workspace version and applies the shared reader-copy and CMS-template validation before inserting a draft. This action neither approves nor
publishes wording. The handoff preserves check findings. Publication recalculates
the event from its dated key and rechecks the final edited copy; factual errors
block publication while voice suggestions remain advisory.

A published `cms/lunation-article/{UTC-date}/{phase}/{sign}` takes priority for
that calculated event. Calendar and the Sky lunar article use one shared source
selector. The Calendar preview shows the full opening paragraph, with Read more
revealing the rest of the same body. A known retired or unavailable publication
cannot expose older wording underneath it. If no dated article exists, the
approved reusable sign reading is used. Dated ruler positions are never copied
to the reusable sign key. Personal rising-sign readings remain distinct.

## Writing contract

The owner's September 27, 2026 request approved applying the developed lunar
reasoning and prose approach to generation for all four event types. That is
approval of a writing approach, not approval of subsequent generated wording.
The executable guidance is `src/astro-writing/lunationArticleInput.mjs`.

- Develop the interpretation from the event's calculated relationships and
  contradictions. Do not append astrology to generic advice.
- New Moons develop the conjunction, season and traditional ruler. Full Moons
  develop the whole opposition and both traditional rulers. Eclipses integrate
  with the corresponding phase instead of stacking unrelated templates.
- Explain the consequence of a choice. Let the reader recognize what changes
  and what matters to the people involved. Allow mixed feelings without
  inventing hidden motives or personal history.
- Replace abstract concluding statements with the observation or consequence
  they skip. Formal vocabulary and stronger adjectives do not repair a missing
  thought. Give technical relationships an explicit subject.
- Retrieve eligible owner-authored lunar paragraphs for phrasing, rhythm and
  emotional depth. Comparisons generated by other models and rejected sentences
  are not positive owner evidence. There is no compulsory paragraph count,
  pronoun opening, myth section or stock theme.

## Sources, persistence and cost

`api/admin/lunation-writing.ts` uses the existing owner authentication and
server-only storage credentials. It calculates event-time Swiss ephemeris facts,
including traditional rulers and selected close major contacts. A Swiss global
eclipse search confirms eclipse classification; proximity to a node alone is
insufficient. Future stations and previous-cycle dates are not supplied.

The canonical writing pipeline uses the `lunation-article` family. Both dated
and reusable writers first retrieve the complete matching
`authored/sky-lunation-macro/{phase}/{sign}` passage from Content Studio. The
owner's October 9 direction permits using elements of this saved writing in
new New Moon and Full Moon drafts. These references take priority over compact
phrase-bank wording and older repository examples. Six eligible same-family
passages and a register reference from the governed index remain supporting
evidence; their existence does not replace the live saved passage.

`loadLunarSavedWriting` also selects the complete Calendar Season write-ups for
the calculated Sun sign. A Full Moon in Aries therefore receives Aries Full
Moon writing and Libra season context. Season originals come from the same
`lunarJournalPackageRecords` used by Content Studio; a saved revision replaces
its exact original key. The latest active draft wins over a live reference.
Archived/deprecated sources are excluded and suppress their older original.
Empty or invalid saved text, a missing matching lunar passage, or a failed
storage read stops preparation instead of silently reverting to short prose.

The source packet retains complete bodies, hashes, word counts, keys, row IDs,
versions, and the selected field. It does not infer owner authorship or confer
reader approval from a status label. Source dates and placements remain
historical writing context, separate from current calculated facts. The dated
opening rule and owner corrections still govern the new draft. The three
compact Sun/Moon/ruler sentences no longer prescribe the displayed argument.

Both plan previews show the full saved references before supporting examples.
Source snapshots bind the plan hash and persist with the dispatched operation;
source edits require a new plan, while polling retains the original request.
No saved passage is rewritten, published, or regenerated by source retrieval.
Reviewed phase, Moon-sign and ruler-placement sources remain background meaning.
Source
hashes, protocol, facts, shared guidance and exact-key feedback versions bind the reviewed plan.
Nested object keys are consistently ordered before hashing; database JSON key
order alone cannot change the fingerprint. Array order and exact text still matter.

Generation calls the configured writing model only after the owner reviews the
plan and chooses Create draft or Regenerate draft. A version-checked reservation precedes the
request. The Responses API result ID is saved and reused by polling, including
after navigating away. An unknown interrupted request cannot immediately retry
or replace a saved article. Manual saves preserve exact text and use version
checks; generation and plan updates cannot overwrite unsaved local prose.

Drafts use the reserved `studio-lunation/` content-key prefix, article mode,
reference lane and DRAFT status in existing generated content storage. The
general generated-content mutation API rejects these reserved keys. Saves and
generation never confer owner approval or publication authorization.

Private corrections come from the existing `studio_writing_feedback` store,
filtered to the exact reader content key. Missing or malformed feedback stops
preparation and generation. Unrelated placement, aspect or reusable-key
corrections are not silently widened to dated articles. The prepared plan shows
the applicable corrections and guidance revision. Changing either invalidates
an earlier generation approval; an already returned candidate is retained.

Deterministic findings are visible for review. They are not a complete semantic
evaluation or proof that a model follows every style direction. Editorial review
of the actual generated article is still necessary.

## Verification

Integration starts at `31d1c7deb` (the reusable writer), one commit beyond main
`8f9fdfc17`. Tests use isolated dependencies, synthetic storage and provider
responses; no paid generation or production content edits are needed.

- `scripts/test-lunation-writing-studio.mts`: four calculated event types,
  exact prompt submission, shared guidance/private feedback freshness, durable
  one-request recovery, saved versions and non-destructive reader handoff.
- `tests/visual/lunation-writing-studio.spec.ts` and `lunation-studio.spec.ts`:
  both views, saved guidance, drafts, unsaved navigation and heading typography
  at 390/1440 pixels in light/dark themes.
- `tests/visual/calendar-lunation-preview.spec.ts`: opening and final text from
  the same dated source in Calendar and the Sky article, plus reusable previews.
- The full Content Studio API suite, reader-copy boundary, deployment source
  packaging, application typechecks and CSS audit remain required.

Implementation and local verification do not establish production deployment
or the quality of an actual provider-generated article.

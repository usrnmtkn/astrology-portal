# Content Studio Ghostwriter guidance

The owner requested applying the installed Ghostwriter skill to Content Studio
AI writing on October 20, 2026 in
[this task](thread:01a15c03-cf10-71e3-b5e9-798c9f87bd3b).

`src/astro-writing/ghostwritingSkill.cjs` contains a versioned adaptation of
Ghostwriter and its anti-ai-writing foundation from
[`cdeistopened/skill-stack`](https://github.com/cdeistopened/skill-stack/tree/6b2dfeb94b719bc458ec4a4487dea1d5ff76d841/.claude/skills/ghostwriter).
Its source revision and complete instruction-file hashes are recorded in the
module; the upstream MIT license is retained beside it. Runtime requests use
this packaged text without requiring a local Codex skill folder or MCPMarket
connection.

The guidance emphasizes complete source understanding, the author's actual
prose, astrological interpretation, connected reasoning and an editing pass
within the existing writing call. Current owner corrections, selected passages,
saved profiles, surface-specific register, length and output contracts remain
authoritative. Generic persuasion frameworks, desire categories, hooks and
quotable lines are optional. Necessary uncertainty, protected text and source
meaning are preserved.

The October 21 adaptation (`ghostwriter/v2-2026-10-21`) makes advice and closing
lines optional unless the requested field or declared format requires them.
Writers should stop when the thought is complete and use ordinary English.
Synastry's shared prompt no longer requires a practical move, warning, or catch
in every passage. These are instruction changes; existing reader prose and saved
profiles are unchanged.

The October 21 owner follow-up uses `ghostwriter/v3-2026-10-21`. It explicitly
follows [the owner's ingress and editorial-rule request](thread:01a15c54-6709-7872-821c-7303fa4383a8), and
retains the shared AI-pattern rule's distinction between material contextual
failures and words or imagery needing review. The full rule already lives in
`rhetoricalPatterns.cjs`; this adaptation does not add another lexical gate or
semantic judge. Revision identifies the missing observation and develops its
meaning, while preserving natural rhythm and warranted uncertainty. It also
keeps editorial reports outside reader fields and sign symbolism separate from
calculated house topics.

The dated ingress authority uses the app's factual headings and position lines
for the technical inventory. Prose fields develop source-supported observations
without requiring every astrological property to be named in each paragraph.
The close can include an earned reflection or action. Dates, transit
selection, public fields and meaning/voice roles remain unchanged. These changes
follow comparison with the complete approved Sky depth passage and the owner's
supplied revision direction; neither rejected candidates nor editorial reports
become positive author evidence. Private writer instructions remain outside this
repository. The complete approved Scorpio style passage is now packaged through
the existing approved-style registry for dated ingress essays only. It supplies
voice and depth; the complete saved guide and original author article guide the
event-led opening. No hook is required at every section, and no chart-counting
exercise is required at the end. See the
[template repair and tests](INGRESS_EVENT_LED_TEMPLATE_REVIEW_2026-10-21.md).
The subsequent [connected-article change](INGRESS_CONNECTED_ARTICLE_REVIEW_2026-10-21.md)
supplies all 14 eligible original Libra overview paragraphs in order and changes
the dated task from independent field filling to one article returned through
the same fields. The complete Scorpio reference remains style-only. The rejected
paid sample is excluded from positive evidence. The owner's later explicit
[benchmark selection](INGRESS_VOICE_BENCHMARK_2026-10-21.md) makes the complete
revised Libra reader article the primary dated ingress style benchmark. Its
approval is limited to voice use. It is assistant-written, supplies no meaning or
current facts, and creates no saved Content Studio draft or publication authority.

The separately installed personal Codex Ghostwriter skill was also adapted to
the owner's editorial rule. It retains the complete private reference, scopes
the Personal Transit roadmap to its own task, and removes conflicting quotas
for quotable lines and blanket hedge removal. The app uses this packaged module,
not that local skill folder, so deployment is still required for runtime changes.

New shared writer/reviser requests receive the guidance at the provider
boundary. Injected draft and revision requests include it when prepared, so it
is part of their existing instruction/request hashes. Horoscope and lunar plan
hashes include the guidance, requiring fresh plan review after it changes.
Daily Calendar preparation also binds it into the reserved request hash.
Stored OpenAI, Gemini
and Claude requests retain their exact previously reserved instructions;
deployment does not alter, replay or upgrade an in-flight request. The two
legacy Claude Content Studio paths receive it as their system guidance.
Planners and reviewers keep their existing roles and policies.

This adds no model stage, automatic rewrite, saved-profile mutation, reader-copy
edit or publication authority. Private writer instructions continue through the
existing governed/private evidence path. The explicitly selected Libra benchmark
and approved Scorpio reference have metadata in the server's approved-style
registry, with hashes, source provenance and ingress-only scope. Complete texts
are supplied through protected `PRIVATE_INGRESS_STYLE_REFERENCES` storage, never
committed to the public repository. Missing or corrupt storage fails before any
dated ingress provider call.

`tests/astro-writing/ghostwriting-skill.test.mjs` checks actual provider payloads,
role isolation, stored-request preservation and revision boundaries. The Studio
article and horoscope handler regressions additionally inspect the dispatched
writing instructions. These fixture checks establish instruction delivery and
safety boundaries, not literary quality or owner approval of future prose.

# Global writing directive (owner-issued 2026-08-15)

Canonical knowledge is locked. Reader copy is surface-specific.
This directive applies to all TLDR Astro writing tasks unless an explicit
owner instruction overrides it.

There are TWO separate layers. Never collapse them.

## A. Astrological knowledge layer

The app already knows the astrology. Canonical knowledge answers: WHAT DOES
THIS PLACEMENT / ASPECT / HOUSE / TRANSIT MEAN?

This layer includes repository equivalents of: AstrologySupport, Mechanism,
canonical placement meaning, canonical aspect meaning, house evidence, source
rows, provenance, source hashes, temporality, identity, directionality,
natal/transit classification, approved source interpretation.

Treat this layer as locked. A writing task must never re-author canonical
astrology unless the owner explicitly authorizes a KNOWLEDGE task.

## B. Reader-facing writing layer

The writing layer answers: HOW SHOULD THIS APPROVED MEANING BE EXPERIENCED
AND EXPLAINED ON THIS SURFACE?

The writer may transform presentation, not meaning. It may: translate
canonical meaning into ordinary language; show recognizable behavior; show
consequences; correct register; improve naturalness; vary sentence structure;
remove stale or awkward language; create source-supported lived scenes; adapt
length and architecture to the surface.

It may not: invent astrology; expand canonical meaning from memory; import
neighboring house or sign material; invent biography; invent motives;
generalize a sign- or planet-conditioned example into universal doctrine; use
reader-facing prose as new source truth.

## C. Temporality and register are deterministic

Determine register from the canonical object before writing.

- NATAL: standing pattern in the person's life.
- TRANSIT: time-bound pressure, opening, event, or change.
- COLLECTIVE CALENDAR: shared sky condition; main copy may avoid personal
  natal framing.
- RELATIONSHIP / SYNASTRY: interaction between two people's placements.

Do not let the writer choose temporality stylistically.

## D. Natal register

Natal placement copy should usually be: direct second person; present tense;
standing pattern; lived; consequence-aware; non-transit; non-collective; not
default coaching.

The reader should feel "this is how this pattern tends to work in my life" —
not "this is what is happening to everyone right now" and not "the astrologer
is giving me instructions for today."

Preferred:

> "You hear the useful part of the question quickly and want to answer before
> the rest catches up. That speed is useful until the sentence you skipped
> turns out to be the part that changes the answer."

Avoid collective/descriptive: "Aries changes Mercury's pace. Long
explanations become harder to tolerate."

Avoid transit/coaching: "Say what you mean and make the first move when the
facts are clear."

Avoid trait summary: "You tend to be direct, quick-thinking, and impatient."

Second person alone is not enough. The prose must show recognizable behavior.

### Target structure (not a rigid template)

Strong natal copy often contains: 1) recognizable recurring behavior; 2) the
mechanism behind it; 3) a lived consequence, cost, or contradiction; 4) a
perspective that sharpens the pattern.

Preferred:

> "You put enough of yourself into an idea that a correction can land like
> someone is correcting you. Then the conversation stops being about the work
> and starts becoming about defending your judgment."

Over: "You are proud of your ideas and may take criticism personally."

### Do not default to imperatives

Natal copy should not routinely end with: Say... Ask... Let... Stop... Try...
Remember... Make sure...

Advice is allowed only when the field or surface explicitly calls for advice.
The default natal close should reveal the pattern rather than coach the
reader out of it.

### Do not force "you tend to"

Standing-pattern copy does not require repetitive phrasing ("You tend to...",
"You often...", "You may usually..."). Ordinary present tense conveys the
standing nature.

### Preserve sign differentiation

Do not let neighboring signs collapse into the same prose architecture. For
Mercury specifically, preserve distinct mechanisms such as:

- Aries: speed; answer can arrive before all information does
- Taurus: what holds up; enough evidence before changing position
- Gemini: multiple lines of information/options moving at once
- Cancer: memory, context, tone, personal relevance
- Leo: identification with the idea; correction can feel personal
- Virgo: precision, revision, missing detail
- Libra: comparison, fairness, another point of view affecting the decision
- Scorpio: what is unsaid, inconsistency, missing motive/fact
- Sagittarius: scale; conclusion can outrun details
- Capricorn: usefulness, structure, what can be acted on or relied upon
- Aquarius: independent thought, systems, unconventional conclusion
- Pisces: associative thinking, intuition, imagery, porous boundaries between
  ideas

These are examples of differentiation, NOT permission to rewrite the
canonical astrology. The canonical knowledge remains authoritative.

## E. Transit / Calendar register

Transit and Calendar copy may describe: what may happen; why it matters; what
changes; what can still move.

For Calendar, prefer message-first reader logic: WHAT MAY HAPPEN -> WHY IT
MATTERS -> WHAT CAN MOVE. Astrology explanation may live in Details after the
forecast. Do not make the reader process planet -> sign -> aspect -> modality
before understanding why the event matters.

## F. Surface strategy

Use one governed evidence contract, but do not make every surface sound
alike. Each surface may have its own length, architecture, scene density,
advice allowance, collective/personal register, headline behavior, ending
behavior, historical depth, and timing requirements.

Retrieve voice examples from the SAME SURFACE first. Do not let Daily cards
become the primary model for Sky Placement articles. Do not let long-form
Saturn essays determine Daily-card cadence.

## G. Write from canonical mechanism, not old copy

When reader copy is weak, stale, rejected, or historically outdated: DO NOT
polish the old prose unless explicitly instructed. Instead:

canonical AstrologySupport / Mechanism -> supported human situation ->
source-supported scene -> consequence -> perspective

Old prose may be audit or reference material. It is not automatically writing
evidence.

## H. Owner-approved voice evidence

Only current, eligible owner-approved prose should be used as positive voice
evidence. Historical approval does not automatically mean
currentVoiceEvidence = true. Review drafts, rejected candidates, old fallback
copy, stale prose, and unapproved rewrite proposals must not enter the
positive voice corpus merely because they exist.

## I. Current writing standard

Strong copy tends to contain: recognizable situation -> behavior ->
consequence -> useful perspective or next move. Not every paragraph must
follow that order. Use natural, everyday language. The reader should not have
to translate the sentence.

Owner-cited good examples:

> "The reply you wanted can still create more work than the silence did."
> "A boundary is easy to write down. The test comes when somebody asks for an
> exception and you still say no."
> "Someone else's pressure can easily bait you into overreacting."
> "Crossed wires only become a confrontation when you start fighting the
> story in your head instead of the words being said."
> "Stop making the decision based on what they say they could do. Pay
> attention to what they actually do after the conversation ends; that is the
> only data that matters."
> "Being vulnerable does not mean giving up your position."
> "The mistake is letting their cancellation take the rest of your day with
> it."
> "Everyone else may have decided what the plan is before you had much say in
> it. That puts you in the position of responding to terms you did not
> choose."

## J. Naturalness rule

Do not flag or rewrite language merely because it is rhetorical, stylish,
compact, unusual, or emotionally direct. Flag it when the reader has to work
harder than the meaning requires. Prefer ordinary verbs attached to actual
behavior.

- Weak "open the statement" -> better "check the account"
- Weak "working harder for a warmth" -> better "trying harder for a response"
- Weak "fix the mood" -> better "smooth it over"

Do not mechanically simplify strong owner language.

## K. Advice rule

Advice is not banned. It must arrive from the problem.

Useful: "Ask what should come off your list if this now takes priority."
Useful: "Get clear on what is actually required, what can still be changed,
and what needs to be clarified in writing."

Weak generic coaching: "Trust yourself." "Choose growth." "Honor your truth."
"Be more aligned."

Natal reference copy should not default to advice. Daily and transit surfaces
may use practical next moves more often.

## L. Source fidelity check before every draft

1. Resolve the canonical target.
2. Retrieve canonical evidence.
3. Confirm temporality.
4. Confirm surface.
5. Retrieve only eligible owner voice evidence.
6. Identify the supported mechanism.
7. Identify supported scene territory.
8. Write reader copy.
9. Validate that no new astrology was introduced.

## M. Hard fail-closed conditions

Do not call the writer if: canonical evidence is missing; target identity is
unresolved; temporality is unresolved; source hashes are stale; required
house or scene license is unavailable; the only concrete scene would require
unsupported invention.

After writing, fail closed for: unresolved placeholders; banned deterministic
language; unsupported source claims; register mismatch; cross-surface
evidence contamination; canonical meaning drift.

## N. Review governance

Writer output is never self-approving. Reader Judge is advisory forever.

Reader Judge may identify: constructed sentence; explanatory-not-lived prose;
abstraction over consequence; invented motive; unearned assumption; metaphor
requiring translation; generic astrology copy; textbook scaffold; batch
furniture.

Reader Judge may NOT: approve, reject, block, rewrite, stage, promote, or
serve. Owner remains the prose gate.

## O. Required change report

For any rewrite task, report separately:

- KNOWLEDGE CHANGES: should be ZERO unless explicitly authorized.
- READER-COPY CHANGES: list exact fields and rows changed.
- SOURCE GAPS: list anything that could not safely be rendered.
- REGISTER CHANGES: list rows corrected from collective/transit/hybrid into
  their canonical register.
- VOICE CHANGES: list awkward, stale, abstract, or constructed wording
  changed.

Confirm: canonical meaning preserved; source fields untouched; provenance
untouched; hashes untouched; no new astrology introduced; owner approval not
inferred; output remains pending owner review.

## Voice rules summary

Use: natural everyday language; concrete behaviors; work, messages,
decisions, plans, timing, relationships, money, or ordinary consequences when
source-supported; direct cause and consequence; varied sentence architecture;
strong endings that add meaning.

Avoid: textbook astrology narration; generic trait lists; therapy language;
invented biography; invented motives; collective "we" unless the surface
requires it; transit timing language; repetitive coaching turns; abstract
growth/meaning/guidance conclusions; decorative metaphors requiring
translation; em dashes; "whether".

## Global principle

The app should never pay a writer to rediscover astrology it already knows.
The knowledge base supplies the meaning. The writing engine supplies the
reader experience.

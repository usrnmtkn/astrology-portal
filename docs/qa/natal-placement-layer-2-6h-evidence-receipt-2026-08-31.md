## Writing target

Each row should move naturally through:

planet operating in ordinary 6th-house life -> recognizable repeated behavior -> consequence -> a distinction that lets the reader understand their own pattern more clearly.

The distinction must grow from the astrology. It is not an appended lesson or motivational closer.

Examples of the intended depth:

- useful vs well used;
- adaptation vs fit;
- understanding details vs having to manage every detail;
- pleasant terms vs fair terms;
- solving a problem vs improving the system;
- generosity vs unlimited capacity;
- reliability vs a system dependent on perfect attendance;
- freedom from routine vs a routine that creates freedom;
- caring about the purpose vs agreeing to every task;
- crisis competence vs authority to prevent the crisis.

## Surface and register

Full natal placement detail, Layer 2 planet-house baseline, second person. Each row describes an enduring natal pattern for one planet operating through the 10th house and must remain valid across all twelve signs.

The 10th house is treated as visible contribution, public responsibility, reputation, accountability, authority, and long-term direction. Employment is one possible manifestation and is never required for the interpretation to make sense. A 10th-house planet is not automatically a planet conjunct the Midheaven.

## House mechanism boundary

The 1st house describes identity as lived and presented, physical presentation, self-directed initiative, and the way a person enters situations. It is not interchangeable with the Ascendant. A planet in the 1st house may strongly color self-presentation and initiative without being authored as a conjunction to the Ascendant.

Cross-house QA rules applied before drafting:

1. House-removal test: the behavior must remain recognizably about self-definition, presentation, initiative, or entering situations after obvious 1st-house nouns are removed.
2. Noun-swap test: replacing identity/presentation/initiative nouns with another house's nouns must break the interpretation.
3. Planet-purity test: the planet must produce the behavior; the house only gives the life function through which it operates.
4. All-sign test: each baseline must remain valid across all twelve signs and leave sign expression available for Layer 3.
5. Angle-boundary test: do not write these as Ascendant conjunctions or first-impression-only copy.

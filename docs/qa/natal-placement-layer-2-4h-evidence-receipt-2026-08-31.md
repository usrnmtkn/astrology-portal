## House mechanism

The 4th house covers home, family and chosen family, roots, privacy, belonging, memory, inherited roles, and the private conditions a person returns to when public demands fall away.

Do not invent a difficult childhood, a close family, estrangement, moving history, a conventional household, parent dynamics, or any other biography from the house placement alone. A natal planet in the 4th describes how that planetary function operates through private life and roots, not what definitely happened there.

## Shared Layer 2 rule

A planet-house baseline must answer:

1. What function does the planet perform?
2. What changes when that function operates through this house?
3. What observable behavior can follow?
4. What becomes useful about the pattern?
5. Where can the same function become costly or distorted?
6. What changes in behavior when the placement is handled more deliberately?

The baseline must remain valid across all twelve zodiac signs. Sign-specific temperament belongs to Layer 1 and Layer 3, not here.

The 10th house in this batch means visible responsibility, public contribution, reputation, authority, accountability, what gets associated with the person over time, and long-term public direction. Employment is one possible manifestation, not a prerequisite.

## Sun in the 10th house

### Source-preserved meaning

Lofthus supplies attainment, achievement, ambition, visible example, power, and the difference between self-serving achievement and contribution that benefits others. The current TLDR contract keeps the visible-direction mechanism but removes guaranteed success, moralized reward/punishment, and the claim that ambition defines every person with this placement.

### Ten-line argument core

1. Sun function: identity, vitality, conscious direction, ownership, recognition, and the wish to participate as oneself.
2. 10th-house change: these Sun concerns become especially visible through responsibilities, contributions, decisions, and roles that can be associated with the person beyond private life.
3. Recognition distinction: being recognized can matter strongly, but the chart does not guarantee praise or require a conventional career.
4. Lived evidence field: putting a name on a project, taking responsibility for a decision, becoming the person expected to represent or lead something, choosing what kind of contribution deserves sustained effort.
5. Useful expression: a visible role can clarify identity because the person has to decide what they actually want to stand behind when other people can see the result.
6. Complication: public response can become too entangled with self-definition, so praise, criticism, a title, a loss of status, or being overlooked may carry more personal weight than the situation itself warrants.
7. Boundary: do not claim they are naturally ambitious, famous, admired, watched, successful, or destined to lead.
8. Boundary: do not make the paragraph about employment alone; school leadership, caregiving/community responsibility, creative authorship, advocacy, volunteering, mentoring, and retirement-era contribution must all fit.
9. Mature operation: distinguish the contribution from the reaction it receives; take ownership of work or responsibility because it represents something the person means to stand behind, not only because it produces recognition.
10. Exact-synthesis handoff: Layer 3 will later explain how the sign changes the way the Sun seeks visibility, handles authority, defines achievement, and responds to public recognition.

### What the replacement must remove from the current approved row

- “Ambition is real in you” as a universal opening.
- The guaranteed external claim that people watch the person more closely than they realize.
- “The trap / the reward” scaffold.
- Moralized “someone worth imitating” ending.

## Venus in the 10th house

### Source-preserved meaning

Lofthus supplies a publicly visible Venus function through friendliness, voice, aesthetics, diplomacy, social ease, and the ability to please or inspire. The current TLDR contract keeps visible relating/value/aesthetic mechanisms while removing guaranteed charm, money, professional advantage, and universal positive response.

### Ten-line argument core

1. Venus function: affection, value, pleasure, taste, attraction, reciprocity, social judgment, and the way a person makes choices about what or whom they want to invest in.
2. 10th-house change: Venusian values, taste, relating style, aesthetics, diplomacy, or social judgment become more visible in what the person contributes and what others may associate with them.
3. Public-value distinction: this can appear through paid work, but it can also appear through art, community involvement, advocacy, hosting, mediation, creative direction, public-facing communication, or the standards a person brings to a visible responsibility.
4. Lived evidence field: noticing how something is presented, caring about the tone or terms of a public interaction, helping two sides work together, attaching a name to work that reflects personal taste or values, being selective about associations because they affect reputation.
5. Useful expression: Venus can make the person attentive to how participation feels and to the relational or aesthetic consequences of public decisions, not only to whether a task gets completed.
6. Complication: approval, attractiveness, politeness, consensus, or a polished image can become too important if preserving them begins to interfere with a necessary decision, disagreement, boundary, or standard.
7. Boundary: do not write generic “people pleasing.” Name the public Venus decision when a concrete candidate is drafted.
8. Boundary: do not promise that charm opens doors, money comes easily, people like them, or a pleasant manner substitutes for skill.
9. Mature operation: let values and relationships shape the contribution without letting approval determine every public choice; visible taste or diplomacy should support substance rather than replace it.
10. Exact-synthesis handoff: Layer 3 will later show how the sign changes what Venus values publicly, how it seeks cooperation or recognition, and which terms feel acceptable.

### What the replacement must remove from the current approved row

- “You meet the world with warmth, and it tends to answer in kind.”
- Guaranteed career-door opening.
- Guaranteed public pleasing/inspiration.
- “Optimism is your professional asset.”
- “real craft underneath the charm” as a generic clever closer.

## Saturn in the 10th house

### Source-preserved meaning

Lofthus supplies self-reliance, perseverance, responsibility, authority, integrity, humility, and consequences around power. The current TLDR contract keeps responsibility, standards, endurance, accountability, and authority while removing guaranteed success/failure, throne/downfall morality, and father/parent biography.

### Ten-line argument core

1. Saturn function: responsibility, limits, time, standards, consequence, endurance, structure, and authority.
2. 10th-house change: these Saturn concerns become visible through what the person is expected to answer for, sustain, enforce, complete, or carry in public life.
3. Authority distinction: authority is not merely a title; it is the ability and obligation to make decisions whose consequences remain attached to the person.
4. Lived evidence field: being trusted with a serious responsibility, being the person who has to make a standard hold, having a decision examined after the fact, carrying work that takes years rather than weeks, becoming responsible for what happens when a plan meets a limit.
5. Useful expression: patience and endurance can build public trust because standards are demonstrated repeatedly rather than announced once.
6. Complication: responsibility can accumulate faster than authority, resources, or support, or the person can start treating carrying more as proof that they deserve the role.
7. Power complication: a standard becomes harmful when the person enforcing it is insulated from the time, money, labor, or consequences it creates for everyone else.
8. Boundary: do not assume a difficult father, conventional career, late success, failure, public downfall, pessimism, or a fixed ambition story.
9. Mature operation: connect authority to accountability, make responsibility visible, and judge a role by what can actually be sustained rather than by how impressive the title or burden looks.
10. Exact-synthesis handoff: Layer 3 will later show how the sign changes the person’s relationship to standards, authority, recognition, patience, and public responsibility.

### What the replacement must remove from the current approved row

- “Career is where life tests you hardest and pays you best.”
- Guaranteed climb/success language.
- “the top” metaphor and moralized success frame.
- Any dominant/absent-parent biography inherited from the source.

## Cross-row regression questions before drafting

- If every reference to a job or employer disappeared, would the meaning still work?
- Could the paragraph be pasted onto another planet with only the planet name changed? If yes, the planet function is not specific enough.
- Does every complication arise from the same planetary function described in the opening?
- Is any external reaction stated as fact when the chart can only support a possibility?
- Has the 10th house been confused with the Midheaven?
- Has any sign behavior leaked into the house-only baseline?
- Would Layer 3 have something meaningful left to add, or has Layer 2 already smuggled in a sign-specific synthesis?

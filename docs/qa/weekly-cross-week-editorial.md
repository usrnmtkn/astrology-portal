# Weekly comparison across editions

The Weekly planner and reviewer previously compared the current twelve signs
without loading prior editions. The repaired path supplies historical comparison
text separately from the saved profile, calculated current facts and voice evidence.

Free plan preparation reads up to four distinct prior Weekly windows within six
weeks in the same timezone. Only complete headline/body passages from saved Draft,
Live or Archived editions are included. Dates, identities, status, exact text hashes
and complete passages are retained; none is designated approved by retrieval.
The bounded query reads edition sections and identity metadata, never provider or
rejection history. Missing history is explicit. Storage failure or invalid/oversized
history stops preparation without a paid call or shortened owner text.

The snapshot stays frozen through the approved batch. Its hash participates in the
plan, cache and shared-check bindings. A new free preparation refreshes history and
invalidates an earlier review when that snapshot changes. Polling an already-sent
request keeps its original input, schema and budget. Legacy approved batches do not
acquire extra paid stages and cannot claim an unavailable historical comparison.

Every planned concern cites supported current event IDs, explains their specific
contribution, develops its own thought and quotes a relevant prior passage when
available. The review supplies twelve individual assessments with exact current and
historical wording and valid event IDs. Separate checks cover recycled prior-week
interpretations, astrology used as decoration and repeated rhetorical scripts.
Findings require quoted evidence; prior-week findings identify both editions.
The sign writer receives current meaning decisions without historical citation
fields; comparison notes are not voice evidence or current facts.
The existing warning treatment displays the earlier quote alongside current text.

A legitimate continuing theme may recur. The current configuration must explain
what develops differently; this is not an emotional quota or a ban on contrasts,
planet names or ordinary grammar. Existing saved profiles, approved examples,
calculation rules, sign output schema and publication approval remain unchanged.
The maximum batch budget remains one plan, twelve drafts, twelve prose checks and
one full-set review. No automatic rewrite or paid retry is added.

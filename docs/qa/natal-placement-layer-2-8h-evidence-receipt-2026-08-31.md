## House mechanism

The 8th house is the part of life where something important stops belonging to one person alone. It includes joint resources, debt, inheritance, taxes and other obligations, intimacy, sex, privacy, secrets, trust, loss, endings, dependency, and agreements in which another person's choices can alter the reader's financial or emotional reality.

Do not use `transformation` as a substitute for explaining what changes. When regeneration or change is relevant, name the arrangement, identity, resource, responsibility, access, or attachment that can no longer remain in its previous form.

## Semantic sources

Primary semantic source: Myrna Lofthus, *A Spiritual Approach to Astrology* (1983), especially the Eighth House outline and individual planet-in-house delineations.

Lofthus supports joint resources, death/regeneration, personal sacrifice, taxes, inheritance, shared responsibility, and planet-specific functions in the 8th house. Her source is not a voice authority. Do not carry forward karmic certainty, past-life causation, guaranteed inheritances or financial outcomes, medical predictions, fixed marriage outcomes, psychic certainty, or moralized ego claims.

Planet-specific source boundaries:

- Sun: identity and self-direction are changed by sharing resources, risk, trust, and power; do not guarantee inheritance or occult ability.
- Moon: emotional response and sensitivity become tied to trust, shared resources, and what another person is carrying; do not predict a partner type or financial gain.
- Mercury: analysis, investigation, questions, and information operate around shared resources, motives, hidden facts, and private agreements; remove medical and gossip claims.
- Venus: affection, value, pleasure, reciprocity, and desire operate where resources or vulnerability are shared; remove guaranteed prosperity and generic laziness claims.
- Mars: action, assertion, anger, investigation, and the desire to get to the heart of a problem operate in shared stakes; remove deterministic financial outcomes.
- Jupiter: faith, generosity, opportunity, and expansion can operate through pooled resources and trust; remove guaranteed gain, legacy, and psychic uplift.
- Saturn: limits, responsibility, patience, restraint, and dependency operate through shared obligations; keep clear terms and shared responsibility, remove karmic moralizing.
- Uranus: independence, change, surprise, and unconventional terms operate in joint resources and intimacy; reject predictions of death, trauma, cheating, or sudden fortune.
- Neptune: sensitivity, projection, blurred boundaries, trust, and honesty operate where resources and vulnerability are shared; reject psychic certainty and deterministic loss/deception claims.
- Pluto: power, control, consequence, financial upheaval, insight, and dominance operate where lives are materially or emotionally entangled; reject clairvoyance and inheritance guarantees.

## Batch test

Each paragraph should answer a different 8th-house question because each planet performs a different function. The reader should be able to remove the planet name and still infer the planet from the behavior. The house should remain recognizable through shared stakes, trust, access, obligation, intimacy, loss, or another person's consequential choices.

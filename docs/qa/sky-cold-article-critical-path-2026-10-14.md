# Guest Sky article critical path

A fresh known-sign Sky placement route already prepares the complete selected
placement snapshot and its writing while the full Sky request runs. The article
previously waited for that full response even after its own facts and source
planes were ready. In the observed production profile, full Sky took about
2.3 seconds; selected calculation and content completed earlier.

The change allows only an established anonymous guest to commit the complete
article from that same validated placement snapshot. Pending/restoring accounts,
saved profiles, remote accounts, auth errors and stored sessions retain the
existing full-Sky boundary. Other routes and member personalization retain their
existing behavior. Full Sky continues loading for the surrounding page.

The existing worker supplies all base positions, the selected full timing
windows, in-sign and retrograde aspect lists, and Moon context. Registry,
source-plane completion, publication identity and final ingress eligibility
still precede the single article commit. Source writing, formatting, arithmetic,
API contracts and selection freshness rules are unchanged.

## Verification

The held-response browser regression checks the complete Sun opening and ending,
New Moon context and dates before full Sky returns. It then waits for the exact
full response to be accepted into the verified cache, observes 30 animation
frames, and compares the whole article including dates and aspect sections.
Saved-profile and delayed remote-profile tests retain the full-Sky boundary.
Existing route abandonment, cold/warm date/location rejection, publication
revision/retirement, formatting stability and personalized parity remain covered.

Full local API/content suites, fresh affected browsers, types, source contracts,
CSS, privacy, all exact-head hosted checks and production acceptance are required.
Production measurements must compare a fresh baseline after any preceding content
publication, verify first and post-full-response complete-body hashes, and retain
stable formatting on direct load and reload. Fixture results are not production
latency claims. No production content writes are part of this release QA.

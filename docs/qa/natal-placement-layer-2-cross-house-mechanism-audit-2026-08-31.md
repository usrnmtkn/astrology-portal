# Natal placement Layer 2: cross-house mechanism audit

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: editorial QA / review-only
Branch: `agent/natal-placement-three-layer-audit`
Scope reviewed: 60 conventional planet-house candidate passages across the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 7th, 8th, and 10th houses.

No serving copy is changed by this audit. Candidate revisions remain `proposed / needs_review`.

## Owner correction that triggered this audit

The owner correctly rejected the opening of Sun in the 2nd house because the prose used money as scenery for an autonomy mechanism:

> With your Sun in the 2nd house, money gets personal because it affects how much choice you have. You care about having enough of your own to make choices, protect what matters, and say no without every decision depending on somebody else's approval.

The problem was not that money was absent. The problem was that the behavior was fundamentally about independence from other people's approval. The 2nd house was supplying nouns while another mechanism supplied the meaning.

## Mechanism-integrity test going forward

A Layer 2 planet-house baseline must pass all of these checks:

1. **House-removal test:** remove the obvious house nouns. The remaining behavior should still be recognizably shaped by that house mechanism.
2. **Noun-swap test:** if the house nouns can be replaced with another house's nouns while the paragraph still works, the row fails.
3. **Planet-purity test:** the planet must explain the behavior. A Sun row cannot become Pluto because control appears; a Venus row cannot become generic relationship coaching; a Uranus row cannot become generic independence detached from the house.
4. **House-function test:** the house changes what the planet is doing, not merely where the examples happen.
5. **All-sign test:** the passage must remain true across all twelve signs and leave sign expression available for Layer 3.
6. **Angle-boundary test:** 10th-house writing may include public role, visible responsibility, accountability, reputation, contribution, leadership, recognition, authority, and long-term direction, but it must not become an MC conjunction by default.

## Conclusion

The audit does not support reopening every previous passage. Most rows preserve both planet purity and house mechanism. The corrected failure mode is now explicit: a house cannot function as a bag of example nouns wrapped around a psychological mechanism borrowed from another house.

Before authoring any remaining Layer 2 house family, run the house-removal and noun-swap tests before the voice pass. Voice quality cannot rescue a row whose astrology mechanism is wrong.

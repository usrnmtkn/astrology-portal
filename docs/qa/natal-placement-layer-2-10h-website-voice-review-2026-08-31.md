## V2 diagnosis

### Sun / 10

Directionally strong and astrologically cleaner than the serving row. The main remaining issue is sentence architecture. The paragraph moves through a balanced sequence of examples -> explanation -> complication -> example -> conclusion. That structure is audible. The website more often lets one central tension carry the paragraph and varies sentence length around it.

The opening phrase `the things attached to your name can become deeply personal` is close to the website's public-name language, but the following four-item inventory makes the construction feel generated. The final line `Recognition matters here, but it cannot be the only evidence...` explains the lesson after the paragraph has already demonstrated it.

### Venus / 10

V2 is the weakest voice match. It contains too many content-design nouns in one paragraph: `wording`, `terms`, `presentation`, `cooperate`, `diplomacy`, `aesthetics`, `social judgment`, `relationship-building`, `goodwill`, `polished image`, `standard`, `association`, `reputation`. The astrology is defensible, but the prose reads like a careful semantic inventory.

The website's Venus writing is more embodied and selective. It chooses a few behaviors that prove the Venus mechanism, then shows what becomes complicated. V3 should use fewer categories and more direct choice/consequence language.

### Saturn / 10

V2 is the strongest of the three. The responsibility/authority distinction fits both current doctrine and the website's Saturn cadence. The paragraph can still be tightened. `Authority can build slowly because the record of what you complete accumulates over time` is correct but explanatory. The strongest section begins with `Carrying more can start to feel like proof...` because it shows the mechanism turning into a costly pattern.

## Cross-row voice ruling

The V2 set is too uniformly engineered. All three paragraphs visibly move through the same authoring logic. For V3:

- get to the human stake faster;
- reduce category inventories;
- let the strongest behavior do more explanatory work;
- use short sentences only where they genuinely land;
- do not append a lesson after the consequence is already clear;
- keep natal possibility language without making every sentence sound qualified;
- preserve the three-layer astrology boundary and do not import sign behavior into Layer 2.

## Website-source boundary

Several older website pages describe the 10th house primarily as career/professional life and sometimes make claims about people recognizing, rewarding, or responding positively to the reader. Those passages remain valid owner voice evidence, but they do not override the current doctrine that Layer 2 must work without employment and cannot guarantee external reaction.

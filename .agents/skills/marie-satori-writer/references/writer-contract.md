# Marie Satori writer contract

## Purpose

Write and revise TLDR Astro copy so every sentence is observed, natural, exact, and authored. Passing lint is necessary but insufficient.

Use only the provided astrology facts and governed evidence. Never invent a domain because it is convenient. Never treat an unapproved candidate as proof of voice.

## Before drafting

Identify:

- one lived pressure at the center of the article;
- recognizable behavior that proves it;
- the choice made under pressure;
- the consequence;
- the contradiction the turn should reveal.

Build one sequence. Do not inventory several possible manifestations.

The writer may invent that sequence from the governed planet and sign meanings inside the listed supported domains. Do not require an existing owner article about the placement or a prewritten owner scenario. Invent the scene, never the astrology.

Warmth, tenderness, permission, emotional intelligence, and moderate lyrical cadence are allowed. Do not solve thematic drift by making the prose colder.

Marie determines what the article notices. When the astrology supports them, prefer owner-recurring pressures: technology, power, gatekeeping, being underestimated or pushed down, access, recognition, work, money, status, control, exclusion, invisible labor, someone else setting the terms, being expected to accept less, and refusing a limiting role.

Collective does not mean advocacy by default. Do not reach automatically for campaigns, institutions denying services, public complaints, organizing, policy reform, advocacy work, social movements, collective healing, community care, or systemic-harm language. Use those domains only when the governed astrology and eligible owner material directly support them.

Write human behavior before or alongside astrology. Do not begin by defining a planet and sign unless the same sentence contains a recognizable event.

## Sentence standard

A sentence is not finished because it is grammatical or accurate.

Rewrite any sentence that:

- summarizes an experience instead of showing it;
- makes an abstract noun act like a person;
- sounds polished but requires interpretation;
- replaces an ordinary action with negotiation, policy, report, or workshop language;
- could belong to several placements;
- uses a category list where one ordinary example is stronger;
- repeats the previous sentence;
- explains a lesson the article already showed;
- turns advice into administrative instructions;
- adds a second conclusion;
- sounds like a competent content writer imitating Marie Satori.

Prefer ordinary nouns, exact verbs, recognizable situations, supported life domains, specific costs, contradictions, and one charged sequence.

Do not confuse simplicity with flatness. A simple sentence is strong when it names the behavior. A sentence is flat when it reports only the topic.

## Current Sky contract

- Use `we`, `someone`, a named group, or a concrete collective subject.
- Never use the generic noun `people`; name who or what is actually acting.
- Never use `you`, `your`, `yours`, `yourself`, or `yourselves`.
- Keep timing proportional to the transit.
- Distinguish planet function from sign expression.
- Do not drift into houses or natal advice.
- Keep moves collective and concrete.
- Keep moving dates, degrees, signs, and motion in the ephemeris layer.
- Use ordinary current wording for communication. Default to `message` or `response`, with `reply` and `what they were told` as natural alternatives. Use email, screenshot, notice, or a physical letter only when the delivery method matters to the scene. Do not modernize `letter` into technical wording such as `portal notification`, `electronic correspondence`, or `automated communication`.

### Continuous Sky Placement fallback

For the current app fallback, write exactly five fields: `opening`, `tension`, `development`, `close`, and `try_this`.

- `opening` shows recognizable ordinary evidence and contains the literal `{{entryDate}}` slot once.
- `tension` names one pressure and shows how the same strength creates a cost.
- `development` continues that exact pressure through practical choices.
- `close` is one realistic choice containing the literal `{{exitDate}}` slot once.
- `try_this` contains two specific actions by default and three only when the third adds something different.

The engine owns the headline, fact line, dates, and approved aspect insert. Do not generate a tagline for this contract. Do not move dates or aspect facts into prose. Every candidate remains `needs_review` and render-ineligible until the owner approves the exact wording and separately authorizes import.

## Internal drafting process

1. Read the ranked packet.
   The packet contains four to six verified owner-authored passages selected by same-sign, same-planet, then adjacent affinity. It must not use a fixed universal six.
2. Confirm that the lived scenario comes from the governed astrology and eligible owner writing.
3. Check whether collective scale led to an unsupported activism or policy scenario.
4. When supported, consider whether technology, work, power, access, recognition, money, or gatekeeping expresses the same astrology more naturally.
5. Write three hook options privately.
6. Reject hooks that open with definition, abstraction, slogan, or atmosphere.
7. Choose the clearest lived pressure.
8. Draft the complete article around one sequence.
9. Read every sentence literally.
10. Replace polished shorthand with the event or cost it represents.
11. Remove repeated beats.
12. Remove the weaker of two conclusions.
13. Strengthen moves so each contains a choice and consequence.
14. Compare the draft with the retrieved owner examples and corrections.
15. Rewrite until the candidate should require minimal owner editing.

## Authorship audit

For every sentence ask:

- Does it make literal sense on first read?
- Does it name behavior, a decision, or a consequence?
- Is an abstraction acting like a person?
- Is it merely explaining the placement?
- Does it sound observed or composed?
- Does it repeat an idea?
- Does it require translation from polished shorthand?
- Could it move to another placement?
- Does it advance the same sequence?
- Is there a stronger ordinary noun or verb?
- Is the cost specific?
- Is the sentence needed?
- Does a communication scene default to ordinary `message` or `response` language when its delivery method is irrelevant?
- If the copy names an email, screenshot, notice, or physical letter, does the scene specifically depend on that medium?
- Is `harm` hiding a less severe or more exact word such as damage, cost, pressure, loss, or what happened?
- Would someone actually describe this dinner, message, plan, or disagreement with these words?
- Is the sentence hiding an ordinary action behind words such as `counteroffer`, `both sides`, `meaningful influence`, `real say`, or a labeled `compromise`?
- Does `tilt` stand in for a change that could be named directly?

At article level require:

- a behavior-first hook;
- astrology that accompanies rather than replaces observation;
- one advancing sequence;
- no lived inventory;
- a behavioral turn with consequence;
- one ending;
- moves with pressure, choice, or consequence;
- no Current Sky second person;
- natural language over polish.
- no advocacy-default subject matter unsupported by the astrology and owner evidence;
- warmth is preserved when changing an unsupported lived domain.
- no stock sign scene; the lived sequence must follow from the combined placement at the transit's actual scale;
- moves use ordinary actions rather than facilitation, negotiation, or project-management language;
- no more than two supplied owner voice devices, and zero is allowed.

Every failed check triggers rewritten wording.

## Output

Return the complete candidate, substantive changes, evidence used, and intentionally preserved lines. Never return diagnosis without replacement wording. Never grant approval or change governance metadata.

# Compatibility cards — Moon (sample for review)

Model: **Shared · Different · Watch · Try**, one line each, composed from authored sign traits + relationship type. Tier voiced-original-grounded, DRAFT. This replaces the app's generated 'water-led rhythm / moon topics' template.

## Moon — You: Scorpio · Them: Cancer  _(same_element)_

*The Moon is how each of you feels safe, soothes, and needs care.*

- **Shared** — Both water Moons read emotional tone quickly.
- **Different** — Scorpio protects deep, private feelings and skips shallow reassurance; Cancer seeks familiar safety in home and chosen people.
- **Watch** — One person may go quiet while the other reaches for reassurance.
- **Try** — Name whether you need space or comfort before reacting.

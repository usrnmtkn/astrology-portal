# Venus signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (Parker DK + Lofthus). **Marie** = your phrasebank
wording (compat gift/shadow); banned words already swapped. Venus = how a person loves, what they find
attractive, and what they value. Write final copy in your voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md and lint every line.

- **Aries** — Lived: loves passionately and on impulse, falls in and out fast, likes the chase; shadow:
  selfish in love, fickle, moves on quickly.
  Marie: "You love fast and direct, and you'd rather chase than be kept guessing, though you can chase the
  spark and lose interest once you've won."
- **Taurus** — Lived: sensual and loyal, wants a bond that lasts, shows love through touch and staying
  power; shadow: possessive, ties love to money and things.
  Marie: "You love steadily and sensually; loyalty, touch, and the good things say it best, though you can
  get possessive and cling to the familiar over growth."
- **Gemini** — Lived: flirtatious and playful, needs conversation and variety; shadow: fickle, bores
  easily.
  Marie: "You love through words and wit, and a good conversation is your love language, though you can get
  restless and need constant novelty."
- **Cancer** — Lived: the romantic; loves tenderly, needs a home and a lasting bond; shadow: clingy, moody,
  easily wounded.
  Marie: "You love by caring and being cared for; closeness means feeling safe with someone, though you can
  smother, cling, and fear being left."
- **Leo** — Lived: loyal and generous, loves grandly, wants to be adored; shadow: needs admiration,
  dramatic, proud.
  Marie: "You love generously and out loud; you want romance with a little grandeur, though you can need to
  be adored and make it about you."
- **Virgo** — Lived: shows love through small acts of care and being useful, modest; shadow: critical,
  reserved, holds back, frets over flaws.
  Marie: "You love through the practical: showing up, fixing, remembering the small things, though you can
  pick at your partner and withhold when it's imperfect."
- **Libra** — Lived: made for partnership; wants harmony, beauty, and someone to share it all with; shadow:
  loses themselves in the other person, avoids conflict.
  Marie: "You love beauty, balance, and partnership itself; harmony is the point, though you can lose
  yourself in the other and avoid conflict at any cost."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: loves all the way in, intense and loyal, nothing halfway; shadow: jealous,
  possessive, controlling.
  Marie: "You love all the way in: intense, loyal, and allergic to anything halfway, though you can get
  jealous, possessive, and controlling in love."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: loves freely and needs room and adventure, generous and fun; shadow: dodges
  commitment, restless.
  Marie: "You love freedom and adventure in a partner; keep it honest and keep it moving, though you can
  dodge commitment and keep one foot out the door."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: loves with commitment and takes it seriously, loyal, slow to give their heart;
  shadow: reserved, treats love as a duty, holds back the affection they feel.
  Marie: "You love with commitment and staying power; you invest where it can last, though you can treat
  love like a duty and hold back your affection."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: loves as a friend first and needs space inside the closeness, unconventional;
  shadow: detached, keeps love at arm's length.
  Marie: "You love as a friend first, and you need freedom inside the closeness, though you can keep love
  at arm's length and stay detached."
- **Pisces** — Lived: tender, romantic, and self-sacrificing, sees the best in whoever they love; shadow:
  idealizes, loses their boundaries, clings or drifts.
  Marie: "You love romantically and boundlessly, seeing the best in whoever you fall for, though you can
  lose your boundaries and idealize whoever you fall for."

# Saturn signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (Lofthus, cross-checked with Liz Greene, *Saturn*).
**Marie** = your phrasebank wording (compat gift/shadow); banned words already swapped. Saturn = where a
person is serious, where they feel not-enough, and how they handle responsibility, limits, and the long
haul. Write final copy in your voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md and lint every line.

- **Aries** — Lived: shoulders it alone, self-reliant and pushing against the odds; impatient, jumps in
  before it's ready; fear: inadequacy; shadow: frustration, going it alone.
  Marie: "You build discipline around action: learning to start wisely, not just fast, though you can rush
  the work and resent the discipline."
- **Taurus** — Lived: patient, stubborn, and hardworking, careful with money and slow to risk it; fear:
  losing what they've built; shadow: rigidity, clinging to money and things.
  Marie: "Your structure is around worth and security; you learn what counts as enough, though you can
  harden into stubbornness and fear of change."
- **Gemini** — Lived: a careful, disciplined thinker who takes their own mind seriously, precise with words
  and slow to commit to a view; fear: not knowing enough, not being taken seriously; shadow: cynicism,
  pessimism, nervous overthinking.
  Marie: "You build discipline around your mind and voice: say less, mean more, though you can scatter your
  focus and dodge the deep work."
- **Cancer** — Lived: guarded and heavy around home and feeling; feels responsible for the people they love
  but pulls back to protect themselves from being hurt; shadow: insecurity, withdrawal, gloom.
  Marie: "Your work is around emotional security: building a foundation you didn't inherit, though you can
  wall off your feelings in the name of security."
- **Leo** — Lived: strong will and a perfectionist grip on creative work; struggles to show affection so
  comes across cold, isolates; fear: not being good enough to be admired; shadow: stiffness, pride.
  Marie: "You learn to earn recognition rather than demand it; earned confidence takes time, though you can
  let pride block the humility the lesson asks for."
- **Virgo** — Lived: exacting, orderly, useful work; reserved, wants to be left alone to do it right; fear:
  it's never good enough; shadow: harsh criticism of self and others, worry.
  Marie: "Your discipline is around service and standards: useful, not perfect, though you can turn
  discipline into perfectionism and self-criticism."
- **Libra** — Lived: serious about fairness and commitment, learns what they owe through partnership; fear:
  being alone; shadow: loneliness, resentment if they dodge relating.
  Marie: "You build structure around commitment and fairness; you learn what you owe and are owed, though
  you can avoid the hard call and over-owe yourself to others."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: tight self-control and secrecy, wastes nothing, loves and hates with the same
  intensity; fear: being exposed or controlled; shadow: holding power too tightly, cutting people off.
  Marie: "Your work is around trust and control: learning what's safe to depend on, though you can grip for
  control and struggle to trust the process."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: independent and principled, a natural teacher who matures late and tests a
  belief before they trust it; fear: being boxed in; shadow: preaching, rigid convictions.
  Marie: "You build discipline around belief: testing your faith instead of borrowing it, though you can
  dodge commitment and call it freedom."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: ambition and self-control, a patient worker who earns the reward and turns ideas
  into results; fear: failure, losing respect; shadow: all work and no play, a grim sense it's all on them.
  Marie: "Your structure is around authority and time; you become your own hard-won authority, though you
  can overwork, go cold, and tie your worth to output."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: principled and duty-bound to the group, works for a cause and guards their
  independence; fear: losing their freedom; shadow: rigid ideals, keeping people at a cold distance.
  Marie: "You build discipline around vision: turning ideals into things that actually hold, though you can
  rebel against structure and detach from the work."
- **Pisces** — Lived: disciplined through compassion and service, giving shape to what has none; fear:
  being swallowed by other people's needs; shadow: overwhelmed by duty, slipping into escape.
  Marie: "Your work is around faith and boundaries: giving form to the formless, though you can avoid the
  boundary and let responsibility dissolve."

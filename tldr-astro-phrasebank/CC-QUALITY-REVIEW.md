# CC quality review — do my transits/aspects match?

Honest verdict: **not yet.** My copy is accurate, on-voice, and seam-safe, but it reads about one notch more **clinical** than CC's, and it's missing CC's signature moves on the transit card. The gap is real but fixable — and it's mostly in the *rendering/assembly*, not the underlying content.

## The four concrete gaps

1. **No creative headline.** CC's card headline "evokes the felt experience or the advice, NOT the astrology" (their own rule): DEEP BREATH, ABUNDANCE ON TAP, DIRECT MESSAGE, FLY YOUR FREAK FLAG, HOT UNDER THE COLLAR. Mine is defaulting the headline to the aspect name — the exact thing CC avoids. **Every transit needs an authored, evocative CAPS headline.**
2. **Capitalization bug.** My rendered scene starts lowercase ("the urge to move…") because the slot text is lowercase and the template drops it at a sentence start. Simple render fix.
3. **The assembly frame is clunky.** Template 4A joins slots as "{habitual_response} may intensify {specific_cost}" → "Pushing recklessly or freezing in place may intensify wasted effort." CC would never write that. The *content* is fine; the *frame* stitching it is the weak link.
4. **Register: clinical vs. warm hook-first.** CC opens with a felt experience or a practical hook ("Breathwork is your friend right now"; "Hot under the collar, you come across as…") and reassures on hard aspects ("It's not at all a bad thing, it's just something to be aware of"). Mine opens with the diagnostic scene and stays tighter.

## What matches CC already (keep)

- **Moon phase / Moon sign / daily** are close in quality — short, warm, imperative. My Balsamic phase vs CC's are near-identical in register.
- The **footer-last** discipline, the **one-situation** rule, and the **constructive reframe on hard aspects** are all present in my content.
- The **content is accurate and specific** — the raw material is right; it's the packaging that trails.

## Target (my content, rewritten to CC quality)

**Mars square Saturn** — headline **SMALLER STEPS**
> Your drive keeps running into a wall right now: a limit, a delay, or a wave of self-doubt. It's frustrating, and pushing harder or freezing up will only wear you out. This is a moment for the long game. Break the goal into smaller, structured steps, and let the resistance show you exactly where you need to get stronger.
> _The astro: Mars squaring your Saturn_

Same facts, same reframe — but a felt-experience opener, a warm middle, smooth flow, and a real headline.

## Recommendation

The fix is a **transit revoice pass**, and it's the highest-value quality work left (bigger than synastry/composite for the reader). Three parts:

1. **Author an evocative CAPS headline for every aspect** (~470 aspect-pairs + angle/outer contacts). This alone closes the most visible gap.
2. **Fix rendering**: capitalize the sentence-start slot; drop the clunky "{habitual} may intensify {cost}" join in favor of a smoother connective; keep the footer format tighter ("Mars squaring your Saturn," not "Transiting Mars squares your natal Saturn. Orb: 1°.").
3. **A light register lift** toward the hook-first, warmer opening on the highest-traffic aspects.

The underlying slot content mostly survives — this is a packaging + headline pass, not a re-authoring from scratch. My honest estimate: it moves the transit surface from "good and accurate" to "reads like CC." Recommend doing this before finishing synastry/composite, since it also sets the quality bar those will be held to.

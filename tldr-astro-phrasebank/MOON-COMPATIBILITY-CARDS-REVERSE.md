same 66 cross-sign pairs told from the other seat (you = second sign), so the app has a complete, flowing
block for either reader direction and never has to split or re-conjugate a card. Same-sign pairs are
self-reverse and live only in the forward file. Directional: you = reader's Moon, {friend} = the other person.

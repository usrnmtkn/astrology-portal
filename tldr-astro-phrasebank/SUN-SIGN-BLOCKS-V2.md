# Sun sign blocks — v2 closed units (you-voice)

Per CARD-FORMAT-V2.md incl. refinements. Each block is a closed unit, reusable against any partner sign.
The them-voice version of each block will be SEPARATELY AUTHORED after these are approved — never
produced by pronoun replacement. Subject lock: identity, confidence,
visibility, leadership, recognition, what each person wants to be known for. Chain per block: sourced
trait → ordinary behavior → cost when rigid. Aries and Taurus are Marie's refined text (gold standard).

**Aries** — Your Sun is in Aries, so you tend to discover who you are by acting on what you want. You may volunteer before anyone else raises a hand, speak up when a group needs direction, or try something publicly without waiting to know whether you will succeed. Your willingness to be seen trying can make you courageous and easy to recognize. It can also lead you to take the lead before checking whether someone else wanted a say, then mistake another person's hesitation for a lack of conviction.

**Taurus** — Your Sun is in Taurus, so you tend to build confidence by knowing what matters to you and remaining consistent with it. You are less likely to change your opinion, your appearance, or the way you carry yourself simply because the people around you expect something different. Others may trust that you will be the same person in private that you are in public. The difficulty is that you can keep defending a choice after you know it is no longer working, simply because changing it would require you to admit that you have changed too.

**Gemini** — Your Sun is in Gemini, so you tend to show who you are through curiosity. You may be the one who brings a new idea to every conversation, gets the quiet table talking, and keeps things playful, and you take some pride in never being the boring one. Others often rely on you for exactly that. The difficulty is that wanting to know everything can scatter you across so many interests that people have trouble saying what you are actually about.

**Cancer** — Your Sun is in Cancer, so you tend to build identity through taking care of your people. You like others to know they can come to you at any time, and in most groups you quietly become the one who remembers, checks in, and gets the call when something goes wrong. You would often rather be essential than impressive. The difficulty is that being available to everyone can crowd out the rest of you, and you can end up known for what you do for people rather than for who you are.

**Leo** — Your Sun is in Leo, so you tend to know who you are by what you can give an audience. You may tell the story at dinner, take the visible role at work, and put real effort into being generous, because being respected matters more to you than being envied, and you would rather earn attention than demand it. Others often remember you as the brightest presence in the group. The difficulty is that you can begin measuring yourself by the reaction, and a flat response to something you are proud of can land as a judgment of who you are.

**Virgo** — Your Sun is in Virgo, so you tend to build identity through being useful and exact. You may point out the flaw nobody else caught, redo work that was already called finished, and volunteer for the unglamorous part because someone has to do it right. Others often trust your judgment precisely because you rarely oversell it. The difficulty is that your standards can turn inward, and no achievement quite counts once you have seen everything wrong with it.

**Libra** — Your Sun is in Libra, so you tend to find out who you are through other people. You may be the one who forms the group, makes the introductions, and keeps the peace, and you often work harder than anyone notices at being someone every kind of person can like. Others may describe you by your relationships: the partner, the best friend, the one who brought everybody together. The difficulty is that adjusting yourself to each audience can succeed so well that being liked starts to stand in for being known.

**Scorpio** — Your Sun is in Scorpio, so you tend to build identity through what you refuse to share casually. You may let most people see only the surface, commit yourself rarely and completely, and lose respect fast for anyone who treats other people's trust as entertainment. When something gets complicated for someone, you are often at your best, and people in real trouble tend to find you. The difficulty is that the privacy that protects you can read as suspicion, and people may give up on reaching you before you intended to let them in.

**Sagittarius** — Your Sun is in Sagittarius, so you tend to build identity through what you have seen, learned, and believe. You may be the one with the strong opinion and the story from somewhere else to back it up, and you share what you know because you believe it will help. Your optimism is usually one of the first things people mention about you. The difficulty is that you can overextend, promising yourself to more plans and people than anyone could honor, until commitments start to look like threats to your freedom instead of choices you made.

**Capricorn** — Your Sun is in Capricorn, so you tend to build identity through what you can point to and say you earned. You may turn down credit for anything you did not do yourself, trust years of work over talk, and accept responsibility so reliably that people stop asking whether you wanted it. Others often treat your presence as proof that things will get done. The difficulty is that the record can quietly replace the person, and rest can start to feel like losing your place.

**Aquarius** — Your Sun is in Aquarius, so you tend to build identity through standing slightly outside the group you care about. You may argue the position nobody else will, notice the person everyone overlooked, and think about what the whole group needs while others think about themselves. Being unlike the crowd is not a pose for you; it is often the most honest thing about you. The difficulty is that being the exception can harden into a rule, and agreeing with the majority, even when they are right, can feel like giving something up.

**Pisces** — Your Sun is in Pisces, so you tend to show who you are through what you imagine and how you care for the people around you. You may drift in the middle of a conversation into your own private world, following ideas that mean something only to you, and what you make or notice often carries something other people missed. Your compassion is usually the first thing others can name about you. The difficulty is that the ideas that mean something only to you can stay private for so long that people recognize your care and never learn the rest of who you are.

# Natal placement page contract (owner ruling, canonical)

**Status: owner ruling, 2026-08-17. Amended by the owner-directed mechanism-first rule (2026-08-13), the canonical natal-house bridge ruling (2026-08-20), and the human-pattern ruling (2026-08-21). Defines what a finished natal placement write-up contains, so the surface has a definition of done. Composes with `WRITING-STANDARD.md` (how the sentences work) and `TLDR-NATAL-PLACEMENT-DELINEATION-STANDARD-OWNER.md` (the delineation shape). This document governs the page; those govern the prose.**

## In scope for first launch

A finished planet-in-sign-in-house page has two reader sections: sign and house. The house section is assembled from an approved reusable bridge plus separately governed planet-in-house copy.

### 1. Sign section

The standing read. 120 to 180 words.

- Opens with the chart fact, per sentence mechanic 1 of the writing standard.
- The reader is the actor of the verbs.
- Full placement detail follows placement function, sign mechanism, lived evidence, consequence, complication, and mature expression. The passage becomes more behavioral after its opening, not more abstract.
- Ends on something that reframes rather than summarizes. A closing line that restates the paragraph is a failure; a closing line that changes how the reader understands what came before is the target.
- Soft certainty for interpretation: may, can, might, tends to.
- The difficulty is introduced as "The challenge", never an invented substitute.

### 2. House section

Where the placement plays out. 120 to 180 words.

- **The header names the house topics in full**, in the app's own voice: "in the 9th house of travel, education, publishing, religion, astrology and philosophy". The reader is told what the house is before being told what it does to them. This header is engine-owned and consistent per house; it is not part of the authored prose.
- The first prose unit is the exact approved `fallback-hook/house-meaning/{house}` bridge and opens `It's in your [Nth] house, meaning...`.
- A separately governed exact-key planet-in-house passage follows in direct reader voice. It starts with the reader doing, noticing, choosing, encountering, or experiencing something in that arena. New candidates do not reopen with `Planet in the Nth house...`.
- The planet-in-house passage is authored from the approved AstrologySupport mechanism and source constraints. Existing serving prose is not used as a draft or paraphrase source.
- **It carries both the meaning and the outcome** (owner ruling, 2026-08-17). The house topics as the reader experiences them stay: "Big questions pull at you: what life means, what is true, and what is worth believing." Occupations and life results are then named on top of that: teaching, mentoring, publishing, going back for a further qualification. Neither substitutes for the other. Dropping the meaning to list professions is as much a failure as stopping at the topics.
- Same sentence rules as the sign section.

### House-section serving gate

- The bridge ruling approves the composition contract, not new planet-specific wording.
- Existing serving copy stays unchanged until the owner approves the fully rendered bridge-plus-candidate passage.
- Friend copy remains separately authored. It is never derived through pronoun replacement or inflection of the You passage.

## Resolved

- **Do house sections name occupations and life outcomes?** Yes, and they keep the meaning as well. Ruled 2026-08-17; recorded in the house section above. Occupations are added to the topical read, never in place of it.

## What this contract does not include

No epithet, no summary block, no at-your-best / if-you-are-struggling / taken-to-the-extreme structure. Those appear in reference material but are not adopted.

## Coverage arithmetic

The placement authoring workload remains 360 exact rows: 192 planet-in-sign rows and 168 planet-in-house rows. The house renderer also reuses 12 already-approved `fallback-hook/house-meaning/{house}` bridges, one per house. Those bridges are shared composition units rather than another planet-by-house authoring matrix, so this still does not become 1,440 combinations.

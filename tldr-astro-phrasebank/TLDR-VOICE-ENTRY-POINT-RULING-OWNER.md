# Entry point by person contract: self speaks to the reader, friend speaks from the room (owner ruling, canonical)

**Status: owner ruling, 2026-08-13. Generation and judge rule, not reader-facing copy. Applies to every surface that renders in both self and friend voice: natal placements, natal aspects, empty houses, house cusps, angles, patterns. Companion to `TLDR-AUTHOR-FROM-MECHANISM-RULING-OWNER.md` and `TLDR-FRIEND-NATAL-VOICE-RULING-OWNER.md`.**

## The ruling

Self voice and friend voice are not the same passage with different pronouns. They enter the scene from different positions.

**Self (natal, "You"):** the writing speaks to the reader. The reader is the person being addressed, and the passage enters through their own experience.

**Friend ("Name"):** the writing speaks about someone the reader knows. It enters through what other people in the room observe.

Owner comparison, 2026-08-13, on the same mechanism (Jupiter conjunct Pluto):

| Voice | Entry | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self | reader's own experience | "You can step into a complicated situation and see the larger move quickly." |
| Friend | observer's position | "People start looking at Name when the meeting reaches the decision nobody wants to make." |

## Why this matters

Friend copy produced by pronoun-swapping self copy fails for a structural reason, not a wording reason: a passage written to be read by its own subject, then handed to a third party, has no vantage point. It either coaches an absent person or narrates their interior as if the reader could see it. The owner rejected 41 of 43 friend candidates in the round-1 review for exactly these symptoms. Re-entering from the observer's position removes the cause rather than repairing the output.

## Generation contract

1. Author the self passage from the mechanism, entering through the reader's experience.
2. Author the friend passage from the SAME mechanism, entering through what people around the person notice: what they get handed, asked, told, forgiven, relied on for, or braced for. The friend passage is authored, not derived.
3. Interior states appear in friend voice only where an observer could reasonably infer them from behavior. What the person feels privately, unobserved, belongs to self voice.
4. Neither voice instructs the reader about how to handle the other person. Friend copy describes; it does not coach.

## Judge additions (blocking)

- `friend-entry-position`: a friend passage whose opening could be converted to self voice by pronoun substitution alone fails. The opening must locate the reader as an observer.
- `pronoun-swap-derivation`: a friend passage whose sentence structure matches the corresponding self passage fails as a derivation rather than an authoring.
- `friend-interior-access`: a friend passage asserting an unobservable interior state fails.
- `friend-coaching` (existing, retained): advice directed at managing the other person fails.

## Effect on pass 2

Pass 2's authored-friend approach stands, with the derivation method replaced: friend rows are authored from the mechanism at the observer's entry point, not derived from approved self rows. The V13-derived plan for `placement-sentence/*` and `placement-house-sentence/*` uses the approved self row as the mechanism source, never as the sentence structure.

# Jupiter signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (Parker DK + Lofthus). **Marie** = your phrasebank
wording (compat gift/shadow); banned words already swapped. Jupiter = how a person grows, hopes, believes,
and finds meaning, and where they overdo it. Write final copy in your voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md and
lint every line.

- **Aries** — Lived: grows through bold action and going first, optimistic and enthusiastic; shadow:
  reckless risks, leaps before looking.
  Marie: "You grow by going first and betting on yourself; boldness tends to pay off for you, though you
  can leap without looking and burn out fast."
- **Taurus** — Lived: grows through the good life, pleasure, and making money, generous and easygoing;
  shadow: overindulges, stubborn in their beliefs.
  Marie: "You grow through patience and building; abundance comes to you slowly and stays, though you can
  grow so slowly you slide into complacency."
- **Gemini** — Lived: grows through learning, ideas, and variety, curious and quick; shadow: spreads thin,
  a bit of everything and none of it deeply.
  Marie: "You grow through learning and connecting; your luck runs through ideas and people, though you can
  chase every idea and go a mile wide, an inch deep."
- **Cancer** — Lived: grows through home, family, and caring, intuitive with a good instinct for money;
  shadow: over-attached, excess around belonging.
  Marie: "You grow through care and roots; your good fortune lives in home and belonging, though you can
  cling to the familiar and mistake staying put for growth."
- **Leo** — Lived: grows through self-expression, confidence, and being seen, generous; shadow: arrogance,
  showing off, too much of everything.
  Marie: "You grow by expressing yourself fully; confidence and creativity open your doors, though you can
  let confidence tip into arrogance and excess."
- **Virgo** — Lived: grows through useful work, service, and getting the details right, conscientious;
  shadow: worries the big picture into small pieces.
  Marie: "You grow through craft and usefulness; small, careful improvements compound for you, though you
  can shrink your vision with over-analysis and doubt."
- **Libra** — Lived: grows through relationships, fairness, and beauty, friendly and diplomatic; shadow:
  over-accommodating, needs to be liked.
  Marie: "You grow through relationship and fairness; partnership expands your world, though you can
  over-accommodate and lose your own direction."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: grows through depth, intensity, and getting to the bottom of things, resourceful;
  shadow: obsession, extremes, all or nothing.
  Marie: "You grow through depth and transformation; you gain by letting the shallow things go, though you
  can fixate and refuse to let the shallow things go."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: (Jupiter's own sign) grows through travel, philosophy, and freedom, always
  after the meaning; shadow: overpromises, restless, too much.
  Marie: "You grow by reaching for meaning and horizon; faith and adventure are your fuel, though you can
  over-promise, overreach, and dodge the follow-through."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: grows through discipline and achievement, ambitious and cautious with their
  optimism; shadow: success that brings little joy.
  Marie: "You grow through discipline and structure; you earn your expansion the hard way, though you can
  gatekeep growth behind rules and grind."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: grows through ideas, causes, and the future, inventive and forward-looking; shadow:
  chases novelty, detaches, eccentric.
  Marie: "You grow through vision and community; your luck runs through the unconventional, though you can
  chase novelty and detach from what's practical."
- **Pisces** — Lived: (Jupiter's traditional sign) grows through faith, art, and compassion, visionary and
  giving; shadow: escapism, gives past their own limits.
  Marie: "You grow through faith, art, and compassion; you gain by trusting the current, though you can
  drift on faith without grounding it in anything solid."

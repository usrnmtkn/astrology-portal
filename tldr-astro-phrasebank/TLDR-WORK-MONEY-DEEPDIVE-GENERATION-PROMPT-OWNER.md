# Work & Money deep-dive generation prompt (owner ruling, canonical, verbatim)

**Status: owner ruling, 2026-08-09. Defines the WORK & MONEY deep-dive as a separate paid product alongside the General report: NOT an excerpt or filtered General report; factor selection re-runs against the complete calculated bundle, prioritizing work/money/business domains, with other domains included only when they materially change the Work & Money story. New rules relative to the general standard: natural-paragraph formatting (no isolated rhetorical one-liners), visible astrological reasoning (factors modify one another), money logic in real financial consequences (rates, hours, scope creep, client concentration, payment timing), business/entrepreneurship logic (growth is not assumed desirable), three-status job manifestation guidance without mechanical branching, key-date format contract. Reference implementation: `artifacts/marie-satori-work-money-2026-owner-v1.md` (owner-authored). Owner text verbatim below.**

PROSE AND FORMATTING
==================================================

Write in natural paragraphs.

Do not isolate every rhetorical question or short sentence on its own line.

Related questions belong inside one paragraph.

Preferred:

sustain, and which compromise solved something instead of only delaying
the problem."

Short declarative sentences can be used when they genuinely land:

"A career can change before the résumé does."

"A plan that only works under perfect conditions needs another revision."

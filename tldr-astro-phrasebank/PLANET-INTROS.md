# Planet intro descriptions (compatibility)

One fixed line per planet, prepended above the pair-specific compatibility card (Co-Star style, Marie's
voice, framed for two people). Not directional, so it's the same for either reader. Linted.

- **Sun** — The Sun is your core identity, the part you lead and shine with. It shows how each of you takes up space and wants to be seen.
- **Moon** — The Moon rules your feelings, moods, and what you need to feel safe. It shows how each of you handles emotion and what you reach for when you're hurt.
- **Mercury** — Mercury rules how you think, talk, and take things in. It shows how each of you reasons, communicates, and needs to be understood.
- **Venus** — Venus rules love, affection, and what you find beautiful. It shows how each of you gives love and what you want from closeness.
- **Mars** — Mars rules drive, desire, and anger. It shows how each of you goes after what you want and how you fight.
- **Jupiter** — Jupiter rules growth, belief, and where you reach for more. It shows how each of you expands and takes chances.
- **Saturn** — Saturn rules responsibility, limits, and fear. It shows how each of you carries weight and where you feel not-enough.

3. Evidence filter: retain only manifestations supported by the supplied house, aspect, approved source text, and actual context. A sign is not a house; a domain is not a life event. Preserve the supplied ranking and timing.
4. Draft: choose a small number of distinct, recognizable, source-supported examples to make the mechanism understandable. When only the category is supported, write at the category level. Do not add props, biography, or personal circumstances for color.
5. Outcome check: remove or broaden any unsupported implication of divorce, breakup, quitting, illness, firing, moving, or major financial loss. Adding “may,” “could,” or advice to do it does not make an unsupported consequential event admissible. Additional chart symbolism alone does not establish that an event will happen.

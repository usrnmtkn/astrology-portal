# Sun signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (Parker DK, cross-checked with standard sources).
**Marie** = your phrasebank wording (compat gift/shadow) to draw voice from; banned words already swapped.
The Sun = core identity, vitality, how a person leads and takes up space — not how they think (Mercury)
or feel (Moon). Write final copy in your voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md and lint every line.

- **Aries** — Lived: leads by going first; direct, brave, competitive; shadow: rash, self-centered, runs
  people over.
  Marie: "Your identity comes through bold and direct: you'd rather start something than wait to be
  asked, though you can act before you think and charge in on impulse."
- **Taurus** — Lived: reliable and unhurried, values loyalty and the good things, hard to move once set;
  shadow: stubborn, possessive, digs in against change.
  Marie: "You know who you are slowly and solidly, and you don't move until it feels worth it, though you
  can dig in and resist change until it costs you progress."
- **Gemini** — Lived: curious, quick, sociable, needs variety and to be in the conversation; shadow:
  restless, spread thin.
  Marie: "Your sense of self runs on curiosity and talk; you try on ideas the way others try on clothes,
  though you can spread yourself thin and skim the surface."
- **Cancer** — Lived: protective and caring, tied to home and their people; leads by looking after others;
  shadow: moody, defensive, retreats into their shell.
  Marie: "You're most yourself when you're caring for something, and you lead with feeling, though you can
  lose yourself in caretaking and forget your own needs."
- **Leo** — Lived: confident and generous, shines when seen, wants to be at the center; shadow: proud,
  needs the spotlight, domineering.
  Marie: "You come alive when you're seen; your identity wants to be admired, to play, and to make
  something, though you can let pride tip into arrogance and need too much of the spotlight."
- **Virgo** — Lived: modest, useful, precise, at their best being genuinely helpful; shadow: critical,
  anxious, never satisfied.
  Marie: "You know yourself through what you refine and improve; being useful is how you shine, though you
  can chase perfection and be hard on yourself."
- **Libra** — Lived: fair, charming, drawn to partnership and keeping the peace; shadow: indecisive,
  people-pleasing, avoids the hard call.
  Marie: "Your identity forms in relationship; you find yourself in the space between you and another,
  though you can lose yourself in others and struggle to decide."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: intense, private, all-in, a will that doesn't quit; shadow: controlling, secretive,
  holds a grudge.
  Marie: "You're most yourself in the deep end, where things get honest, private, and intense, though you
  can get guarded, controlling, and slow to trust."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: adventurous, honest, freedom-loving, after a bigger horizon; shadow: blunt,
  restless, promises more than they deliver.
  Marie: "Your sense of self needs room to roam: a bigger view, something to chase, a horizon, though you
  can get careless and dodge responsibility."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: ambitious, disciplined, responsible, playing the long game; shadow: cold,
  joyless, all work.
  Marie: "You know who you are by what you build and take responsibility for, though you can go rigid and
  tie your worth to what you achieve."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: independent and original, more interested in the idea than the approval; shadow:
  detached, aloof, contrary.
  Marie: "Your identity is a little outside the norm; you want to build things that shift the status quo,
  though you can detach, rebel for its own sake, and dig in on being right."
- **Pisces** — Lived: compassionate, imaginative, adaptable, feels their way through life; shadow: escapist,
  boundary-less, slips into self-pity.
  Marie: "You're most yourself at the edges, where feeling, imagination, and compassion blur the lines,
  though you can drift, avoid, and let your boundaries blur."

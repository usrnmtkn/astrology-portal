## 2. The consolidated do-not-use list

Most rejections trace to this list. It merges `WRITING-STANDARD.md` with every later ruling.

**Never:**

- Em dashes. One-sentence paragraphs.
- `real` or `true` as intensifiers. The fix is naming what the thing actually becomes, never a synonym swap.
- `settle`, `settles`, `steady`, `steadiness`, `comfort`, `warmth` as standalone abstractions. Say the concrete thing: food on the table, a routine that holds, someone checking in.
- The `room` metaphor: in the room, a room's mood, the smartest in the room, read the room. Say who is actually there. (Room to breathe or room to roam is fine; that is space, not a setting.)
- `the catch`. **The correct wording is `the challenge`.** Do not invent a substitute. "The harder side" was invented for this purpose and shipped in 35 passages while the documented replacement already existed.
- `the danger is`, `the upside?`, `on the one hand / on the other hand`.
- `this is about`, `this placement becomes`, `this part of the chart`, `the connection asks you to`.
- `alignment`, `activation`, `holding space`, `navigating`, `leaning into`, `your journey`, `a version of yourself`, `the thread`, `moves through your topics`.
- `the native`, `house themes`, `this placement indicates`, `directs this planetary energy`, `activates this area of life`.
- Keyword lists dressed as prose. Abstract endings. Clever closing lines that need decoding. A last sentence that drifts off the point.
- Clinical register: nervous system, emotional deprivation, inner support, regulate, emotional maturity, healing journey.
- Invented childhood, parents, trauma, or attachment history.
- Metaphor the reader must translate back into behaviour. A comparison is allowed only when it makes the meaning more obvious.
- Permission endings and generic coaching conclusions.

**Always:**

- The reader is the actor of the verbs. "You proofread the application, pack the charger." Never "the application gets proofread."
- Soft certainty for interpretation: may, can, might, tends to.
- Name the planet's real domain. The Moon is feelings, Venus love, Mars drive, Mercury the mind, Sun identity, Jupiter belief and growth, Saturn responsibility.

**Friends and relationship copy**

- Relationship prose stays directional: one person acts, the other responds, and a pattern develops. Astrology may explain why the pattern is easy to enter but may never excuse the observable behavior.

- Do/Don't columns remain semantically separate and grammatically parallel; a transitive instruction names its object. See `TLDR-HUMAN-PATTERN-AND-RELATIONSHIP-COPY-RULING-OWNER.md` (2026-08-21).

# Mercury signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (Parker DK + Lofthus). **Marie** = your phrasebank
wording (compat gift/shadow); banned words already swapped. Mercury = how a person thinks, talks, and
needs to be understood. Write final copy in your voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md and lint every line.

- **Aries** — Lived: mind works fast, sharp and decisive, says it straight; shadow: hasty, blunt, argues,
  jumps to conclusions.
  Marie: "You think fast and say it faster; your mind moves in quick, direct bursts, though you can speak
  before you think and cut people off."
- **Taurus** — Lived: mind works slowly and carefully, practical, good memory, won't be rushed; shadow:
  fixed opinions, slow to change their mind.
  Marie: "You think slowly and thoroughly, and once you've decided you don't budge easily, though you can
  dig into an opinion and refuse to move off it."
- **Gemini** — Lived: quick, curious, versatile, a natural talker; shadow: scattered, skims the surface.
  Marie: "Your mind is quick, curious, and everywhere at once; you learn by talking and linking ideas,
  though you can scatter across topics and skim the surface."
- **Cancer** — Lived: thinks with feeling and intuition, remembers everything, reads the mood; shadow:
  takes things personally, dwells on the past, worries.
  Marie: "You think with your feelings, and memory and mood color how you take things in, though you can
  let your mood color the facts."
- **Leo** — Lived: thinks big and speaks with confidence, organized and persuasive; shadow: fixed on
  being right, proud of their opinions.
  Marie: "You think in big, confident strokes, and you speak to be heard and to inspire, though you can
  talk over others and need to be the one who's right."
- **Virgo** — Lived: precise and analytical, quick to spot the flaw, practical; shadow: nit-picks,
  over-analyzes, worries, nags.
  Marie: "Your mind is precise and analytical; you notice the error everyone else missed, though you can
  over-analyze and nitpick."
- **Libra** — Lived: weighs both sides, diplomatic and fair-minded; shadow: can't decide, sits on the
  fence.
  Marie: "You think in comparisons and both-sides, weighing every option before you commit, though you can
  see every side and never land on a decision."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: probing and penetrating, gets to the bottom of things, strong focus; shadow:
  suspicious, secretive, broods.
  Marie: "Your mind digs; you're not satisfied until you've found what's going on underneath, though you
  can get suspicious and read hidden motives everywhere."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: big-picture and optimistic, thinks in ideas and meaning; shadow: skips the
  detail, blunt, overpromises.
  Marie: "You think in big pictures and possibilities, sometimes past the inconvenient details, though you
  can talk in broad strokes and skip the details."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: methodical and serious, thinks a thing through before speaking; shadow: cautious
  to a fault, pessimistic, slow to shift a view.
  Marie: "Your thinking is structured and practical; you want the plan, not the brainstorm, though you can
  go rigid and dismiss ideas that aren't practical."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: original and independent, sees the angle no one else does, questions the obvious;
  shadow: stubborn, contrary, detached from how people feel.
  Marie: "You think in systems and original angles, wired to question the obvious, though you can argue
  just to be contrary and detach from how people feel."
- **Pisces** — Lived: intuitive and imaginative, thinks in images, picks up what isn't said; shadow:
  vague, forgetful, skips the detail, easily swayed.
  Marie: "You think in images and intuition, following what feels right more than what's on paper, though
  you can drift, lose your place, and mistake a hope for a fact."

# Report-level redundancy pass v1

**Status:** owner ruling, active when the assembled report is structurally clean.
**Version:** `report-redundancy-pass-v1`
**Owner ruling:** 2026-08-11
**Surface:** Report only.
**Governance:** Findings-only editorial pass. It never writes reader copy, changes astrology, changes the unit judge, or authorizes publication.

Review the complete assembled report after all drafted units have independently passed their unit validators, fact-lock, and judge.

Identify each major thesis and manifestation. Allow one primary treatment plus brief callbacks. Compare Overview, What-the-Year-Is-About, main narrative, seasonal intros, Key Dates, and Year-in-Review for semantic duplication. Adjacent sections answering the same question require one bounded finding that instructs the writer to merge the necessary meaning or delete the redundant treatment.

Enforce these report-level rules:

1. A major thesis receives one primary treatment and only brief callbacks.
2. A manifestation menu appears once at its strongest interpretive anchor. Later units refer back without re-listing.
3. Key Dates compress chronology. They never repeat seasonal prose.
4. Year-in-Review synthesizes what changed. It never restates the report section by section, and it may be omitted when it adds nothing.
5. Mechanism statements are confident. May, can, could, and might are reserved for event manifestations.
6. A possibility space is named once. Later units say the work, the decision, the opportunity, or another accurate carry-forward term rather than turning one example into an established storyline.
7. A lived sentence that reveals the astrology is not followed by an interpretive paraphrase of itself.
8. When eclipses form one sequence, the later eclipse resolves the earlier development explicitly. Do not treat connected eclipses as unrelated events.
9. Preserve the distinction between a reader who no longer wants an arrangement and a reader who can no longer maintain it.
10. Apply the no-cleverness-tax ruling before returning no findings.

Use only the supplied frozen facts, governed manifestation records, owner rulings, and complete assembled report. Do not invent astrology or reader circumstances.

Return findings only. Each finding must name the exact unit, exact location token, zero-based sentence scope, verbatim quote, evidence from another unit or owner ruling, and a bounded correction instruction. Never return replacement prose. Never return a preference for stylistic variety by itself.

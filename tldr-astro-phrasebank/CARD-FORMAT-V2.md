# Compatibility card format v2 (approved sample: Mercury Pisces + Sagittarius)

Supersedes the 3-sentence format for all planet compatibility cards. Approved by Marie 2026-07-17.

## Structure (4 paragraphs)

**P1 — Planet line.** One sentence, plural, subject declaration: "Mercury shows how each of you thinks,
communicates, learns, and makes sense of the world around you." (May render from PLANET-INTROS.md.)

**P2 — Your snippet.** "Your [planet] is in [sign], so…" Closed unit, reusable against any partner sign.
3-5 sentences. Each trait from the reference is translated into an ordinary lived scene in the planet's
own subject ("When someone asks what you want, you may begin with how the situation feels"). Ends with
the flaw stated plainly, softener on the harshest word only.

**P3 — Their snippet.** Same structure, third person, "{friend}'s [planet] is in [sign], so they…"
Includes at least one scene the reader will recognize ("If you tell them about a problem, they may
immediately give you their opinion, even when you only wanted them to listen"). Privileged-knowledge
register allowed ("they can also…", "keep talking past the moment when they should have stopped").

**P4 — Interaction.** Pair-specific. In order: (a) the collision as a concrete scene, quoted dialogue
allowed ("But what do you actually want?"); (b) both misreads, yours and theirs; (c) the mutual gift,
one clause each; (d) direct advice to the reader, imperative mood, 1-2 sentences ("Tell them when you
want an opinion and when you only want them to listen").

## Refinements (Marie, locked)

- Replace absolutes ("depends on", "always") with conditionals: may, can, often, tend to. Use "so" only
  for real cause-and-effect; do not swap "meaning" → "so" mechanically.
- No mind-reading ("bothers them more than they will admit"). Claims stay at what another person could
  observe or reasonably infer.
- No therapy-coded abstractions ("a version of yourself").
- Sign-block chain, traceable: sourced trait → ordinary behavior that demonstrates it → cost when the
  trait becomes rigid or excessive. The lived example translates the trait; it never introduces a new
  habit, motive, or flaw the astrology does not support.
- The interaction must be a recognizable situation inside the planet's domain (Sun: visibility,
  leadership, credit, representation) and show, in order: (1) the situation, (2) what the reader does,
  (3) what {friend} does, (4) how each may misread the other, (5) what each contributes, (6) one
  specific direction for the reader, (7) one specific request the reader can make of {friend}. Advice
  must answer something that happened in the scene.
- Element/modality are supporting logic in the foundation block, not the lived conclusion.
- Counting: directional cards = 144 routes per planet (12 same-sign + 66 cross forward + 66 reverse).
  The forward file (78) plus reverse file (66) is the complete set.
- Gold-standard card: the refined Taurus + Aries Sun card (2026-07-17). The standard: no instruction in
  the advice could be moved unchanged into another planet's card.

## Rules

- Connective is "so", not "meaning". Hedges allowed and encouraged: may, tend to, can, often.
- Lived examples are translations of sourced traits into ordinary situations — never additional
  astrological claims, never behaviors absent from the references.
- Planet-swap test still applies to every sentence.
- Sign facts allowed: element, modality, axis, rulership. State them in the foundation block; in prose
  only when they earn their place.
- No em dashes. Both linters must pass.

## The method (Marie, 2026-07-17) — follow for every planet and sign combination

1. **Start with the planet.** It stays the subject of every sentence. List its domain first (e.g.
   Mercury: thinking, speaking/listening, asking/answering, learning/explaining, deciding, handling
   misunderstandings). Affection = Venus, hurt = Moon, anger = Mars. Planet-swap test on every line.
2. **Sign = how the planet operates**, never a full personality. The sign supplies vocabulary and
   tendencies; the planet determines where they appear.
3. **Cross-check traits across sources.** Build only from tendencies that repeat across the supplied
   references. Establish the shared foundation before writing any example.
4. **Translate each trait into observable behavior.** Every example answers: what would another person
   actually see or hear? Examples are translations of established traits, not new claims.
5. **No unsupported leaps.** The chain is: source meaning → tendency → ordinary example directly
   demonstrating that tendency. Every step follows from the one before.
6. **Sign blocks stand alone.** Your-block never mentions their sign and vice versa, so each block is
   reusable as planet × sign × person.
7. **Interaction = two behaviors meeting.** No verdict-first. What you do, how {friend} responds, what
   you each think the other's response means. Neither person is the problem.
8. **Advice answers the actual problem** — never "communicate clearly" or "meet in the middle." Tell
   each person what to do differently, tied to the specific collision.
9. **Natural-language standard.** No technical astrology in reader copy, no trait lists without
   examples, no therapy language, no decorative metaphors, no motive claims we cannot know. Longer
   paragraphs so the reader sees the logic: trait → behavior → consequence.

Pipeline: planet domain → sourced sign tendency → observable behavior → felt consequence → two
behaviors meeting → each person's reading of the moment → specific direction.

Provenance labels: traits SOURCE-SUPPORTED; compatibility reasoning DERIVED SYNTHESIS; lived examples
SA_STYLE_ORIGINAL; source trail informed_by.

## Planet domains — scene menus (Marie, locked)

Every card's scenes come from its planet's menu. This defines both the subject and the situations
allowed on the card.

**Sun:** Your Sun shows how you take up space, express who you are, lead, seek recognition, and react
when you feel overlooked. Their Sun shows the same for them. Write this through scenes where one of you
wants credit, takes the lead, sets the tone, feels unseen, or expects the relationship to move in the
direction of their identity.

**Moon:** Your Moon shows how you feel, seek comfort, give care, recover after hurt, and settle after
stress. Their Moon shows what makes them feel safe or exposed. Write this through scenes involving
mood, home, food, rest, reassurance, withdrawal, body signals, or the quiet question of whether someone
knows how to care for the other person.

**Mercury:** Your Mercury shows how you think, speak, ask questions, listen, explain yourself, make
decisions, and handle misunderstanding. Their Mercury shows how their mind moves and how they expect
conversation to work. Write this through scenes involving texts, tone, interruptions, overexplaining,
missing details, direct questions, unclear answers, or the moment a small misunderstanding becomes a
bigger issue.

**Venus:** Your Venus shows how you give affection, receive love, enjoy pleasure, and recognize proof
that someone cares. Their Venus shows what makes them feel wanted, chosen, appreciated, or close. Write
this through scenes involving time together, touch, gifts, invitations, romance, compliments,
attention, beauty, pleasure, or the moment affection is offered but not received in the way it was
intended.

**Mars:** Your Mars shows how you pursue what you want, initiate, protect yourself, compete, express
anger, and move through conflict or desire. Their Mars shows how they act when the stakes rise. Write
this through scenes involving who makes the first move, who pushes, who waits, who gets heated, who
wants action, who needs space, or how attraction and conflict can live close together.

**Jupiter:** Your Jupiter shows how you take risks, follow belief, encourage possibility, trust the
future, and sometimes go too far. Their Jupiter shows what they believe is worth trying, promising,
learning, or expanding. Write this through scenes involving opportunities, travel, school, faith,
confidence, spending, big plans, or the moment something exciting becomes larger than either of you can
realistically carry.

**Saturn:** Your Saturn shows how you handle responsibility, commitment, fear, limits, labor, and
consequences. Their Saturn shows what they take seriously and where they may feel pressure, duty, or
caution. Write this through scenes involving deadlines, promises, money owed, repeated
responsibilities, saying no, asking for help, showing up consistently, or staying when the relationship
requires real work.

Note: this menu is internal guidance. Reader-facing copy still passes the linters (e.g. the menu says
"reassurance"/"settle" to name the territory; the cards themselves never use those words).

## Addressing rule

Write to the reader directly, always. Use "Your Sun…", "Their Sun…" ("{friend}'s Sun…" in templated
copy), "Between you…". Never "Person A's Sun…", "Person B's Moon…", or "Each person…". The card speaks
to the reader; it does not analyze two chart objects from a distance.

## The planet translation layer (Marie, 2026-07-17)

Raw sign-bank lines describe the sign generally and often belong to another planet. Before any line
enters a planet's bank it must pass a translation layer:

```
raw sign behavior
→ planet translation (for the Sun: what this reveals about identity and what the person wants to be known for)
→ planet lived example
→ planet cost
```

Worked example (Aries, Sun): raw "You make the first move before everyone agrees on the plan" →
translation "You want to be known as someone who is willing to go first" → lived "When a group needs
someone to take the lead, you may volunteer before you know whether everyone agrees with your
direction" → cost "You can become so identified with being the brave one that another person's caution
feels like a challenge to your confidence."

Sorting guide for raw lines: answers/messages/questions/decisions = Mercury; feelings/moods/withdrawal
= Moon; affection/love/closeness/compromise = Venus; acting immediately/frustration/conflict = Mars;
promises/responsibility/what keeps working = Saturn. The same behavior can serve the Sun only when the
sentence gives it an identity reason (who the person is, the role they occupy, what they want
recognized).

## Production structure (source of truth)

Do not store repeated pairing files as the source of truth. Store per planet:

```json
{
  "sun_sign_blocks": {
    "aries": {
      "you": "You tend to...",
      "them": "{friend} tends to...",
      "source_traits": [], "lived_evidence": [], "shadow": "...",
      "provenance": "SA_STYLE_ORIGINAL", "informed_by": []
    }
  },
  "sun_interactions": {
    "aries__taurus": { "reader_sign": "aries", "friend_sign": "taurus",
      "scene": "...", "reader_misread": "...", "friend_misread": "...",
      "mutual_contribution": "...", "reader_direction": "...", "friend_request": "..." },
    "taurus__aries": { "...": "the reverse route is its own authored record" }
  }
}
```

Sign blocks are stored once (you-form and them-form **separately authored** — never produced by global
pronoun replacement). Only the interaction gets a directional route: 144 routes per planet, each with
its own scene, misreads, contributions, reader direction, and friend request.

Corrections locked at source (do not regress): "closes a cabinet" → "closes a door"; "open three more
conversations instead of finishing the difficult one" → "keep changing the subject instead of saying
the thing that makes you uncomfortable."

## Foundation block (not rendered; kept under each card in source files)

- [Sign] [planet]: the source traits used, comma list.
- The interaction: shared/differing element-modality note in one line.
- Provenance line: SA_STYLE_ORIGINAL, informed by Co-Star + named references.

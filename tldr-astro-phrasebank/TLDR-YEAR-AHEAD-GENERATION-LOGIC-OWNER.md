# Year Ahead report generation standard (owner ruling, canonical, verbatim)

**Status: owner ruling, 2026-08-09, GENERATION STANDARD (27-point version with CORE RULE coverage gate). Supersedes the same-day 26-point and 20-point versions. `TLDR-YEAR-AHEAD-MANIFESTATION-SETS-OWNER.md` remains valid for its record format and worked examples. Generation rule, not reader-facing copy. Owner text verbatim below. Reference implementation: `artifacts/marie-satori-year-ahead-2026-FINAL.md` (owner-authored final, 2026-08-09; eligible as owner_authored_final voice evidence for the Year Ahead surface). Owner-approved signature refrain: "One easy yes can take/become three hours you did not have."**

**Headline change from prior versions: the COVERAGE GATE. Do not add a life domain because a report feels incomplete without it; do not leave genuine chart material behind. The chart decides the coverage. New domain rules: HOME/MOVING/FAMILY (8), LOVE/RELATIONSHIPS (9), SPIRITUALITY/MEANING (10), DETACHMENT/WITHDRAWAL (11). VOICE now formally bans "whether" and em dashes in generated copy.**

```text
YEAR AHEAD REPORT GENERATION STANDARD
Owner ruling, 2026-08-09

CORE RULE

Do not add a life domain because a Year Ahead report feels incomplete without it.

If the chart has meaningful information to add, add it fully.
If the chart does not support meaningful information, leave the topic out.

The chart decides the coverage.

1. CALCULATION FIRST

Lock:
- natal chart
- Solar Return chart
- annual profection + Lord of the Year
- SR-to-natal overlays
- major returns
- eclipses
- major transits
- dates
- pass counts
- houses
- calendar-year boundaries

The writer cannot change calculated facts.

2. REPO DOCTRINE FIRST

If an aspect already has governed interpretation in the repo,
retrieve that meaning before writing.

Year Ahead may:
- apply house/domain context
- place the aspect inside the annual chronology
- add chart-earned manifestations
- connect repeated annual factors

Year Ahead may not silently redefine the aspect.

3. COVERAGE GATE

Before writing a domain, ask:

DOES THE CHART HAVE MEANINGFUL INFORMATION HERE?

Possible domains:
career/work
money
health/capacity
home
moving/property
family
caregiving
love/dating
sex
relationships
friendships
creativity
children when earned
travel
education
communication
spirituality/faith
privacy/withdrawal
grief/closure
public recognition

If no:
omit.

If yes:
build a manifestation set.

Do not manufacture breadth.

4. REQUIRED PATH

astrology
-> governed meaning
-> activated domain
-> possible manifestations
-> DO NOT ASSUME
-> narrative role
-> prose

5. SPECIFICITY CEILING

Specific about event type.
Less certain about exact event.

Allowed:
"A move may become necessary or desirable."
"An application may turn into an offer or rejection."

Not allowed:
"You are moving."
"You will get the job."

6. CHANGE IS NOT ALWAYS VOLUNTARY

Do not interpret Uranus automatically as rebellion or chosen reinvention.

A role can stop fitting because:
- the person chooses differently
- health changes
- physical capacity changes
- caregiving changes
- location changes
- access changes
- finances change
- available time changes
- priorities change
- the arrangement becomes unsustainable

Leave room for:
"I chose to change this."
and:
"I could no longer keep doing this the old way."

7. HEALTH / CAPACITY

Include only when earned.

Use:
sleep
meals
appointments
movement
medication/routine when supported
workload
recovery
caregiving
physical capacity
daily schedule

Do not invent:
symptoms
diagnoses
medical crises
psychological causes for symptoms

8. HOME / MOVING / FAMILY

When earned, possible manifestations include:
move
lease change
combining households
separating households
repair
property paperwork
storage
maintenance
caregiving
parent/family responsibility
household labor
accessibility changes at home

Use possibility language.

9. LOVE / RELATIONSHIPS

Include only when earned.

Possible domains:
dating
attraction
sex
pleasure
time together
availability
money
travel
caregiving
changed health/capacity
different expectations
who adjusts
who organizes
who gets priority

Do not assume partnership, breakup, commitment, or marriage.

10. SPIRITUALITY / MEANING

Include when supported by:
12th house
9th house
Neptune
Pisces
Jupiter
their rulers
repeated annual factors

Possible lived manifestations:
prayer
meditation
astrology
ritual
spiritual study
private reading
retreat
dreams becoming more noticeable
questioning an old belief
finding a new philosophy
wanting more time alone

Do not automatically predict:
spiritual awakening
psychic ability
addiction
confusion
depression

11. DETACHMENT / WITHDRAWAL

Detachment is not automatically pathology.

When earned, it can look like:
answering messages more slowly
reduced interest in visibility
pulling back from a group
losing interest in an old routine
feeling less attached to a title
still being capable of a role but feeling finished with being known for it

Always specify what the person may feel detached from.

12. PLANETS KEEP THEIR OWN JOBS

Jupiter:
expansion, opportunity, increase, confidence, excess

Saturn:
structure, limit, responsibility, sequence, durability

Neptune:
imagination, meaning, inspiration, idealization,
permeability, uncertainty, faith

Uranus:
changed conditions, disruption, new options,
instability, deviation from the old setup

Pluto:
leverage, pressure, control, consequence, concentrated power

Neptune does not mean "bigger."
Jupiter carries expansion.

13. LIFE-STATUS NEUTRALITY

Do not assume:
employment
partnership
home ownership
children
full-time work
able-bodied status
financial security

Use covering language naturally.

14. NO ASTROLOGER PERSONA

No:
"I think"
"I'm watching"
"this makes me think"

Show the reasoning directly.

15. PROSE MOVEMENT

lived tension
-> astrology
-> why it creates the tension
-> complication / alternate manifestation
-> concrete examples
-> consequence
-> observation, question, or action

Do not force every section into the exact same structure.

16. VOICE

Write with observation and consequence.

Prefer:
"A role may disappear before the replacement is obvious."

Over:
"This transit can create identity changes."

Prefer:
"One easy yes can become three hours you did not have."

Over:
"Be mindful of overcommitment."

No "whether."
No em dashes.

17. SPECIFICITY IS NOT SCRIPTING

Bad:
"You postpone the dentist because an email arrives at 2 PM."

Good:
"An appointment keeps getting postponed because the schedule is full."

18. DENSITY

Use one primary manifestation menu per major factor.

Elsewhere, compress.

Do not repeatedly list:
application / offer / title / client / publication

A manifestation run should usually be:
3-5 possibilities in one sentence
OR
up to 4 short sentences.

19. MULTI-PASS TRANSITS

Pass 1:
introduce/test.

Pass 2:
review what held and what failed.

Final pass:
keep, resolve, formalize, hand off, or leave behind.

Do not regenerate the first-pass menu.

20. SECTION ARCHITECTURE

Use:
YYYY OVERVIEW
WHAT YYYY IS ABOUT
domain section if strongly repeated
WINTER YYYY
SPRING YYYY
SUMMER YYYY
AUTUMN YYYY
YYYY IN REVIEW
WINTER YYYY+1

Avoid opaque headings.

Do not add a Solar Return mechanics section unless it genuinely helps
the reader understand the year.

21. CURRENT-YEAR REVIEW

YYYY IN REVIEW summarizes that calendar year only.

Next-year events remain in the next-year seasonal section.

22. SYNTHESIS COMPRESSES

Overview and Review connect themes.

They do not:
re-list every manifestation
introduce unsupported domains
turn into inventories

23. TECHNICAL ATTRIBUTION

Main prose explains lived experience.

Technical lines preserve:
dates
degrees
houses
aspects
passes
rulers

24. ADVICE IS OPTIONAL

A section can end with:
observation
consequence
question
action
unresolved tension

Do not turn every section into coaching.

25. SATURN RETURN

Include Saturn Return material only in an actual Saturn Return year.

26. GOVERNANCE

Generated wording is not owner-authored wording.
Owner-directed revision is not exact owner wording.
Judge pass is not owner approval.
Clean lint is not owner approval.

Only explicit exact owner approval promotes wording into the calibration corpus.

27. STOP RULE

Once astrology, logic, specificity, natural language, and voice are correct,
stop rewriting.

Do not change strong copy just to create surface variation.
```

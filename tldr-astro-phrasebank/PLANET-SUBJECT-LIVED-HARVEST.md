# Planet-subject lived-example harvest

Verbatim quoted lived-experience lines from the resource books, filed per planet-in-sign.
Every quote was kept only if it passes the planet-swap test (it could not be moved unchanged
to another planet's card). Trait-soup adjectives discarded. Quotes are clipped to the lived clause;
obvious OCR artifacts (stray accents, spacing) silently corrected, wording untouched.

Sources and citation keys:
- **Hand** = Planets in Youth, Robert Hand (843287735-Planets-in-Youth-Patterns-of-Early-Development.txt)
- **Tanaaz** = The Ultimate Guide to Astrology, Tanaaz Chubb (897673014)
- **CC** = You Were Born for This summary, CC Nicholas (1045500252)
- **Forrest** = The Book of the Moon, Steven Forrest (742243087)
- **Unravel** = Astrology: Unravel the Secrets (887025494)
- Parker DK (510191106) and Secret Language (613266882) are two-column OCR jumbles; planet-in-sign
  text is interleaved across columns and could not be quoted cleanly. Not used.

One deliberate cross-filing: a line from Hand's Sun-in-Cancer section describing a Moon-subject
behavior (kept familiar objects) is filed under Moon/Cancer with its true source line noted.

# Pull-quote sourcing spec — what to hunt for

You're collecting **standalone Marie lines** to use as verbatim CONFIRMED closers on transit cards.
Below is the exact shape, the send format, and a themed most-wanted list (where lines unlock the most cards).

## The shape of a good pull-quote

- **Length:** one sentence, 4–16 words (hard ceiling ~20 words / ~120 characters). Two very short sentences is the max. If it needs a run-up to make sense, it's too long.
- **Self-contained:** it has to land alone on a card with no setup around it. No "and that's why…", no dangling "it".
- **Person:** second person ("you / your") or a bare imperative. Occasionally a flat universal truth in third person.
- **No jargon:** no astrology terms (no "Saturn return", "8th house", "square") unless the line is doing it on purpose the way "The fourth house is where the bodies are buried" does.
- **Job of the line:** it *reframes* or *names a hidden pattern* or *gives permission* — it does not give step-by-step advice (that's the separate `marie_advice` field).

### The four cadences that work (with real examples already in the bank)
1. **"Stop ___"** — drop a self-defeating pattern. *"Stop measuring your worth by your output." / "Stop wearing burnout like a badge of honor."*
2. **"Let / Trust / Become / Start ___"** — permission or affirmation. *"Let yourself want what you want." / "Your needs are not too much." / "Become the home you're desperately searching for in others."*
3. **Flat aphorism** — a plain truth stated once. *"Undercharging is not humility." / "Grief is so physical that it takes on its own shape." / "Your pace is not too slow."*
4. **A question** — a provocation left open. *"Who have you become in your relationships?" / "If the foundation crumbles, who do you become?"*

### Two flavors per theme — I need BOTH
- **Challenge lines** (for hard aspects — square/opposition/conjunction): friction, cost, the thing you avoid. Usually cadence 1 or 4.
- **Affirming lines** (for soft aspects — trine/sextile): ease, permission, an opening. Usually cadence 2 or 3.

## How to send them
Plainest workable format — one line each:

```
<the line>  |  <theme tag(s)>  |  <flavor: challenge / affirming>  |  <source>
```

Example:
```
Rest is not a reward you earn after everyone else is comfortable.  |  boundaries, self-care  |  challenge  |  IG post, 2024
You are allowed to be a work in progress and still be worthy now.  |  self-worth, healing  |  affirming  |  newsletter
```

`.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, or `.docx` all work. The source note is what lets me keep them tier **CONFIRMED** (verifiably hers). Theme tag + flavor are what let me route them to the right cards. If you don't know the flavor, leave it blank — I'll judge from the line.

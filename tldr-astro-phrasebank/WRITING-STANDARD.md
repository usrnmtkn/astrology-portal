# TLDR Astro — Writing Standard (Marie Satori voice)

The authority for all authored and generated copy: compatibility, synastry, natal, transit, email,
social. If a generation pass or a Codex handoff produces reader-facing prose, it follows this.

**Source ownership:** Marie wrote and owns the source material (MarieSatori.com + *As Above, So Below*
+ the confirmed-quote bank). Preserve her voice, cadence, vocabulary, and editorial habits. Ground new
lines in her actual writing, not a generic idea of "astrology voice."

---

## Voice (habits, with her own lines as the reference)

- **Direct and plain.** Short declaratives. She names the pattern, then says what happens because of it.
  "The way you think and the person you are speak with one voice, so your opinions can feel like
  extensions of your identity."
- **Blunt when it earns it.** "Stop wearing burnout like a badge of honor. It's just burnout."
- **Imperative and permission-giving.** "Let yourself want what you want." / "Trust that your voice is
  enough." / "Stop measuring your worth by your output."
- **Second person, contractions, ordinary words.** Talks to the person, not about the chart.
- **Soft certainty for interpretation.** The placement is stated directly; the consequence gets *may*,
  *can*, *might*, *tends to*.
- **One clear point, then a concrete consequence.** Not three balanced clauses. Vary the rhythm.
- **Name the planet's real domain, never a generic placeholder.** The Moon is feelings and emotions, so
  say that, not "styles," "energy," "way," or "approach." Same for each planet: Sun is identity, Mercury
  the mind, Venus love, Mars drive, Jupiter belief/growth, Saturn responsibility. If a sentence could
  belong to any planet, it is too generic.

## Construction rules (verdicts and short interpretations)

1. Two or three natural sentences when the idea needs them. Don't compress everything into one compound
   sentence, and don't pad to three when one lands.
2. Name people and placements: "{name}'s {planet} in {sign}" (add house only under the house rule below).
3. Assign elemental roles to the correct person. Not "the water Moon" — "Jose's Moon in Cancer."
4. Chart-based only. "Same sign" means a shared style, nothing more. It does not prove both people avoid
   reassurance, carry unfair burdens, or make the same assumptions. Don't invent a specific problem from
   a broad category, and never invent budgets, deadlines, work problems, childhood history, or events.
5. Vary the construction across a set: direct comparison; cause and consequence; recognition then blind
   spot; one person's pattern then the other's response; a plain practical observation.
6. Don't force a reassuring synthesis. Some pairings just create a recurring difference. Name it and stop.
7. Aspects: call placements conjunct/trine/etc. only when the real chart shows it within the approved
   orb. Sharing a sign or house is not an aspect. "Mirrored," "polarized," "across an axis" are for
   oppositions only — never for same-sign.
8. Say "the connection," not an assumed romantic/professional/family frame.

## Houses — all or nothing

- Use houses only when **both** people have reliable birth times. If either is missing, approximate, or
  rounded: omit houses for both, don't do a one-sided read, don't say the data is missing, don't fall
  back to a noon/solar chart. The sign interpretation must stand complete on its own.
- When both times are reliable: weave the house into the sentence, named to the right person, and say
  how they *live* that area — don't append a textbook house definition.
  - Not: "Marie's Sun is in the 9th house, so belief, education, and travel are important to her identity."
  - Yes: "Marie's Sun is in the 9th house. She needs room to question what she was taught and decide what
    she believes for herself."

## Source the facts from the matching surface

Voice can come from anywhere Marie wrote. **Astrological facts must come from the same kind of
placement.** A natal Moon in a sign is a fixed trait; a Full Moon / New Moon / lunation in a sign is a
transit, and in Marie's horoscopes it's keyed to the reader's Sun/Rising *houses*, not to a natal
placement. Never anchor natal copy in transit/lunation writing — they mean different things.

- Natal Moon in a sign → Forrest's *The Book of the Moon* (Moon-in-sign), Lofthus, *As Above, So Below*,
  and the natal phrasebank. Not the Full Moon horoscopes.
- The remedy/advice for a natal placement derives from that placement's own healing direction (e.g.
  Forrest's "Secret of Happiness & Healing" per Moon sign), not from a transit article.

## Sentence mechanics (from the MarieSatori.com passages + As Above, So Below)

Match how the sentences move, not just the word list. From the source:

1. **The chart fact leads the sentence.** Name the placement first, then the behavior it produces, in
   second person: "With Aries driving your identity here, you're quick to start things and slow to wait."
   Not "you are quick to start things; this comes from Aries."
2. **The shadow turns on one hinge.** "But that same drive can make you act before you've thought it
   through, leading you to start fights you didn't mean to." One sentence: same-trait → "can/may" →
   participle consequence ("leading you to…"). Don't stack a separate balanced clause for the flaw.
3. **Behavior gets a concrete range, not a keyword.** "wants to be first at everything, from a work
   deadline to a pickup game," not "competitive, assertive, driven."
4. **Vary length: a developed sentence, then a short flat one that lands it.** "Stop wearing burnout
   like a badge of honor. It's just burnout." The second beat is deadpan and final.
5. **Possibility stays conditional** with may / can / might whenever the chart supports a range, not a
   certainty.
6. **Close on a concrete cost or move, never an abstraction.** "…and you feel what that silence costs,"
   not "…and growth becomes possible."
7. **Stop once the pattern and its reason are clear.** No summary sentence restating the point.

## Do not use

Em dashes. One-sentence paragraphs. "Real"/"true" as vague intensifiers. "Settle," "settles,"
"steady," "steadiness," "comfort," "warmth" (as standalone abstractions — say the concrete thing:
food on the table, a routine that holds, being touched, someone checking in). This ban overrides the
source phrasebank: if her own line uses a banned word, replace it. **The "room" metaphor** — "in the
room," "a room's mood," "the smartest in the room," "no one in the room," "read the room." Say who or
what plainly (the people around you, whoever's with you). ("Room to breathe / room to roam" is fine —
that's space, not a setting.) "The catch" (use "the challenge"). "The upside?" / "The catch?" "On the
one hand / on the other hand." "The danger is…"
"This is about…" "This placement becomes…" "This part of the chart…" "The connection asks you to…"
"Alignment," "activation," "holding space," "navigating," "leaning into," "your journey," "a version of
yourself," "the thread," "moves through your topics." Repeated "one placement does X while the other does
Y, so Z." Long strings of *while / though / which / allowing / making*. Keyword lists dressed as prose.
Abstract endings ("emotional clarity," "lasting stability," "meaningful growth," "what the connection
requires"). Clever closing lines that need decoding. A last sentence that drifts off the paragraph's point.
"The native," "house themes," "this placement indicates," "expresses identity through the same zodiacal
style," "directs this planetary energy," "activates this area of life."

## Naming and direction

You = the reader. Use the other person's name. "Both of you" only when it applies equally. Never "one
person / the other person" when names are known. The reader is one of the two, so never call the pair
"they/their/them" collectively — it is "you and {name}" or "your two ...". Keep directional
relationships directional; do not reverse who affects whom.

## Gold-standard reference (compatibility verdicts, You / Jose, sign-only)

- **Sun · Aquarius / Aquarius:** Your Sun and Jose's Sun are both in Aquarius. You lead the same way,
  independent and a little contrarian, more interested in the idea than in being liked, and you recognize
  elements of yourself in each other almost right away. The challenge is you may both want to be the
  independent one, so you end up competing to stand apart instead of standing together.
- **Moon · Scorpio / Cancer:** Your Moon in Scorpio and Jose's Moon in Cancer are both water signs, so you
  feel things the same way and rarely have to explain a mood. Your Scorpio Moon keeps feelings private and
  is slow to trust; Jose's Cancer Moon is more openly affectionate and wants closeness sooner. Because your
  Moon and Jose's are in trine, you set each other at ease instead of on edge, but you both pull inward when
  upset, so if you go quiet at the same time, your guard goes up while Jose feels shut out, and the gap grows
  instead of closing.
- **Mercury · Pisces / Aquarius:** Your Mercury in Pisces picks up on tone, mood, and what isn't being said.
  Jose's Mercury in Aquarius works through logic, systems, and the counterargument. Those are water and air,
  two different ways of thinking, so even a simple conversation can get missed, and you might have to talk
  more directly instead of leaving room for interpretation.
- **Venus · Capricorn / Capricorn:** You and Jose both have Venus in Capricorn, so you love the same way,
  treating it with deep commitment and responsibility, steady and loyal and slow to give your heart. Because
  you want the same things, affection between you comes without much effort. The challenge is you may both
  wait for the other to move first and hold back your warmth, so what you each feel stays unspoken and you
  end up unsure where you stand.
- **Mars · Aquarius / Aquarius:** Your Mars and Jose's Mars are both in Aquarius, so you each go after what
  you want on your own terms and resist being told how. When you want the same thing, you work well together.
  When you want different things, you may both dig in, call it principle, and get nowhere.
- **Jupiter · Leo / Leo:** Your Jupiter and Jose's Jupiter are together in Leo, close enough to count as a
  conjunction. You grow through the same things, expressing yourselves, building confidence, and being seen,
  and you believe in each other's big ideas without much doubt. The risk is that you build each other up so
  much that neither of you wants to be the one to call a plan unrealistic and let the other down.
- **Saturn · Virgo / Virgo:** You and Jose both have Saturn in Virgo, so you handle responsibility the same
  way, through service, high standards, and getting the details right. You understand each other's seriousness
  without having to explain it. The challenge is you may hold each other to the same impossible standard, so
  instead of easing the pressure you raise it, and the work never feels good enough to either of you.

## Check before returning anything

Which placement belongs to which person? Which sign/house supports the claim? Is the aspect within orb?
Did I invent a behavior the chart doesn't establish? Does the ending name what happens, or retreat into
abstraction? Does it read like a person speaking plainly, or like a template being filled?

## Structural implication

Because verdicts now carry names, placements, and in-orb aspects, they can't be a static six-per-type
library. They're generated per pair from each person's signs (and houses when both birth times are
reliable) and the aspects that are actually within orb. This standard is the authority for that generator.

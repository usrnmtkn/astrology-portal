# Moon signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (what the Moon actually does + its shadow), from
Steven Forrest, *The Book of the Moon*, cross-checked with Lofthus. **Marie** = your own phrasebank
wording (reviewed `natal_sign_story` + compat gift/shadow) to draw voice from. Write final copy in your
voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md, and swap any banned word that appears in a Marie line (see the do-not-use
list) for the concrete thing. The Moon = how a person feels, soothes, and what they need to feel safe.

- **Aries** — Lived: reacts fast and hot, acts on a feeling the second it lands and is over it just as
  fast; shadow: rage, insensitivity, picking fights.
  Marie: "You feel things fast and hot, and you need to act on a mood before it passes, though you can
  flare up and act on a feeling before you've thought it through."
- **Taurus** — Lived: soothes through the senses (touch, taste, food, a familiar setting) and is slow to
  shift a mood; shadow: rigidity, digging in, refusing to be moved.
  Marie: "You calm down when life is calm and predictable; food and a routine you can count on soothe you,
  though you can get stuck in a mood and refuse to be moved."
- **Gemini** — Lived: processes feelings by putting them into words, needs someone to talk it out with;
  shadow: staying in the head and skipping the feeling underneath.
  Marie: "You process feelings by talking them out, and your mood shifts with the conversation, though
  you can talk around a feeling instead of actually feeling it."
- **Cancer** — Lived: absorbs the mood of whoever is around, easily hurt and remembers every slight,
  needs home and belonging; shadow: fearful withdrawal, hiding behind caretaking, over-giving.
  Marie: "Your feelings run the show, and safety comes from home and the people who feel like yours,
  though you can over-give and neglect your own needs."
- **Leo** — Lived: needs appreciation and attention said out loud, feels low when ignored; shadow:
  self-importance, needing to be the center.
  Marie: "You need to feel appreciated, with a little spotlight, to feel okay; affection lands loudest for
  you, though you can need too much reassurance and take a slight to heart."
- **Virgo** — Lived: calms down by fixing, tidying, and being useful; shadow: over-criticism of self and
  others, never feeling it's good enough.
  Marie: "You feel safest when things are handled; a tidy detail can lift your whole mood, though you can
  pick your feelings apart and never feel done."
- **Libra** — Lived: needs harmony and a partner, hates it when things get tense; shadow: avoiding conflict,
  over-accommodating, losing itself in the other person.
  Marie: "You feel calm when things are fair, and conflict unsettles you fast, though you can keep the
  peace at the cost of your own needs."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: feels everything all the way in or not at all, engages deeply, and wants to get to
  the bottom of things; private and slow to trust (Forrest: "intense, probing, suspicious"; Secret Language:
  the Moon is in fall here, so connection and possessiveness run strong); shadow: jealousy, holding
  grudges, isolation.
  Marie (approved): "Your feelings go all the way in or not at all, and you'd rather get to the bottom of
  what's wrong than be told everything is fine."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: needs room to breathe and something to look forward to, restless when boxed
  in; shadow: dodging the heavy feeling by staying on the move.
  Marie: "You feel best with room to breathe and something to look forward to, though you can bolt from a
  heavy feeling and call it freedom."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: manages feelings through control and work, slow to lean on anyone; shadow:
  coldness, repression, a grim sense it's all on their shoulders.
  Marie: "You'd rather handle a feeling than sit in it, though you can shut feelings down and call it
  fine before it is."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: needs space to feel from a distance, keeps people at arm's length; shadow:
  coldness, dissociation.
  Marie: "You need space to feel from a little distance; you process feelings by understanding them,
  though you can detach from your emotions and go cold."
- **Pisces** — Lived: soaks up whatever the people around it are feeling, empathic; shadow: losing its own
  boundaries and not knowing whose feelings it's carrying.
  Marie: "You soak up the emotional weather around you and feel everything at the edges, though you can
  soak up everyone else's feelings and lose your own."

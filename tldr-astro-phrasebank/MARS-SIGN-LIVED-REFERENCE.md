# Mars signs — lived reference (grounding for compatibility/natal copy)

Two layers per sign: **Lived** = accurate doctrine (Parker DK). **Marie** = your phrasebank wording (compat
gift/shadow); banned words already swapped to Mars's own domain word, drive. Mars = drive, desire, anger,
and how a person goes after what they want. Write final copy in your voice per
WRITING-STANDARD.md and lint every line.

- **Aries** — Lived: goes straight at what they want, first and fast, and needs to burn it off through sport
  or they get irritable; shadow: explodes, bulldozes, doesn't notice the damage.
  Marie: "You go after what you want head-on, and sitting still is the hardest thing you do, though you can
  go off half-cocked and pick fights."
- **Taurus** — Lived: goes after what they want at their own pace and won't be rushed; sensual and patient;
  shadow: immovable once roused, slow to let go, stubborn.
  Marie: "You move slowly but you don't stop; your drive is stubborn and built to last, though you can dig
  in, stall, and refuse to move."
- **Gemini** — Lived: chases what they want with words and quick moves, mind and body always busy; shadow:
  scattered, starts more than they finish.
  Marie: "Your drive goes into words and quick moves; you fight and flirt with your wit, though you can
  scatter your drive and argue instead of act."
- **Cancer** — Lived: acts on feeling and protects their own, tenacious once they care; shadow: goes sideways
  instead of head-on, fights by pulling away or guilt.
  Marie: "You act to protect what's yours, and your drive runs on feeling, not logic, though you can go
  passive-aggressive and act from hurt."
- **Leo** — Lived: goes after what they want with confidence and flair, wants to be seen doing it; shadow:
  pride, needs the spotlight, sulks when not admired.
  Marie: "You act with flair and pride; you want your effort seen and your courage recognized, though you
  can make it about pride and sulk when unrecognized."
- **Virgo** — Lived: works hard and precisely, turning restless nerves into useful effort; shadow: picky,
  frets, burns out on the details.
  Marie: "Your drive goes into doing it right; you work hard and sweat the details, though you can nitpick
  and turn drive into anxious busywork."
- **Libra** — Lived: pursues through charm and partnership; their drive comes alive with someone alongside;
  shadow: indecisive, dodges direct conflict.
  Marie: "You act through others and hate a fight, so you push by persuading, not shoving, though you can
  avoid conflict until it leaks out sideways."
- **Scorpio** — Lived: goes after what they want completely and quietly, and will not quit (Mars's own
  sign); shadow: obsessive, controlling, holds a grudge.
  Marie: "Your drive is intense and strategic; you go quiet, then you go all the way, though you can get
  controlling and let anger go cold and vengeful."
- **Sagittarius** — Lived: chases what they want with enthusiasm and needs a challenge or an adventure,
  blunt and direct; shadow: restless, over-commits, drops things once bored.
  Marie: "You chase the big goal with enthusiasm, sometimes overshooting the practical part, though you can
  act careless and burn out on impulse."
- **Capricorn** — Lived: drives toward the goal with ambition and discipline, plays the long game to win;
  shadow: cold about the objective, ruthless, buries the wanting.
  Marie: "You work with discipline toward a result that lasts; your ambition is patient and relentless,
  though you can grind past your limits and go cold."
- **Aquarius** — Lived: acts on their own terms and won't be told how, drawn to the unconventional or the
  cause; shadow: contrary for its own sake, detached, digs in.
  Marie: "You act on principle and your own timing, and you resist being told how to move, though you can
  rebel just to rebel and detach mid-fight."
- **Pisces** — Lived: their drive rises and falls with their mood, acts on inspiration and for other people;
  shadow: fizzles out, avoids confrontation, escapes instead of pushing.
  Marie: "Your drive ebbs and flows with your mood; you move best toward something you believe in, though
  you can avoid confrontation and let your drive dissolve."

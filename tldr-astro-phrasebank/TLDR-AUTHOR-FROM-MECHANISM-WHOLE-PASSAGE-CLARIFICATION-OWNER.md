# Author-from-mechanism whole-passage clarification — owner ruling

**Status:** Owner ruling, 2026-08-13. Generation and review rule; not reader-facing copy.

> A passage does not pass because it contains one photographable clause. Every sentence must either advance the lived scene, state a specific consequence, or provide necessary astrology-to-life perspective. A generic astrology-summary sentence fails the passage even when another sentence passes the photograph test.

## Application

- The photograph or overhear test is a necessary minimum, not an overall editorial verdict.
- Observable-noun counts are deterministic lint only. They do not establish voice quality, coherence, or causal scene density.
- Review the prose row by row. Do not infer that one model's entire batch is better because it has more concrete nouns or clears deterministic lint.
- A lived sentence cannot launder an abstract sentence elsewhere in the same passage.
- A sentence that does not advance the lived scene, state a specific consequence, or provide necessary astrology-to-life perspective blocks the passage.

## Calibration failure exposed by this ruling

The sentence "In this karmic framework, repetition carries a philosophical or spiritual lesson" fails the astrology-summary gate even when a preceding teacher, trip, disappointment, opportunity, or repeated question clears the photograph-test minimum.

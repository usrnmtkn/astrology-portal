# Aspect meanings: the measured-aspect slot

Source: Natal-Aspect.txt (planet-to-planet aspect doctrine), distilled to the psychological core and
written in Marie's voice per WRITING-STANDARD.md. The source's fatalistic framing (health verdicts, karma
claims, parent-blame, marriage and divorce predictions) is reframed the correct way: as tendencies,
inherited patterns, and growth lessons written straight to the reader, never as a verdict. No em dashes.
Linted.

**This is the SYNASTRY section, not compatibility.** Compatibility is the separate same-planet, sign-only
view (your Sun vs their Sun), Co-Star style, and needs no birth time. This file is the cross-planet aspect
layer (your Venus to their Mars, your Moon to their Saturn), which the app shows only in the synastry
section and only when BOTH people have accurate birth times, because an aspect depends on real degrees.
Gate: if either birth time is missing, approximate, or rounded, this whole layer is hidden for the pair.

Written by function (feeling, mind, drive, and so on), so an entry reads for either direction the resolver
loads (your Venus to their Mars, or your Mars to their Venus). It is the body the app overlays when the two
placements are within the approved orb.

Each aspect carries a base line plus three sub-notes:
- **Early story:** the pattern you may recognize from your early life, written to you, never as blame or fate.
- **The lesson:** the growth the aspect asks for (the Chubb-style "karmic lesson," in plain words), as a direct instruction.
- **In love:** how the aspect plays inside a romantic bond. Conditional render: show this line only when the relationship is a partner or an ex. Omit it for friends, family, and coworkers.

Aspect families. **Conjunction:** the two functions fuse and run as one. **Square / opposition:** they pull
against each other. **Trine / sextile:** they run in easy agreement (a real gift that can go slack without pressure).

Planet functions: Sun is identity and will. Moon is feelings and needs. Mercury is the mind and how you talk.
Venus is love and what you value. Mars is drive and anger. Jupiter is growth and belief. Saturn is
responsibility and fear.

## Sun + Moon

**Conjunction:** Identity and feeling want the same thing, so what you want and what you feel rarely argue; it's a single, whole drive that can push hard in one direction, though it can leave you unable to see a mood that isn't your own.
- *Early story:* You may have grown up where one person's needs set the weather of the house, so your wants and feelings learned to move as one.
- *The lesson:* Let in a need or a mood that isn't yours without feeling it threatens who you are.
- *In love:* You and a partner can feel merged fast, so the growth is staying yourself inside the closeness.

**Square / opposition:** What you want and what you feel pull against each other, so the push to achieve and the need to be looked after keep getting in each other's way, and you have to keep choosing between them.
- *Early story:* You may recognize an early home where two people pulled in different directions, and you learned to carry both.
- *The lesson:* Stop the tug-of-war between what you want and what you feel, and let them share the same day.
- *In love:* You can be drawn to partners who split you between duty and desire, so the work is not making one of them wrong.

**Trine / sextile:** Identity and feeling agree, so you usually want what you're actually doing and move through the day with will and instinct in step, though the ease can tip into coasting.
- *Early story:* You may have had an early home where wanting and feeling weren't at war, and you carry that ease forward.
- *The lesson:* Keep the drive lit so the natural harmony doesn't quietly turn into drifting.
- *In love:* You partner without much inner conflict about it, though easy can quietly slide into passive if you let it.

## Sun + Mercury

**Conjunction:** Identity and mind fuse, so you think what you are and your opinions feel like extensions of you; that makes you clear and persuasive, though you can be too close to your own ideas to hear a different one.
- *Early story:* You may have been the child whose thoughts and self were treated as the same thing, praised or corrected together.
- *The lesson:* Hold an opinion without feeling that a challenge to it is a challenge to you.
- *In love:* You take a partner's disagreement personally, so the work is hearing it as a different view, not an attack on who you are.

- *In love:* You give and attract affection easily, so the growth is letting a partner see the days you don't feel charming.

**Trine / sextile:** Identity and drive move together, so you act on your beliefs, decide fast, and follow through, courage that reads as natural rather than forced.

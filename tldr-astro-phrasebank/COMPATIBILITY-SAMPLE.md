# Compatibility cards — all 7 planets (book-grounded sample)

Model **Shared · Different · Watch · Try**, one line each. Traits grounded in Marie's *As Above, So Below* (Sun/Moon/Mercury/Venus) and phrasebank `natal_sign_story` (Mars/Jupiter/Saturn). 1,008 sign-pair cards total. Tier voiced-original-grounded, DRAFT.

### Sun — You: Aquarius · Them: Aquarius  _(same_sign)_

*The Sun is how each of you shines, leads, and knows who you are.*

- **Shared** — You share a core identity, so you get each other's drive instantly.
- **Different** — You both stand outside the norm and want to change things — the risk is a shared blind spot, not a clash.
- **Watch** — You can compete for the same spotlight instead of sharing it.
- **Try** — Root for each other; there's room for two to shine.

### Moon — You: Scorpio · Them: Cancer  _(same_element)_

*The Moon is how each of you feels safe, soothes, and needs care.*

- **Shared** — Both water Moons read emotional tone quickly.
- **Different** — Scorpio feels intensely and privately and won't do shallow reassurance; Cancer feels strongly and finds comfort in home, family, and the familiar.
- **Watch** — One person may go quiet while the other reaches for reassurance.
- **Try** — Name whether you need space or comfort before reacting.

### Mercury — You: Pisces · Them: Aquarius  _(mixed)_

*Mercury is how each of you thinks, talks, and needs to be understood.*

- **Shared** — You both want to be understood — you just get there by different routes.
- **Different** — Pisces thinks in images and intuition and speaks empathetically; Aquarius thinks innovative and unconventional and loves progressive ideas.
- **Watch** — One wants the headline while the other wants the whole story.
- **Try** — Ask which they want: the short version or the long one.

### Venus — You: Capricorn · Them: Capricorn  _(same_sign)_

*Venus is how each of you loves, shows affection, and decides what's worth choosing.*

- **Shared** — You love in the same language, so affection lands easily.
- **Different** — You both show love through loyalty and follow-through — the risk is a shared blind spot, not a clash.
- **Watch** — You can mirror the same need and leave it unspoken.
- **Try** — Say out loud what would actually feel like love today.

### Mars — You: Aries · Them: Cancer  _(friction)_

*Mars is how each of you goes after what you want and handles conflict.*

- **Shared** — You both go after what you want — you just attack it differently.
- **Different** — Aries goes after what it wants head-on and hates sitting still; Cancer acts to protect what's its, driven by feeling over logic.
- **Watch** — One charges while the other stalls or simmers.
- **Try** — Name the goal and the pace out loud before acting.

### Jupiter — You: Sagittarius · Them: Virgo  _(mixed)_

*Jupiter is how each of you grows, hopes, and finds meaning.*

- **Shared** — You both want to grow — you just believe in different roads.
- **Different** — Sagittarius grows by reaching for meaning and horizon; Virgo grows through craft and small, compounding improvements.
- **Watch** — Your ideas of growth can point different ways.
- **Try** — Find the one goal you both actually want.

### Saturn — You: Capricorn · Them: Cancer  _(opposition)_

*Saturn is how each of you handles responsibility, limits, and the long game.*

- **Shared** — You both take the long game seriously — in different arenas.
- **Different** — Capricorn's structure is authority and time; it becomes its own authority; Cancer's work is emotional security, building a foundation it didn't inherit.
- **Watch** — Your sense of duty can pull in opposite directions.
- **Try** — Agree on who carries what before resentment builds.

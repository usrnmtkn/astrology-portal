# Voice and narrative contract

Use the supplied CHANI materials to study information order, specificity, pacing, and functional anatomy. Do not imitate proprietary wording, recurring catchphrases, or visual identity. Final copy must be original TLDR Astro/Marie editorial writing.

## Narrative unit

Every interpretation chooses one coherent situation. It does not translate each symbol into a category list.

A useful internal sequence is:

1. recognizable lived moment or clear opening claim;
2. development of the same behavior or tension;
3. meaning that follows from that scene;
4. proportionate agency, when useful;
5. cycle/pass context, when genuinely relevant;
6. technical facts in the designated footer.

These are possible functions, not mandatory paragraphs or stock transitions. Suppress a beat if it repeats, abstracts, or merely names what the previous sentence already showed. Stop when the thought is complete.

## Surface-specific voice

- Personalized transit: one behavior under pressure or opportunity; practical direction; technical facts last.
- Planetary horoscope: one house-selected life scene; related questions may deepen that scene; close with compassion or agency.
- Collective Sky: one collective situation; no claim that the reader personally has a particular natal house.
- Moon phase: cycle role plus phase-appropriate action.
- Moon sign: short embodied guidance and direct imperatives.
- Daily horoscope: one concrete daily scenario and a specific, proportionate suggestion.
- Natal: a durable integrated pattern, not time-bound advice and not a list of definitions.

## Rejection rules

Reject copy that:

- enumerates categories where a scene should be;
- says a planet `brings` a topic to a sign/house;
- says one symbol `meets` another as a grammar shortcut;
- uses `topics`, `conditions`, or `circumstances` as empty containers;
- forces the same opening on every record;
- repeats title, dates, or footer inside the body;
- diagnoses, guarantees, moralizes, or presents astrology as certainty;
- assembles prose from keyword arrays;
- copies CHANI sentences or replaces nouns in them;
- turns feedback about good writing into the published writing itself.

## Questions and metaphor

Questions are optional and must remain on the chosen subject. Metaphor is optional, concrete, and brief. Neither is a template requirement.

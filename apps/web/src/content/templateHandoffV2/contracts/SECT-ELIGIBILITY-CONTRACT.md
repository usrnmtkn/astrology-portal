# Day-chart, night-chart, and sect contract

## Natal interpretation

Sect-dependent natal copy is eligible only when the chart calculation has reliable birth time, location, horizon/house data, and a validated day/night result.

- Day chart: Sun is the sect light; Jupiter and Saturn belong to the day team.
- Night chart: Moon is the sect light; Venus and Mars belong to the night team.
- Mercury's sect is calculated from its actual relationship to the Sun and horizon rules used by the accepted engine. It must not be guessed from a static team.

The renderer may select a reviewed sect clause only when its eligibility predicate is satisfied. Otherwise omit the sect slot without fallback prose.

Sect modifies an integrated natal interpretation. It does not become a detached textbook paragraph.

## Transit ranking

Sect-based transit ranking or severity weighting is a separate experimental product decision. Keep it disabled unless repository tests and explicit product approval already enable it. Natal sect eligibility does not authorize transit-ranking changes.

## Required tests

- eligible day chart selects the correct day clause;
- eligible night chart selects the correct night clause;
- Mercury uses calculated eligibility;
- missing/uncertain birth time suppresses sect copy;
- initial and hydrated results match;
- no transit-ranking behavior changes as a side effect.

## Moon in Scorpio in the 6th house (you)

The Moon is where your emotions and instincts live. It shapes what makes you feel safe, and how you comfort yourself. Your Moon is in Scorpio, meaning you feel things intensely, privately, and all-or-nothing, and what you want most is depth and honesty. You keep your guard up until you're sure about someone, and quietly wonder if there's a catch. You feel things to extremes, get hurt more deeply than you ever show, and hold onto old hurts longer than you want to. Pouring all of that into something creative is what actually settles you. Pushed too far, this side of you can tip into reactivity, moodiness, or shutting people out. At your best, you make people feel cared for, comforted, and safe enough to be honest.

It's in your 6th house, meaning this side of you comes out at work, in your health habits, and in your daily routine. Your feelings go straight to your body: worry shows up in tension, appetite, and sleep. You are the one who can repair, improve, or reorganize almost anything, and helping genuinely feels good. Try not to eat or decide anything important when you are upset or worn out. Astrologers call the Moon 'in fall' in Scorpio, meaning feelings this deep are heavy to carry, and you carry them better than you give yourself credit for.

Astrologers consider Saturn uncomfortable in Cancer, meaning structure did not come naturally to you, so you figured it out on purpose. That usually makes you more thoughtful about it than people who never had to try.

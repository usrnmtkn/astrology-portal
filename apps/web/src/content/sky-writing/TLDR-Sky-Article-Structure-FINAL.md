ABSOLUTE RULE (owner, on approval): no em dashes anywhere in serving copy, this surface included. Colons, commas, periods, semicolons only. The Sun in Leo worked example in chat contained em dashes; they are corrected at import and every article lints for the character before staging.

## The structure (from the published pieces)

**1. Seasonal context opener.** The article begins inside the current Sun season, then introduces the transit as an amplifier or counterweight: "During Virgo season, we're already primed for analysis... Add Mercury in Virgo to this mix, and the mental chatter becomes deafening." Fast movers always get this frame; slow movers open with the collective moment instead.

**2. Ingress statement, date in prose.** "Mercury entered Virgo on {{entryDate}}. This is Mercury in its home territory." The dignity note rides the ingress sentence when the planet is in domicile; otherwise the rulership translation appears here.

**3. Collective read.** Three beats in open prose, no labels: the trap ("the cultural mythology that our worth equals our usefulness"), the gift ("Virgo's gift is discernment... What's really being asked of us now is integration, not endless correction"), and the permissions ("You don't have to fix everything. Some problems aren't yours to solve... You are allowed to be human. Even with Mercury in Virgo. Especially with Mercury in Virgo."). Optional mantra line: "It may help to say this part out loud: '...'" (owner construction, licensed on this surface).

**4. Dated aspect sections.** Each exact aspect in the window gets its own H3 with the date in the header: "The Mercury-Uranus Square: {{aspectDate}}". Body names the mechanics in plain consequence ("perfectly organized systems meet pure chaos. The plan falls apart.") and what it asks. Direct questions are licensed on this surface; they are the owner's documented register ("This square is asking: What would happen if you stopped managing everyone's perception of you?").

**5. Event interactions.** Lunations and eclipses inside the window get their own dated section, read THROUGH the transit ("maximum tension between the need to control (Virgo) and the need to surrender (Pisces)").

**6. Exit in prose, tone shift.** The exit date arrives mid-prose with the post-event turn: "As Mercury continues through Virgo until {{exitDate}}... Once the eclipse passes, the tone shifts. Virgo's precision no longer needs to be a weapon turned against yourself. It can become a tool for care."

**6b. Historic movement (conditional; owner ruling on approval).** For planets with a historic return, the article carries a lookback: the last time this planet was in this sign and close degree, with the years in prose from {{historyEntryDate}}/{{historyExitDate}} and the degree range when the engine supplies it. Renders for Jupiter, Saturn, Chiron, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, and the nodes. NEVER for the Sun, Moon, or fast-moving planets in direct motion; fast movers earn a lookback only in their retrograde editions (the Mercury Retrograde in Leo piece's 2012 lookback is the model). The reader instruction uses the five-case reflection from the owner's aspect template (alive and remember, alive without memory, not born yet, family lived it, no direct link), converted to statements. The section never implies the same events repeat.

**6c. Retrograde variant (conditional).** When the planet stations retrograde in the sign, the article upgrades per the Mercury Retrograde in Leo blueprint: shadow entry date in prose, station dates as their own dated sections, the shadow-to-shadow window governing serving, degree precision on stations, and the lookback licensed regardless of planet class. The direct-motion article resumes at post-shadow exit.

**7. Horoscopes for {Planet} in {Sign}.** All twelve rising blocks, public, in the article. Each block: the transit's house for that rising with the house named in life-terms ("Mercury in your 6th house of routines and health"), the window's aspects threaded through that rising's houses ("Mercury square Uranus hitting your communication sectors... The opposition to Saturn shows..."), and the permission close ("You're allowed to have a morning routine that isn't a hot mess. You don't have to optimize every second of your existence." / "Stop censoring yourself into palatability.").

## Register rules for this surface (from the pieces, they override the fallback-row bans here)

- Questions licensed, in the "asking:" construction and direct.
- "You're allowed to..." and "You don't have to..." are the horoscope close signatures; "Stop X. Start Y." licensed.
- Contractions on throughout.
- Second person dominant; "we" for the collective read.
- Dates always in prose, always from the engine fact object; degree precision when the engine supplies it.
- The epistrophe construction is licensed ("Even with Mercury in Virgo. Especially with Mercury in Virgo.").
